## Script 1: Masayoshi Son, "the most expensive handshake" (242 words, about 83 s)

### 1. Script as read, with beats
- **Hook (0 to 8 s):** This might be the most expensive handshake in history, and one man in it is about to find out if he can do it twice.
- **Comparison (8 to 15 s):** He's basically the opposite of Warren Buffett. Buffett waits years to buy, and this man once decided in six minutes.
- **Name plus paradox (name at 15 s):** His name's Masayoshi Son, and the same gut that made him a legend nearly wiped him out.
- **Dated events with numbers (21 to 46 s):** In 2000, Son became the richest man on Earth, for about three days. Then the dot-com crash hit, and he lost about 70 billion dollars. But around that time, he had met a Chinese founder named Jack Ma for about six minutes. Son put 20 million dollars into Ma's company, Alibaba. By 2020, that stake was worth more than 200 billion dollars. That's ten thousand times his money, from one gut call.
- **Turn (46 s, 55%):** So you'd think he'd learned to slow down, but he did the opposite. Son has now put more than 60 billion dollars into one company, OpenAI. S&P, the credit rating agency, has already moved SoftBank's outlook to negative. That's a warning its credit rating could drop. And in May, Bloomberg reported executives asking what happens if OpenAI fails.
- **Real soundbite (66 to 69 s, 80%):** Son has said it himself: "We lost around 70 billion dollars."
- **Zoom-out to now, with "you" (69 to 77 s):** On November 10, SoftBank reports its results again. And every time you open ChatGPT, you're using a piece of that bet.
- **Mirrored kicker:** Last time, a 20 million dollar bet saved him. This time, he's betting more than 60 billion.
