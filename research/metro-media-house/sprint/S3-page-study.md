Findings:
- Two caption styles. Organic reels (PAL, ACK): short (about 370 chars), casual lowercase, opinion voice, a few hashtags. Paid reels (EIN, YC): about 4x longer, sentence case, no hashtags, a mini-essay that retells the story.
- The caption hook never repeats the video's first line. Video always opens with a "This is the most X ..." superlative plus a pop-culture or photo frame (Tony Stark, Wolf of Wall Street, "iconic photo"). The caption opens with a different angle (a known fact to subvert, a judgement on the person, or a dated scene).
- CTA: the organic captions have no call to action. They end on a punchline, then hashtags (PAL: #palantir #founder; ACK: #founder #startups #investor). Both paid captions end with the same sponsor block: a line about broadening access to venture capital for U.S. investors starting at $500, then "Paid for by USVC's adviser AngelList Asset Management, which creates incentives to promote." (verified in info.json).
- Paid posts (EIN, YC) get far more comments per like (6.25 and 1.16) than organic (0.26 to 0.34). The captions show no comment prompt or giveaway, so the reason is unverified: possibly paid reach to cold viewers, or people arguing about the investment pitch. Read the comments when Instagram is reachable.

## 3. Top vs weak among the 4 (likes only, no views)

Rank by likes: PAL 51,224 > ACK 41,062 > YC 18,304 > EIN 7,919. Comments per 100 likes runs the other way (EIN 6.25 > YC 1.16 > PAL 0.34 > ACK 0.26).

| What | Top two (PAL, ACK) | Weak two (YC, EIN) | Separates them? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid or organic | both organic | both paid (YC by a USVC adviser, EIN on USVC's own account) | yes, fully |
| Topic | one living, famous, divisive man (Thiel, Ackman) | a group photo and a history story (YC batch, 1927 Solvay) | yes |
| Video hook | "This is the [man] ... and he's basically [movie hero] in real life" (Tony Stark, Wolf of Wall Street) | "This is/might be the most [iconic/important] photo ..." | yes |
| Caption | short, lowercase, opinion, hashtags | long essay, sentence case, no hashtags | yes |
| Length | 78.4 s, 95.0 s | 80.2 s, 119.4 s | no (PAL and YC are near equal) |
| Words a minute | 184, 171 | 174, 177 | no |
| Shots a second | 1.08, 1.32 | 1.55, 0.99 | no |
| Median shot | 0.75 s, 0.60 s | 0.54 s, 0.80 s | no |
| Saturation / vivid pixels | 0.29 / 17.7%, 0.36 / 21.6% | 0.25 / 14.5%, 0.17 / 6.8% | weakly, top two are more colourful |

Reading: pace and length do not explain the like gap. What lines up with likes is the package: organic, one famous living person, a "he's basically [movie hero]" hook, and a short punchy caption. But four things change together (paid, topic, hook, caption), so this cannot say which one matters. Confounds: EIN sits on a different account with a different audience; likes keep growing after posting and we do not know when each info.json was pulled, so the newest post (YC) may be undercounted; paid reach can add likes from cold viewers who do not engage. Treat as a hypothesis, n = 4.

## 4. What a screenshot study needs (when Instagram can be opened)

Capture, with the date and time of each screenshot:
1. Profile header of metromedia.house: followers, following, post count, bio, link in bio, category, highlights, verified badge.
2. Reels tab grid, scrolled to the end or at least the latest 30 reels: the play count under each cover gives the missing views number. Also note pinned reels.
3. Per reel (latest 20 to 30, plus every one marked "Paid partnership"): post date, plays, likes, comments, paid label and sponsor name, collab co-author, audio name, full caption including the last lines (look for a CTA or comment prompt), pinned comment, top 5 comments, cover frame.
4. The 4 sampled reels in full, to explain the comment ratio (check for a "comment X" prompt or giveaway in EIN and YC).
5. Tagged tab of metromedia.house, and the profile, grid and tagged tab of usvchq, to see which other pages USVC pays.
6. Search inside Instagram for similar pages (same "This is the most ..." hook) to place this page in its niche.

How many: the full grid if the page has under about 60 posts, else the latest 30 reels plus the top 10 by plays. With 20 or more reels with plays, a top vs weak split by views (not likes) becomes possible.
