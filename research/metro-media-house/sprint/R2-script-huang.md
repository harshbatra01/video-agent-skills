## 1. Script as read, with beats
- **Hook (0 to 5 s, 13 words, "Nvidia" on screen at 0 s):** Nvidia was planned in this Denny's booth, then came 30 days from broke.
- **Comparison:** Most tech legends start in a garage. This one started at a diner table.
- **Name plus paradox (about 8 s):** His name's Jensen Huang, and he runs the world's most valuable chip company like it's about to go bankrupt.
- **Dated events with numbers:** Before Nvidia, Huang had worked at Denny's, washing dishes. Years later, he walked into a Denny's in San Jose and held the first meetings for a new chip company. He called it Nvidia. The early years were brutal. At one point, Nvidia came within 30 days of running out of money. Thirty days, and the company you know today would not exist.
- **Turn (about 55%):** So you'd think that once it worked, he'd relax. He did the opposite. Nvidia grew into the most valuable chip company in history, and Huang kept saying the same line. He says he has used it for 33 years.
- **Real soundbite (about 80%):** He's said it himself: "We're 30 days from going out of business." That's the man running the most valuable chip company on the planet, talking like a diner owner behind on rent.
- **Zoom-out to now, with "you":** In 2023, Denny's put a plaque on that booth. On November 17, Nvidia is expected to report its results again. And every time you ask an AI chatbot a question, there's a good chance it runs on Nvidia chips.
- **Mirrored kicker (body facts only):** Once, it was 30 days from broke. Now it's the most valuable chip company in history, and it's still run like it's 30 days from broke.
