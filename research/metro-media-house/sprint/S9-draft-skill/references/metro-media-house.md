## Style law (short)
- One famous photo opens, revealed through a 3x3 grid of white blocks in the first second. All 4.
- Hook line: "This is / This might be ... the most ...". All 4.
- Signature cut (Metro only): 1 colour-inverted frame, sometimes 1 white or black frame, then the new shot. About every 3 to 5 s in ACK and YC.
- Captions: every word on screen, one at a time on its word start, small white neo-grotesk sans (closest free match judged as Inter Medium), cap height about 3.4 to 3.7% of frame height, centred at 44 to 52% height, spread across the width, black on white paper. 1 to 3 big bold words per beat.
- Picture: real people first, many clips per name, white paper cards, film callbacks as proof of the comparison, logo bursts on lists, printed numbers, real soundbites.
- Pace is calmer than the AC references in this repo (Altman median 0.38 s).
- Ending: mirrored two-line kicker. Organic adds a callback picture and the page's own logo sting (do not copy). Paid swaps in a 9 to 11 s ad tail.

## Script formula (hypothesis from 4 reels)
1. Superlative photo hook, 0 to 4 s.
2. Comparison, 4 to 8 s ("basically Tony Stark in real life").
3. Name drop plus paradox, 8 to 17 s.
4. Dated run of events with printed numbers, 15 to 55%.
5. Rule-break soundbite, about 40 to 80%.
6. Turn, 35 to 75%.
7. Zoom out to now, 75 to 90%.
8. Mirrored kicker, last 2 sentences. No lesson.
9. Paid only: bridge line plus "Comment WORD".

About 230 to 250 words organic, sentences of 12 to 14 words. Judged scores 20 to 22 out of 24.

## Effect list (summary; timecodes in STUDY.md section 6)
3x3 grid reveal; subject cut-out with dimmed background and light rim; cell-by-cell background swap to the place word; red neon outline; inverted-frame cut; 2-frame thermal flash; zoom blur with RGB fringe; word-by-word spread captions (no repo tool yet); big split words; film callback clips; logo bursts; banknote burst into a counter; built UI receipt; photo walk-through with selective colour; polaroids on off-white paper; tilted 3D screen wall; callback zoom-out; logo sting (not ours); ad tail cards (paid only).
