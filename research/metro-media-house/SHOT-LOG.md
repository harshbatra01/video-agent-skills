# Shot log: what is on screen for each word

Read from every `raw/<id>/shotwords/sw_*.jpg` sheet (one middle frame per ffmpeg scene shot, at threshold 0.25) and the 1-second time sheets.
Shots are grouped by word run. Times are seconds. Device names follow `word-matched-broll`: burst, hold, news proof, symbol, printed number, breath, built object, callback. Two added here: **film** (a feature film or pop-culture clip) and **soundbite** (the real speaker's own voice, judged from frames and first-person wording, not heard).
Judged by eye. Names are checked against on-screen text where it exists.
