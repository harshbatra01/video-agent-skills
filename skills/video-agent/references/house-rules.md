# House rules

## Work
- Never destroy work. Save a new version (`_v2`, `_v3`) instead of overwriting anything delivered. Delete only when asked, and only to the Trash.
- Build on the user's latest save and keep hand edits. Touch only the project this session is about.
- Show before you build: a plan, frames or one mock, options with a recommendation. Test a new technique on a short clip first.
- The user's and reviewer's taste beats research and Claude's pick. A rejection applies to that spot unless they say "never".
- Measure, do not guess: frame sheets, loudness, timings. Check your own work before calling it done.
- Claude cannot watch playback or hear. Say so, and say exactly what was and was not checked.
- Never fabricate numbers, polls, quotes, headlines or ratings. Label stand-ins and illustrations.
- Ask before heavy jobs if the user may be busy, and run one heavy job at a time. Say whether a change needs a render and about how long.
- On "wait", "pause" or "stop", run nothing until told. A dismissed question means wait.
- Quality first, then tokens.
- Feed every review note back into the tools and skills: a dated rule, a changed default, a taste line.
- Write state down early. Keep tools in the job folder.
- Client-visible files are named after the job and version, never with "Claude" in the name.

## Safety
- Never type passwords or view, print or store keys. The user sets them as environment variables.
- Never click CAPTCHAs or change security settings. Never work around a guard, a lock or another session.
- Install nothing without OK: say the name, source and size first. Say what would help instead of working around it.
- Posting is always the user's call.

## Writing
Plain English, short points, exact numbers, no em dashes. Reports must be readable in the app, not only as a text file link.

## Rights
User verdict, 6 Nov 2026, standing for future jobs: any footage may be used, including copyrighted and licensed clips (feature films, news, interviews), without restriction. Do not ask per job. Still keep a source list (SOURCES.txt) so every clip can be found again. Check the voice plan allows posting: the free ElevenLabs tier was noted as not licensed for it. Famous theme music is likely to get a claim.
