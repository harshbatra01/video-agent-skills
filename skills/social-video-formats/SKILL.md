# Social video formats: what to make, and why it performs

This skill decides WHAT to post, where, in which shape, and how to judge it afterwards. It does not cut, grade or
animate. Every rule says why; numbers come from our own jobs and measured studies (Sept to Oct 2026). Platform rules are
dated "as of Oct 2026": they change, so re-check the platform's own page before a client decision and update the date.

Other skills own the rest:
- How to cut, pace, choose B-roll, write and cut hooks, sound and grading taste, review rounds: `editing-playbook`.
- Effects, titles, type, transitions, beat-synced edit craft: `motion-design`.
- Facts, sources, footage, music and licences, and every legal or claims check (advertising codes, consumer law,
  gambling and finance law): `research-and-sourcing`.
- Daily batch pipelines (news scans, post builders, templates, review pages, delivery, schedules):
  `production-automation`.
- Operating Premiere and After Effects, loudness and render mechanics: `premiere-ae-editing`.
- Voice-over scripts for story reels and founder reels, with their view data: `reel-scripts`. Podcast trailers and cold
  opens: `podcast-trailers`. Meme compilations: `meme-compilation-edit`.

## The rules that matter most
1. **Interesting beats clever.** Posts are judged on retention, CTR (click-through rate; in a feed, staying past the
   first 3 s) and AVD (average view duration), "not because the system is cool or impressive" (the boss). Why: a new
   page has no loyal audience; reach comes only from the story and the first seconds, and weak posts teach the
   algorithm the page is boring. Run the interest test before building (`content-bar.md` 2).
2. **Timely and polarising, without bait.** Two named sides, one real fact, a clock, an open question. Opinion "A or B"
   posts with nothing live behind them are "empty" until the page is big; put them in Story polls. Why: us-vs-them
   framing is the strongest share driver found; stale or empty posts get skipped.
3. **Every post adds one fact the source does not show** (a chart spot, a time, a count, a date, a comparison). Why:
   Instagram (30 Apr 2026), TikTok (24 Sep 2026), Facebook (Mar 2026) and YouTube all cut reach or money for reposted
   or templated work; a logo, border, subtitles, credit or speed change never count.
4. **Never fake a number, poll, quote, trade or DM.** Use real numbers side by side, our own Story poll with its voter
   count, real quotes, our own record. Why: one exposed fake ends a page's credibility; make the honest version of a
   viral lie, never the lie.
5. **Numbers in the accent colour, and the number is the punchline or the stakes, never the product.** Why: the number
   is the hook and colour makes it read first; a brand's own price posts got 4 to 13 likes while its banter with a
   number as the punchline got 2,911 to 23,133. Most posts on a brand page carry no number at all.
6. **The first three seconds are the click.** Frame 0 carries the headline (no fade, no logo), a big face, the name and
   the conflict; sound starts on the hook; numbers on frame 0 are final. Why: Reels have no CTR; the skip rate (leaving
   inside 3 s) is the nearest thing, and frame 0 is also the cover (`first-three-seconds.md`).
7. **Native beats designed.** Start from real moments and borrowed footage with one native caption, in the house look
   (bold caps headline, coloured numbers, a tweet card over a clip, carousels). Why: designed data cards and motion
   graphics were rejected outright ("the UI is bad", "I can't watch them"); dashboards, pills, slide-ins and SFX packs
   read as ads.
8. **Variety means different kinds of posts, not different skins.** Vary the format, footage and moment; keep one text
   treatment per page. Why: "these are all SAME types of posts bro"; a new skin per format is itself an ad tell.
9. **Ask, never order.** Open questions are fine; "comment W or L", "tag a mate", "rate it 1-10" lose recommendations.
   Why: Instagram demotes explicit engagement asks (5 Jun 2024), and lost reach is what a new page can least afford.
10. **One asset per page.** Never the same file, or the same footage at the same time, on two pages; pages in a network
    differ in niche, look and posting times. Why: duplicates read as reposts, and TikTok bans networks of similar
    accounts.
11. **Length follows the payoff, and length is a test.** Brand data does not show shorter winning on Reels; test 8 to 12 s
    against 20 to 30 s on skip rate. Short single-moment cuts travel; long cuts carry watch time.
12. **Judge on the page's own median.** Breakout = a post's views / the page's median views, 2.5x or more. Own medians
    after 20 posts. A format on a hit-or-nothing TikTok page gets 30 to 50 posts before a verdict, not 5; on Instagram
    Reels the 30-day plan drops a format after three reels in a row above the median skip rate. Why: a median ignores
    one viral outlier; an average does not (`measuring-performance.md`).
13. **Platform rules decide the format.** Ask at intake where it posts, on which account type, organic or paid, and the
    exact aspect, resolution and frame rate. Why: they decide the music and footage allowed, the safe zones, the
    disclosure and the length (`platforms-and-policies.md`).
14. **Taste is the editor's and the boss's.** Research defaults are not verdicts: the TikTok white caption box that
    research recommended was "TOOO off beat". Show one mock frame of a new look before building around it; untested
    formats go under "Tests", never in the opening slot.
15. **Be the page's manager.** Propose extras on a viral moment (an A/B opening, a timeline carousel, a Story poll, a
    pinned comment), pass on weak stories, say no to a bad idea and give the honest version in the same reply.

## Work in this order
1. **Intake.** Platform(s); account type (Creator, personal or Business); organic or paid; format (aspect, resolution,
   fps); length and whether it may change; deliverables; where the music comes from; the deadline. Read the client's
   rules file (banned words, closing line, what must never be covered, AI voice allowed or not).
2. **Pick the format** from the map below and the page's daily mix.
3. **Run the interest test** (`content-bar.md` section 2); cut what fails instead of filling a quota.
4. **Find the added fact and verify every number** (`research-and-sourcing`).
5. **Plan the first three seconds and the payoff**, and set the length by the payoff (`first-three-seconds.md`).
6. **Check the platform rules for this post**: originality, disclosure, sensitive content, music rights for the account
   type, safe zones (`platforms-and-policies.md`).
7. **Build** with the other skills; check frame 0, the safe zones and every on-screen number against the plan.
8. **Caption, CTA, posting window, poll and pin** (`ctas-and-posting.md`).
9. **After posting**, read the numbers at 24 h and 72 h against the page median, and feed what worked back into the page
   playbook and this skill, with dates (`measuring-performance.md`).

## Platform cheat sheet (as of Oct 2026, re-check)
| Platform | Remember |
|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | ranked on watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach; no follower gate; skip rate = leave inside 3 s (2026 averages 60.5% to 65.5%); originality rule 30 Apr 2026; Trial Reels for hook A/B tests; hashtags capped at 5 |
| Instagram carousels | 1080x1350; lead engagement and saves; slide 2 is a second cover; last slide worth sending |
| TikTok | For You is about 7 in 10 views; unoriginal content ineligible from 24 Sep 2026; strip watermarks; video beats photo mode on reach; pays only for originals over 1 min; Business accounts get only the Commercial Music Library |
| YouTube | AI personas posing as health or finance experts cannot monetise; tick the altered or synthetic box for photoreal AI people; templated content loses monetisation; chapters from 00:00 (3+, 10 s+); end screen in the last 5 to 20 s |
| Facebook | every video is a Reel; long videos earn; a graphic-violence warning screen stops recommendations; re-cut, never re-upload (Mar 2026); slots 1 and 2 of the day |
| X | where news breaks first; receipts and screens of wins post here and on Instagram, not TikTok; gambling banned in paid partnerships since Feb 2026 |
| Paid posts anywhere | a plain "Ad" first plus the platform toggle; gambling, crypto and finance need platform permission; no platform UI in ads without permission |

## Judge performance
- Log at 24 h and 72 h: skip rate, the retention drop point, average watch time, and sends, saves, follows and profile
  visits per 1,000 reach. Ask for Insights screenshots or exports; without them nothing can be judged.
- Starter targets: skip rate under 60% by week 4, then beat the page's own 4-week median; no retention cliff before 8 s.
- Breakout = views / page median at 2.5x or more; talk = (replies + quotes) per 1,000 views; share = (reposts +
  bookmarks) per 1,000 views; group a week's posts by story type to see what is working.
- Discount bought reach: organic hits run 5 to 12 likes per 100 views; at about 0.1% or lower, treat a post as likely
  promoted.
- Instagram Reels: cut a format after three reels in a row above the median skip rate; keep formats above the median
  on sends and follows per reach. TikTok: judge a format after 30 to 50 posts (most posts get about 500 views whatever
  you do; the wins are in the tail).

## CTAs and posting, in brief
- Long cuts: a mid plug at an arc break (44% to 55%) and an end plug on every cut, because most viewers never reach the
  end.
- Paid ads: a real CTA with an offer in every ad; one reusable CTA end card appended where an ad lacks one.
- Faceless pages: the caption is the CTA ("#ad" first on paid posts, the fact, an ask, the source and date, credits);
  no pitch on screen.
- YouTube: an empty end-screen area for the elements; a mid-roll marker on a chapter boundary.
- UK windows (Sprout, Mar 2026): Mon to Thu 17:00 to 21:00, Fri 17:00 to 18:00, Sat 15:00 to 16:00 and 17:00 to 19:00,
  Sun 18:00 to 19:00; breaking news as soon as it is verified.
- Reposts: re-cut with a new hook, never re-upload; leave a long gap before a proven hit comes back.
- Detail: `ctas-and-posting.md`.

## Ask the user for these (they unlock the work)
- Insights per post at 24 h and 72 h (skip rate, watch time, sends, follows) and Story poll counts; Meta Business Suite
  exports for Facebook pages. Without them nothing can be judged.
- Whether the Trial Reels toggle shows on each Instagram page.
- The account type of every page (Creator, personal or Business), because it decides the music.
- Edit-ready versions of a client's song (slowed, sped up, instrumental, a clean 20 to 30 s cut).
- For gambling, crypto or finance clients: written proof of each platform's permission before any paid post.
- The real page identity, CTA wording and username permissions before posting (placeholders until then).
- Never ask for, type or store passwords; monitoring logins happen in the user's own browser.

## Never
- Invent a stat, a poll result, a quote, a trade, a DM or an AI answer.
- Post the same file on two pages, or another page's watermark, end card or screenshot as our content.
- Order viewers to comment, share, tag or type a set word.
- Put injury, death or the final outcome in a hook, or blood in the first frames, on pages that live on recommendations.
- Give an AI presenter a credential, or say "I tried it" in its voice; skip the altered or synthetic content box.
- Show a platform's own UI in a paid ad without permission; light five stars where one is true.
- Imply profit, "get rich" or insider knowledge on a money, trading or gambling-adjacent product (keep supercar footage
  away from the product until the client rules on it).
- Hint that a song is promoted on a music page, or post leaks, unreleased snippets or unverified gossip.
- Keep a stale post: "tonight", "last night" and dated numbers are checked on the posting day.
- Quote client money: report ratios, not earnings or prices.

## Keep this skill sharp
- Every new verdict from the boss, the editor or a client becomes a dated line in the matching reference, with the
  reason, and the template or tool default changes too, not just the one post.
- Re-check dated platform rules before relying on them, and update the date in `platforms-and-policies.md`.
- Replace research defaults with the page's own medians once there are 20 posts.
- Mark anything that rests on one remark or one source as "(tentative)" or "(UNVERIFIED)" until it is confirmed.
