# Measuring performance

Platforms publish almost no benchmarks, so decisions come from our own numbers, read the same way every time. The
benchmarks below are vendor data from 2025 to 2026: starting points until a page has its own medians.

## 1. What the boss's words mean on each platform
| Word | Instagram Reels | TikTok | YouTube | Facebook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CTR | no organic CTR; the first 3 s are the click: nearest number is 100% minus skip rate | the first 3 s (proposition by 3 s, hook by 6 s) | thumbnail click-through (long form); the first 2.5 s on Shorts | 3-second views |
| AVD | average watch time | average watch time | average view duration; "Intro" = share still watching at 30 s | average watch time and the retention curve |
| Retention | the Retention chart beside skip rate (Aug 2025) | | retention graph, the most-replayed heatmap | a 41-point retention curve in the detail export |
| What ranking rewards | watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach (sends weigh more for non-followers) | For You (about 7 in 10 views) | | watch time, finishing, skips, full-screen taps; recommendations were 60% of one page's watch time |

## 2. What to log for every post
- At 24 h and 72 h: skip rate, where the retention line drops, average watch time, and sends, saves, follows and profile
  visits per 1,000 reach. Carousels: saves and sends.
- Story poll counts (they become next-day posts).
- Ask the editor or the boss for Insights screenshots or exports: it is the user-side step that unlocks every judgement
  below. Until they arrive, say so in each hand-over.

## 3. Benchmarks and starter targets (as of 2026)
- **Instagram skip rate**, Jan to Jun 2026 (brand Reels): 65.5% at 1K to 5K followers, 63.4% at 10K to 50K, 60.5% at
  100K to 1M. Vendor "healthy" lines of 30% to 40% cite unnamed trackers: treat them as aspirational.
- **Starter target**: skip rate under 60% by week 4, then beat the page's own 4-week median. Aim for no retention cliff
  before 8 s and an average watch time of at least half of a 20 to 30 s reel.
- **Average reel watch time**: about 8.5 s in 2026 (4 s in 2025).
- **Reach and engagement**: reach rate at 1K to 5K followers: Reels 9.78%, carousels 8.80%, images 7.00%; engagement
  (Q2 2026): carousels 0.50%, Reels 0.48%, images 0.33%. Another source: carousels 6.9% of reach vs Reels 3.3%. The
  levels disagree; both say carousels lead engagement and Reels lead reach.
- **Profile visit to follow**: about 13.5% in a typical month (all accounts, data to Jan 2025).
- **TikTok**: the median post gets about 500 views at any cadence; a big brand's routine posts got 1.1K to 1.9K while one
  framed as a joke got 421K. Judge TikTok on the tail, not the median.
- **YouTube music edits** (75 edits with 150K+ views): median 39 s, like rate 2.8% (top 10% above 4.2%), 0.49 comments
  per 1,000 views (`sports-music-edits.md`).

## 4. Breakout: views over the page's median
- **over** = a post's views / the page's MEDIAN views. 2.5x or more = breaking out.
- **talk** = (replies + quotes) per 1,000 views: debate potential (count it only on posts over 5K views).
- **share** = (reposts + bookmarks) per 1,000 views.
- Why the median: one viral outlier drags an average up and hides every other breakout. (A plan that used "3x the page's
  average" was advised to switch to the median.)
- Group the last 7 days by story type ("what's working"). In one week, court and legal stories ran about 1.4x to 1.7x
  usual and a third broke out; throwbacks broke out 0 of 6.
- **Source videos on YouTube**: outlier score = views / the median of the 15 uploads either side (30 neighbours). It
  finds the source videos worth re-cutting; it does not predict other platforms (YouTube views barely predicted Facebook
  views, +0.20).
- **Other pages' posts in research**: rank by views divided by the page's followers (a benchmark page's typical post ran
  0.96x its followers). A sample picked for hits shows what is possible, not what is typical.

## 5. Discount bought reach
- Organic hits run at 5 to 12 likes per 100 views; boosted posts can fall under 0.2. Mark posts at about 0.1% likes per
  view or lower as "likely promoted" and rank on likes, shares, quotes and saves instead (a 23.5M-view brand clip had
  0.17 likes per 100 views).
- Flag paid and co-authored posts: ratios on collab posts mean little.

## 6. How long before judging
- Own medians after 20 posts per page.
- Two clocks, by platform. TikTok (faceless pages): a new format gets 5 posts over 2 weeks to launch and a verdict only
  after 30 to 50 posts, not 5, because TikTok is hit or nothing. Instagram Reels (the 30-day plan, section 7): cut a
  format after three reels in a row above the page's median skip rate; keep formats above the median on sends and
  follows per reach, because skip rate reads fast and reliably there.
- More posts are more tickets, each smaller: on TikTok, against once a week, posting 2 to 5 times a week gave +17% views
  per post, 6 to 10 gave +29%, 11+ gave +34%, but the median stayed about 500 (Buffer, 11.4M posts, Oct 2025);
  platform-wide volume rose about 80% year on year while views per post fell 31% (Metricool, 2026).

## 7. The 30-day launch plan for a new page
| Week | Do |
|---|---|
| 1, baseline | 1 reel + 1 card a day per page; a public professional (Creator) account with about 6 launch posts; look for the Trial Reels toggle; log the metrics in section 2 at 24 h and 72 h |
| 2, hooks | two openings for each day's best story, changing only the first 3 s (Trial Reels, or alternate days); drop the weakest format; add one sendable carousel (a beef timeline, a chart recap) |
| 3, double down | 2 reels and 1 card a day only if skip rate holds under the median; start a daily Story poll; no this-or-that in the feed yet; tag artists only when the post is about them |
| 4, decide | keep formats above median on sends and follows per reach; cut any with three reels in a row above median skip rate; check Account Status; set targets from own medians |
- Why: Instagram publishes no benchmarks, so the plan generates our own and makes every decision from them.

## 8. Which shape earns which metric
- Reels earn reach; carousels earn engagement and saves (about 9x the saves of single images); sends drive non-follower
  reach, so make the last slide worth sending.
- On number posts (faceless pages): label memes earn shares (925,089 plays, 37,220 shares on one), receipts earn saves,
  a favourite-on-a-loser post earns quotes (10,739 on one), price polls earn replies ("too high or too low?": 8.2% likes
  per view).
- Story polls earn real numbers for later posts.
- Comments are not reach: comment-asking captions drew 203% more comments, but comments were not among the ranking
  signals named, and page size and story beat the prompt (a question-ending reel on one page got 31 comments; a
  no-question reel on a bigger page got 910).

## 9. Facebook analytics, when the page shares exports
- Meta Business Suite gives an earnings export (lifetime earnings per monetised video) and a detail export (246 columns,
  a 41-point retention curve). Join them on the video ID in the post's permalink. The built-in browser cannot open
  facebook.com: ask for the exports (weekly) or a read-only token, never a login.
- Match Facebook posts to their source YouTube videos by rare title words plus a plausible length; the exact length breaks
  ties; clip series inherit their longest member's match.
- Client earnings data is confidential: report ratios, not money.

## 10. Code your own posts
- Code each post by hand: topic, angle, hook type, lesson or not, ending type, news peg, real clips or not, who it
  addresses. Compare median views per group and the top 15 against the rest; count words, words per minute, the first
  sentence's length, questions, "But" sentences, numbers. Why: it is how a studied page learned that story-only reels beat
  lesson reels by 170K to 6.9K (`reel-scripts`).
- Before redesigning a format, measure our output against the references with numbers (readable words on screen, tiny
  words, where text sits, cuts per 10 s). It turns "looks designed" into a fix list (`faceless-posts.md` section 9;
  method: `research-and-sourcing`).

## 11. Hook tests and reviews
- Trial Reels and alternate openings: `first-three-seconds.md` section 6.
- Facebook (proposed, untested): judge hook A/B tests by the 3-second view rate; a weekly retention review that maps each
  drop-off to what was on screen at that second.
- After 24 h on any platform: a cliff in 0 to 3 s means rewrite frame 0; a cliff mid-video means cut there.

## 12. Feed results back
- A weekly review (Sunday suggested) updates the page's playbook from its numbers.
- Replace research defaults with own medians as soon as there are 20 posts.
- Record verdicts and their dates in this skill, and change the template or tool default, not just the one post.
