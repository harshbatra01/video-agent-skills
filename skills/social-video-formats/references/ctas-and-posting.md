# CTAs, end plugs, posting times, cadence and reposting

All platform rules here are as of Oct 2026: re-check before relying on them.

## 1. Ask, never order
- Instagram will not recommend posts that explicitly ask for comments, shares, tags or other actions, such as a set word,
  number or emoji (Instagram, 5 Jun 2024). Facebook has its own engagement-bait rules. The penalty is lost reach, the
  thing a new page needs most.
- Out: "Comment W or L", "tag a mate", "like if", "share with 3 mates", "like for A, comment for B", "Rate it out of 10",
  like-follow-tag giveaways. In: "Is that a W or an L?", "Who's winning now?", "Too high or too low?". Vary the wording:
  "rate it" and "W or L" sit close to the banned set-word asks.
- Comment-asking captions drew 203% more comments and questions 36.7% more (general data), but comments were not among
  the ranking signals named. Page size and story beat the prompt.
- Endings that work in narrated reels: a question for the comments, a comment keyword, or a callback that flips the hook.
  On one studied page, comment-keyword lead asks ("comment GROWTH") appeared only on low-reach reels (`reel-scripts`).
- Hashtags: 0 to 2. Instagram caps them at 5 (Dec 2025) and says they add context, not reach; one 24.4M-post study linked
  hashtags to about 32% fewer views (correlation only).

## 2. Plugs inside and at the end of long videos (long cuts)
- **Mid plug, long cuts only**: a 5.24 s clip inviting viewers to join the channel's community, placed at an arc break
  before the next arc, in a real pause (delivered at 44% to 55%). Never split a narrator sentence that runs across the
  seam. Play it at +4.5 dB (it measures -20.7 LUFS); music beds pause under it and resume where they stopped; a quiet
  whoosh and shutter mark its in and out.
- **End plug on every cut** (long, standalone, short): the plug's first 3.62 s (before its "now let's resume" line) at
  +4.5 dB.
- Why the middle: few viewers reach the end of a long video; far more are still watching at the midpoint.
- The short end plug replaced a 26.3 s YouTube teaser end card on 13 Oct, which also shortened standalones by about
  22 s (6:29 to 6:07, 6:24 to 6:01).
- Open: keep both plugs in long cuts, or drop the mid one? (asked, not answered).
- Proposed, untested: a pinned outcome comment with the source channel's YouTube link.

## 3. Paid ads: a real CTA with an offer in every ad
- Every ad ends on a CTA with an offer (the boss's script bar, `presenter-ads.md` section 1).
- One reusable CTA end card, built once as an alpha element timed to the CTA voice and appended as a 5 s end to ads that
  lack a CTA ("only create one for now and re use it on ads which dont have a cta"): kicker A "HEAD OVER TO [BRAND]" at
  0.12 to 1.42 s; kicker B "USE THIS DISCOUNT CODE" from 1.55 s; a code pill scaling in from its centre; the code word at
  2.62 s with a white flash; a shine sweep at 2.98 s; "[N]% OFF" counting up from 3.12 to 3.82 s.
- Build the appended shots from clips already graded and framed elsewhere in the project (a static product shot; a busy,
  moving shot behind the kicker read as blurry).
- A code card starts on the word "code" and holds to the end. The same brand sting (a swish + lock sound) in every ad;
  the music's final chord lands on the code card or the last line.
- An appended CTA adds about 5 s (one ad went from 29.3 to 34.3 s, another from 48.3 to 53.3 s); with no in or out marks,
  exporting the whole sequence picks it up.

## 4. Faceless brand pages: the caption is the CTA
- Caption order: "#ad" first (on paid or per-view posts); the fact line; an ask ("Too high or too low?"); the source and
  the date ("Price from [app], 1 minute before kick-off"); "Not financial advice" on finance; optionally the brand's own
  closing line; the clip credit ("🎥: @handle") and the photo credit (for example "Photo: [name], CC BY-SA 4.0, via
  Wikimedia Commons"); the posting window.
- No pitch on screen ("Trade now", URLs, "zero fees", payouts): it is an ad tell (`faceless-posts.md` section 9).
- Keep the client's CTA in one config line, and check that it is true: one client's "zero fees" line was inaccurate as
  written (a small fee on close or settle since about 5 Sep 2026), and "world's fastest" had no benchmark. Flag an
  inaccurate CTA once, with a fix, and offer the brand's own fixed closer from its brand guide as the alternative (the
  client decides).
- At most one emoji, and never in the brand's closing line.
- Co-author the post with the brand's account when the client agrees.
- Placeholders until the client decides: the page identity ("@yourpage" with a dark "YP" avatar), the CTA, masked
  usernames. Every review page lists what must change before posting.

## 5. Disclosure (platform side; the legal side is in `research-and-sourcing`)
- Anything paid is an ad, including pay per view: a plain "Ad" in the first line or the first frame AND the platform's
  toggle. UK and US regulators both say the platform label alone may not be enough. UK guidance accepts "ad", "advert",
  "advertising" and "advertisement" (not #gift, #spon, #collab or "thank you"), wants the disclosure at the start of a
  video, and a label on each item of a carousel.
- TikTok: the commercial content disclosure toggle labels the post Branded Content; undisclosed commercial content can
  lose For You (there are 24 h to answer a notice) and repeated failure can bring posting limits.
- Instagram and Facebook: the Paid partnership tool plus clear ad text.
- YouTube: the paid promotion tickbox shows a disclosure for 10 s at the start; UK guidance also wants "Ad" in the title
  or thumbnail before the click.
- X: a Paid Partnership label since 1 to 2 Mar 2026; it can be added after posting.

## 6. YouTube long form
- End screen: 15 to 20 s after the last word, with empty space for 1 video element + subscribe; the editor adds the
  elements in YouTube Studio; never add them yourself (zones: premiere-ae-editing `references/motion-design.md`).
- A mid-roll marker on a chapter boundary (the end of chapter 1).
- Open loops between chapters instead of an early sign-off; no outro (none of 22 measured top videos had one).

## 7. Music pages
- Each caption ends on an open question or a short reaction; a pinned-comment line and a Story poll still ship with each
  carousel (a pinned comment with our own answer is common practice, unproven).
- Story polls 2 to 3 a day (followers only); poll results become next-day posts with the voter count.
- Post manually from the phone with the client's official sound picked in the app; add the poll sticker and the pinned
  comment from the batch's caption file.

## 8. Song promo edits
- The song in the title, credited in the caption, the exact audio version linked (`sports-music-edits.md` section 1).

## 9. When to post (as of Oct 2026)
- UK windows (Sprout Social, Mar 2026, 21.6M UK engagements): Mon to Thu 17:00 to 21:00; Fri 17:00 to 18:00; Sat 15:00
  to 16:00 and 17:00 to 19:00; Sun 18:00 to 19:00 (Sunday is the best day).
- Sprout labels these GMT. BST ends 25 Oct 2026, so test an hour either way. India is 4h30 ahead of the UK during BST and
  5h30 after it.
- Breaking news as soon as it is verified; engagement posts 18:00 to 20:00 (a vendor adds 12:00 to 13:00: test 12:30);
  the Friday chart card after the 17:45 reveal.
- Every post's window goes in the batch's caption file, so whoever posts knows when.
- Facebook (one page's data): the day's 1st and 2nd slots, 4 to 8 h apart.
- Vary posting times across the pages of one network.

## 10. How often
| Page type | Cadence | Why |
|---|---|---|
| New music news page | start at 2 strong posts a day; standing 1 video + 1 carousel a day; 2 to 3 Story polls; recaps at most weekly | quality over quota; no source shows that volume wins |
| Faceless brand page | the benchmarks post 6 to 12 a day; test at 1 to 3 a day per page; the daily mix in `faceless-posts.md` section 4 | TikTok is hit or nothing; more posts are more tickets, each smaller |
| Facebook long-video page | slots 1 and 2 only; 1 to 2 re-cut posts a day instead of 2 to 3 full re-uploads (proposal) | the 3rd post of a day got about a third of the views |
- Cadence data (TikTok, Buffer, 11.4M posts, Oct 2025): against once a week, posting 2 to 5 times a week gave +17% views
  per post, 6 to 10 gave +29%, 11+ gave +34%, but the median stayed about 500 views: the gain is in the top tail.
  Platform-wide volume rose about 80% year on year while views per post fell 31% (Metricool, 2026).

## 11. Reposting
- Never re-upload as is, anywhere: re-cut with a new hook and an added fact (the originality rules in
  `platforms-and-policies.md` section 4).
- Facebook long videos: repost proven hits after 180+ days (a median 39% of the first run, against 14% to 22% sooner);
  never burn a hit with quick reposts; flops can win on a repost; hold the biggest hits for the November peak.
- Instagram (one narrated mini-doc page, a friend's data): same-day reposts sometimes beat the first upload, while
  reposts days later never did. Our Facebook data wins for long videos; on other platforms, test before assuming.
- Clipping-campaign briefs ban reposts.
- Never the same file on two pages.
- Expire stale posts: anything that says "tonight", "last night", "today" or "in 12 days" is checked on the posting day,
  with numbers refreshed; a post past its moment is marked do-not-post.
