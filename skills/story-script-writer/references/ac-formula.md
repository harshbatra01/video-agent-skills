# The Attention Compound formula, v1 (3 Nov 2026)

From 3 reels: Sam Altman, Elon Musk (chips factory), Max Verstappen. A working hypothesis from machine transcripts, with no view counts.

**In one line:** one person with a drive, one concrete detail that keeps coming back, a build-up that makes the situation look solved, a moment that breaks it, and an ending that returns to the detail.

## Format
- About 250 to 280 words, 72 to 87 s, 180 to 210 words a minute.
- Average sentence 9.5 to 14.6 words. Short beats are saved for the climax.
- No business lesson. Almost no "you" (0, 2 and 0 uses). Few date stamps.

## Steps
| # | Step | Where | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook in two beats | 0 to 8% | Famous person, the obvious shock, then a bigger promise. About 20 words | Not his power, but what happens to anyone who takes it |
| 2 | Plant the detail | 8 to 25% | A small concrete thing to remember. A real clip is strongest | Kill switch, "five years", a gas station |
| 3 | Name the drive | 25 to 45% | What this person wants or cannot stand | Cannot bear being told no |
| 4 | Build it up | to 35 to 70% | Stack proof until it looks solved or unstoppable | They said no, he did it |
| 5 | The moment | 35 to 75% | One picture where someone acts. Does not need the word "But" | The board presses the button |
| 6 | Second rise | after the moment | Bring it to now or make it bigger | Three Central Parks |
| 7 | Climax in short beats | 60 to 90% | Fragments, repetition, a number you can picture | "Gone", twice |
| 8 | Return to the detail | last 10 to 16% | The first detail comes back changed. No moral | Not the guy who says five years |

## Checks
1. Can a stranger say who this is about in one word?
2. Can they picture the moment?
3. Does the last line use a detail from the first quarter?
4. Is there no lesson and no advice?

## Soft spot
Our Anthropic script has no single named person, by the user's choice. An incident carried the story instead. So step 3 is not a hard rule when a strong event exists.
