## 3. Edit and pacing rules
**The order you set**
1. Study the reference frame by frame, with numbers.
2. Music and beat grid first, then every cut on the grid.
3. Pilot the first 2 to 3 seconds next to the reference and review.
4. Then script review, pacing and B-roll.

**Pace**
- Reference: 142 shots plus 97 micro-shots in 86.5 s, median shot 0.38 s.
- Ours (v15 plan, cut to 40.33 s): 57 planned shots plus the montage images, median 0.47 s, all on a 96 bpm grid.
- Visuals change at least about once a second. Your note: keep changing visuals but not the context.
- A rule break must be deliberate. Reference: "For context," on black at 8 s and a held anchor. Ours kept one 0.15 s black beat on "But".

**B-roll**
- Literal to the word being said. Your first note: images on a timer are "meaningless".
- Name gets that person, number gets a chyron or card, idea gets a fast burst of symbols.
- Subject fills at least 50% of the frame and is never cut by the edge.
- No narrow interiors as filler. No stand-in faces (the Greek bust and Jim Cramer were pulled).
- Score method: shot score = (2 x match + quality + framing) / 2, out of 10.
- Result: ours 7.5, then 8.6 at v10, reference 8.3. Same rater (Claude), one frame per shot, and your notes outrank it.

**Hard gaps that stayed open**
- Moving clips were only about 17% of v18 screen time, plus 34% graphics over footage. The reference was about 67% moving footage.
- You asked for "as much videos as there were in reference" on 19 Oct. Swapping to better-matched stills pushed the share down.
- News footage was 7% at v10 against 29% in the reference. Open-licence sources could not supply it.

**Length and script**
- 80 s was too long. You said 4 s shorter was "nothing" and asked for about 50 s, then cut "very boring" parts further.
- Daddy file and its gate were your idea on 19 Oct, after the pointless title card.
