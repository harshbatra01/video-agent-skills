# Effect vocabulary and taste: what is approved, what is banned, and why

Every verdict our users and reviewers gave on motion and effects, by genre, with their words. Check a new idea against
this file before building it. The general taste library for editing (story, cutting, B-roll, grade) is in
editing-playbook; this file covers motion, titles, transitions and effects.

## 1. How to read a verdict
- A rejection is about THAT placement unless the user says "never". Their words: "if u used it elsewhere and i didnt
  point it out there its fine there but here no". The light painting at 6-7 s went; the one at 25.7 s stayed once cut to
  under half its length. Blanket bans so far, in his words: auras ("do not do auras of this type"), "PowerPoint"
  geometry (spins, checkered or box glitches, closing shutters), whips that show mirrored frames ("looks ass"), black
  frames, colour floods (disliked twice).
- Research defaults are not verdicts. Research said to skip glitch, RGB split and flash effects for a 40-65 premium
  audience; the user then loved RGB-split captions on those very ads. The user's and reviewer's verdicts outrank research.
- Filter research through the taste list before building. Two research "aims" were overturned by reviews: "use the
  whole vertical frame" (he wants the picture in a band, "its not NECESSARY that we HAVE to use full frame") and "a dip
  or black breath before big moments" (he rejected the black frame). Glows, auras and floods are everywhere in top
  edits: take their type and timing, skip the glow.
- Good is not the bar: "this is good. but good wont cut we need GREAT". Effects must LOOK good, not just exist.
- The user's own first edit is a starting point, not the bar: "im not that experienced. it nEEDS TO BE MUCH BETTER".
- When something is cut, always bring a better replacement ("keep the flashes but find cooler shit").
- A new client's brief and references win over these defaults; the user's hand edits in their latest save are the brief.

## 2. Pick the genre first

| Genre | What wins | First rules |
|---|---|---|
| Social ads with captions and cards (vertical paid social, a famous face) | real footage and archive of the star, reference-style dynamic captions, cards with proof | small text on plates, nothing over the face, one counter at a time, hard cuts |
| Premium YouTube explainers (16:9, presenter, health or tech review) | premium type, real footage and 3D over drawings, little text | one thing moves at a time, a change about every 3 s, no stickers or cartoons |
| Clean-techy brand and explainer reels | two-key moves, continuous camera, light native glow | no bounce, overshoot, spins or pops |
| Cracked sports music edits | optical flashes on every cut, type behind the player, flash panels | no geometry, no auras, no long abstract holds |
| Native social posts (faceless pages, news pages) | real moments, borrowed footage, one native caption, the house look | no designed cards, no template motion |
| Documentary cutdowns (long footage cut to stories) | hard cuts, short white flashes on big jumps | `transitions.md` |

## 3. The vocabulary by family (music edits)
Status words: LOVED, KEEP (used, not flagged), ONLY SHORT, OUT (rejected), UNREVIEWED, NOT BUILT. Dates are 13-14 Oct 2026.

### Light and flash (optical: the approved family)

| Effect | Status | Why / words |
|---|---|---|
| Number or word behind the player + 1-frame white silhouette strobe + thin gold rim | LOVED | "at 22 the effect i REALLY like"; build on it |
| White silhouette strobes (1 frame), inverse strobe (background flashes white, player stays) | KEEP | the user's own hand-made signature |
| Flash panels: flat white or cream panels lit frame by frame across a cut (bars one by one, halves, bands) | KEEP, his definition of geometry | "seven vertical rectangles appearing one by one... then fading into the next clip"; first full use not yet judged |
| Silhouette strobe stack (field colour / white silhouette / inverted / white, one look per frame) | KEEP | the only matte transition of that round he did not reject |
| Light blow-out (hot orange, 1-3 flat white, golden haze, shot) | KEEP | flat white lasts 1-3 frames only |
| Line-art strobe (line drawing on white / glowing lines on black, then streak frames) | KEEP | |
| Full white frame on the payoff moment | OUT | it hid the ball going in: keep the payoff visible |
| Black "breath" frame | OUT | "black frame near 20"; a breath is a quiet shot, a freeze or an audio dip instead |

### Blur and streak

| Effect | Status | Why / words |
|---|---|---|
| Zoom cut (scale 100 -> 120% into the cut, radial blur 0 -> 55 -> 0) | KEEP, no longer the main transition | approved when whips went; then "better transitions than the zoom", "good wont cut" |
| Zoom rush (radial streaks around the cut, over-zoomed rolled arrival) | KEEP | |
| Radial chromatic split (rainbow tunnel) | KEEP inside zoom rush | |
| Defocus (melt into haze, arrive as colour blobs, focus over ~12 frames) | KEEP | "okay" |
| Short vertical streak smear | KEEP | |
| Light painting (highlights tinted on black, smeared) | ONLY SHORT | 6-7 s version "TOO long"; a shorter one stayed |
| Long sideways smear, abstract frames hiding the picture for 10+ frames | OUT | too long hides the picture |
| Red lens-streak flash | OUT | it "doesnt even show anything" |
| Ray burst (2 frames of bright desaturated rays with structure, then the shot) | UNREVIEWED | used once, not flagged, not in the latest version |
| Vortex (spin + zoom blur tunnel, bright, little colour) | UNREVIEWED | tuned to a reference but never judged in an edit; a Twirl distortion read as a rigid rotation, so keep it a blur tunnel |

### Colour and tone

| Effect | Status | Why / words |
|---|---|---|
| Invert flicker ("I", "I.I") | KEEP | |
| Negative strobe into a B&W section with 12 fps Posterize Time inside | KEEP | |
| True RGB split (three channel copies offset ~30 px, sliding back) | KEEP | |
| Gradient colour flash in Add (orange -> pink) | KEEP, not flagged | check it never becomes a flood |
| Colour pop (B&W world, colour cut-out) | KEEP on shots with visible background | on a tight close-up it is invisible |
| Colour flood (full red / yellow frames) | OUT | disliked twice ("red frame at 8 s out") |

### Geometry (only one form is approved)

| Effect | Status | Why / words |
|---|---|---|
| Flash panels (see above) | KEEP | geometry carried by light, frame by frame |
| Spins and rotations, checkered or box glitches, closing blades or shutters | OUT | "PowerPoint"; "Anything flashy is good. Anything crazy is good. Anything basic is bad." |
| Wave Warp square-wave glitch slices | OUT | built in the same round, the glitch family was rejected |
| Whips or slides that show a mirrored or static frame | OUT | "looks ass"; the mirror tile shows on the held frame |
| Light crack into flying strips | OUT (14 Oct) | "I don't like the light crack or the silhouette burst"; "look bad" |
| Silhouette burst (window scaling about the player) | OUT (14 Oct) | same review |
| Time slice | NOT BUILT | keep it under 12 frames, it can read as "blades" |

### Glow and aura

| Effect | Status | Why / words |
|---|---|---|
| Glow-outline or energy aura around the player | OUT for good | "REMOVE the aura ik its popular but its ass" |
| Thin gold rim on the cut-out (matte grown 7 px, glow 45, gold [1, 0.78, 0.15]) | KEEP, one per edit | it was part of the loved moment |
| Whole-picture bloom | KEEP only light | a soft bloom turned the pitch neon until threshold 86, intensity 0.16 |

### Type and story devices

| Effect | Status | Why / words |
|---|---|---|
| Picture in a 1080x800 band, type in the black bars | KEEP, his ask | "like [my football edit] so we can see more" |
| Title in the top bar word by word, his head popping out over it | KEEP | "this is good" |
| Story in numbers (real numbers behind him, each captioned) | KEEP | built from the loved device; facts checked |
| Lyric words on the sung syllables in the bottom bar | KEEP | the song stays the hero |
| Type spaced out over the face; bland default type | OUT | "text spaced out and bland" |
| A literal word on an object (GIFT on a ball) | OUT | "this gift one is there for no reason at all" |
| A gimmick motif not from the subject's story (a charging battery) | OUT | "i dont like the charging concept at all" |
| Reticle / lock-on ring | OUT | "the shot effect it just doesnt look nice" |
| Speed ramps, punch, shake | KEEP | |
| Cut-out "pop" with a white rim and a dimmed background | OUT in testing | it read as a pale white wash |
| A janky first frame; a stretched frame; a mirrored head above the title | OUT | framing errors: keep tracking calm, everything in frame |

## 4. Social ads with captions and cards (vertical paid social, a famous face)
**Liked**
- Reference-style dynamic captions with an RGB split: "the animated text which is dynamic looks nice keep it up"; "i
  really apprecieate how you figured out how to do nice dynamic captions, try bringing more of that creativity in".
- The stacked synced words (three list words, one per band, on their spoken word): "perfect" (only the middle rule
  changed to white).
- The partial-word roll for two near-identical words; frosted glass reveals; year counters; headline cards that really
  ran, word for word, with a highlighter; the dictionary card once the text sat on top.
- White type with red for negatives and green for ticks: "the colours u used are fine good infact" (gold not required).
- Dropping subtitles under graphics: "if we wanna remove subs and add graphics i am ALL FOR IT" (disable, never delete).
- A bold condensed name title with a small gold pill and no box, below the caption line; a sports-broadcast strap was
  liked too.
- Real archive of the star "wherever we can find to plug it in"; motion as a whole signed off: "all the motion design and
  everything is good now".

**Rejected**
- Small white text straight on footage: "the white is clashing with the sky", not readable.
- A dark glass plate behind a centred title that covered her mouth: "the background takes away sm. and its wierdly
  placed"; a title option Claude recommended: "why would u even reccomend E bro it is so off putting".
- Every board-reveal concept (marker reveal, mosaic with a glow, lift-off type): "i do not at all like any of the reveals
  you showed". What won: blur the items under a "?" and unblur each on its word.
- 1-2-3 badges ("looks ass"); stars with a background box; a box-edged glow (the star, the "?").
- A bar running off screen ("it going offscreen looks bad"); a cluttered frame ("tooooo cluttered"); opaque glass; a
  dictionary card with pronunciation and "noun".
- A green tint highlight ("this green mask is just bad... it feels cheap"): a tracked spotlight with the rest dimmed won.
- A static ring "stopped half the time" ("uninteresting"); plain B-roll with nothing on it ("boring find ways to make it
  nice").
- Text that does not match the words said ("WE FORMULATED" for "reformulated"); a green rule inside a white stack; a
  second counter right after a counter; any graphic or gradient over her face.
- A fade-out that leaves one frame of the talking head before a cut ("don't fade it out"): hard cut.
- A "corporate", slow feel ("corporate asf"): more archive of the star, faster visual changes, music with stakes.
- Strangers' faces in stock (objects and hands are fine); a stand-in who looks like the named person.

## 5. Premium YouTube explainers (16:9, presenter, review format)
**Liked**
- The user's 3-pillar transition (two slanted lines splitting three blurred scenes, the chosen one zooms in), with
  animated icons ("?", magnifier, "$") instead of 1, 2, 3.
- An exact copy of the channel's earlier score graphic when consistency was asked; the client: "The radar is such a cool
  idea!".
- "Premium calm" type: semibold humanist face, slightly smaller, wider tracking on caps, thin accent bars,
  sentence-case sub lines, pastel backdrops, dark ink on light cards.
- Real footage and 3D renders over drawings; one verdict stamp (NOT PROOF) was fine.
- Clock dials that fill to the real number ("make 30 minutes animation fill up to where 30 is on clock").

**Rejected**
- Heavy poster type ("too poster ish, we wanted premium [calm]").
- Stickers, stamps and labels piled up: "too much text man, try to show".
- A cartoon stand-in when footage or 3D exists ("this is ASSS"); a drawn scalp graphic ("dont like this").
- Square glow edges; icons cut in half by their own crop; dark icons on a dark bar.
- An element animating in again; cutting away from a page still being read; a zoom before the word.
- Highlighting a brand's own claim ("dont highlight 6 mins").
- Breathing or sway on presenter stills ("would look too ai"); AI pillar clips ("isnt usable").
- Mixed presenter versions: one approved still set and wardrobe everywhere (the presenter is the channel's anchor).

## 6. Clean-techy brand and explainer reels (a second house style)
Use these when a brief asks for clean, techy, premium motion; they were learned on launch films, finance and AI reels.
- **Approved:** every move as two keys with named eases; a face-matched montage of one subject; a reference finish
  rebuilt natively; one shared layout handed off at the peak of a whip zoom; a log-space camera curve; a climb that slams
  into a freeze before the big number; recurring devices that run through a film; continuity over hard cuts;
  transitions caused by movement when the script banned morphing. Kept without notes: a field of glass tiles with one lit
  tile; a sticky note on a tile; a pile of real posts; a film scene with a HUD over it; a silhouette made of LED points;
  typed text; a single "GO." text beat; a sheet turning into an inbox with a realistic zoom into a real screen.
- **Rejected:** bounces, pops, overshoot, spins, tilted stickers, wobbly hand-drawn marks, film-burn blobs; a paid glow
  plug-in that washed pastels to white; a 3D chase angle ("dont change angle like that"); numbered points ("The script
  does not have any points, this is pointless"); pointers ("dont do pointers"); two numbers in one counter; a chart card
  held about a second behind a wipe and a pop ("harsh"); captions over designed text ("doesn't make sense"); a flash
  montage with a logo and slides ("all over the place and not cohesive"); backgrounds everywhere ("not throughout, bring
  it in and out"); a brand colour sampled from another film ("weird": use the brand's own hex); a sample element pinned
  in the centre instead of blending in; a transition that showed the edge of its field.
- **Called flat or generic:** a famous painting cut in mid-scene; a big emoji logo with particles; a plain homepage
  screenshot; a number over a collage of screenshots; a stopwatch with a big number; a typed pull quote on paper; a film
  still in a film gate with a red word; a flat infographic; a circular countdown clock; text behind a random paper
  texture; random particles spelling a word; cards that are "a bunch of text".
- Wanted more: "stylization and dither elements here and there", including on objects (`native-looks.md`).
- Devices must fit beats: a corkboard with red thread for the date beats only, no corkboard elsewhere.
- Keep depictions of hacking or other sensitive activity abstract (icons, bars, short public quotes).
- Some reference looks use no glow at all; one measured look per video beats mixing "inspired by" ideas.

## 7. Native social posts (faceless pages, news pages)
- **Approved:** the news-page house look (bold caps headline, coloured numbers, a tweet card over a clip, carousels);
  tweet videos ("fire"); the split format ("format perfect"); platform logo badges; honest numbers carousels; light
  captions with personality.
- **Rejected:** designed data cards and motion-graphic data videos ("these are all SAME types of posts bro", "the UI is
  bad", "I can't watch them"); the TikTok white rounded box with black lowercase text ("TOOO off beat", even though
  research said hits use it); a new look kit per format (weather, broadcast, tabloid, paper, terminal, neon: it read as a
  template pack); a condensed headline font on video (hard to read on a phone); a label pill where a platform logo fits;
  urgency on everything.
- **Ad tells to avoid** (measured on 22 of our videos against 148 references): a logo or brand name on frame 0; template
  chrome (avatar-and-handle headers, category pills, slide counters, footers, legal lines); a designed card or dashboard
  in place of a moment; stock footage as wallpaper; slide-ins, wipes or counting numbers; a pitch on screen; whoosh and
  ding packs. Text reacts; it does not explain.
- Variety means different KINDS of posts (moments, memes, punchlines, real app screens), not different skins: one text
  treatment per page; vary the format, the footage and the moment.
- Motion rules for native posts: `titles-captions-type.md` section 6.

## 8. Glow, bloom and colour
- No paid glow plug-ins (Deep Glow washed pastel colours to white on a client reel); no auras.
- Element glow, native Glow only, lightly: Composite Original On Top, threshold about 55%, radius about a quarter of the
  element, intensity 0.8 or less, so colours hold and only edges glow. One glow on a precomp beats one per layer. Keep
  white article pages clean.
- Whole-picture bloom on broadcast footage: threshold 85+, intensity 0.2 or less (it turns pitches neon otherwise).
- A glow must never end in a square edge (effects on shape layers clip to the shape's box): soft comp-size solids
  instead (`native-looks.md`).
- One colour family per music edit; B&W or a tint for a quiet bar, colour back on the drop; change the flash colour per
  hit; then-vs-now as a grade (B&W past, colour present). Research, partly built.
- Accent colours that were approved in ads: white type, red for negatives, green for ticks.

## 9. Not judged yet: test before you rely on it
- Built for music edits, waiting for a verdict: impact frames (hard B&W threshold, then inverted), the poster silhouette
  (white field, black player), type flash, freeze clone, ghost trail, pop-up cut-out freeze, freeze to photo print,
  numeral sandwich, hit list, stutter strike, flash-on-word, the giant name behind him, loop endings, the first full use
  of flash panels.
- Researched, not built: glass shatter, depth-map strobe or fog, material strobe (only the cut-out turns halftone for
  2-3 frames), 3D extruded word, world swap behind the cut-out, rays shake, anamorphic streak, pose-match flash cut, glass
  or hex refraction, masked soft twitch, halftone ripple, time slice.
- The 18-effect library: each effect's mark is in `effects-library.md`.
- When one of these gets a verdict, move it into section 3, 4, 5 or 7 with the date and the words.
