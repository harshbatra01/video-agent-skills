## Coverage and evidence key (read first)

- [W] = I watched it: a 24-frame contact sheet captured from the video in the browser pane (stills only, so I cannot judge sync to the frame; where I mention timing it is from YouTube "most replayed" peaks or from timestamps on the sheet).
- [M] = metadata only (title, caption, description, search snippet). Every TikTok entry is [M]: TikTok cannot be opened from this PC (DNS block, WebFetch refused, browser pane denied). TikTok video URLs came from web search results. Dates on TikTok entries are decoded from the numeric video ID (first 32 bits = unix time), so they are exact upload dates. Like counts quoted from search snippets are marked "(snippet)" and are unverified.
- YouTube views, likes, subs and dates are from yt-dlp, pulled 2026-10-13.
- Instagram: web search found nothing, but instagram.com opens in the browser pane without a login, both reel pages (the page header gives likes, comments, date and caption) and the "popular" tag grids (for example https://www.instagram.com/popular/footballedit/ lists 12 top reels with creators and play counts). Caveats: the tag grids mix strong and weak edits, play counts come from the grid and likes from the reel page, and several IG handles differ from the same editor's TikTok handle. ## 4. Patterns noticed

1. The big 2024-26 upgrade is a hook before the drop, not more flashes. Hero Edit puts a 9-13 s spoken quote or podcast clip first, then a title beat, then the song (entries 11, 12); replays peak right after the switch (14.8-15.2 s on the Saka edit). Our reference does intro + drop with a music video; a human hook is the variation to test. The highest-reach Shorts I found do the same at scale: heisty710 opens with a meme skit or a VS-card list and saves the football payoff for the last third (37.3M and 17.2M views, entries 5, 6).
2. The borrowed-clip hook is the common recipe of the biggest reels: a skit, interview or viral clip under a caption bar for the first 3-20 s, then the football payoff. Examples: heisty710's 37.3M-view Short (entry 5), FicTic_Editz's 8.9M Short and "What Gravity?" reel (entry 7), Juventus's 1M-like "Number 7" reel (entry 8), benzxma.14's 2.7M-play Mbappe reel (entry 38). Titles follow one formula: a quoted line, the subject, "Edit", then the slowed song name ("... - Cristiano Ronaldo Edit | Montagem Batchi (Slowed & Reverb)"; FicTic 4.18M, 3.73M, Eagle Stan 2.24M, 2.15M). Typical runtimes are 13-35 s.
3. Ultra-short loops dominate the replay metrics: 7-15 s edits (the 12 s "0.5x speed" Short at 1.0M, Hero's 8 s "The GOATS" at 669K (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cva-Vp8kDE4), the 7 s Haaland sound clip at 69M) have "most replayed" peaks at 0-1 s, meaning people loop them. Build the last frame to rhyme with the first.
4. Winners composite real things into footage: cutouts of a person reacting, giant 3D/extruded words, props that carry a theme (Viking helmet), hybrid game or anime scenes (sanchezae, Blue Lock edits; IShowSpeed is the recurring guest in sanchezae's two edits and Blubz's track), Blender-made 3D HUD/rune elements and a custom-cut track (iluxhaep: After Effects + Blender + FL Studio, from a 12.6K-sub channel). That, plus typography, is what separates them from our flash-and-glow level; flashes and shakes are now table stakes.
5. Typography has real design: lyric lines with the key word highlighted (anwftbl), perspective display words, kinetic numerals that morph between scenes (HENYCREATE), counters and badges as a metronome (Hero Memory Reboot). None of it is default caption styling.
6. Named, repeatable formats drive growth, and their originators teach them: Pursuit depth style (audio ISQ - Pursuit, Aug 2026), Brawl Stars Melodie (Oct 2025, copied by Juventus), earth zoom, reverse + promo, time slice, shirt change (watched, entry 30). Creators sell presets, project files, clip packs and courses (Payhip links in descriptions), which spreads the look.
7. Series and numbering are everywhere: "(1/4)", "Pursuit 1/5", "transitions 2", "Part 2", "N ideas in 1 edit", near-daily posting (about six @ftb.aepp posts in ten days).
8. The song is findable and often the star: the highest-reach football-adjacent Shorts name the slowed song in the title and credit it in the description (heisty710: "| Nati Nati (Slowed)", "MONTAGEM SETHRON (Ultra Slowed)", "Music: Sem Controle (Slowed)"; 850K subs; football ones 5.4M to 37.3M views, entries 5, 6). Captions elsewhere use "[Player] x [Song]" (Mbappe x Phantom, Yamal X No Batidao, Messi X Boom Shaka, Nwaneri x Timeless), descriptions credit the track and the software, and editors run separate audio channels (Dr1zzysAudio). Brazilian funk/phonk "Montagem" beds slowed or super-slowed dominate: the official "MONTAGEM GUERREIRO (Slowed)" by Avenxir and SUNJI (released 2026-05-22, label Sketch Music) is at 16.6M views on its own (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vJA3bHNjX0M); "BATIDAO FUNK" at 12.3M (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNxGAPYi01g); the official World Cup song "Dai Dai" powered a 3.2M-view cinematic montage on a 6.5K-sub channel (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZMdZURZs85Y).
9. Sound-first virality is real and sometimes has nothing to do with edit craft: "Haaland (Ha Ha Ha)" reached 69.2M views on a 7 s magic-trick clip and 7.87M on a hand-drawn animation. AI "footballers singing" covers got 35.2M. The takeaway for song promos is to design around the song's 7-10 s hook and make a visual that loops and is easy for others to reuse. (AI generation is out of my lane; flagged only.)
10. One strong concept beats effect count: original 2D animation (Moonneca), a game format (Brawl Stars), a film-trailer wrapper (Ryghtan), split-screen CapCut vs AE (3.02M), AI anime story (Diablo AMV's 1.1M), a fan composited into the career (Speed x Ronaldo). Pick the idea first, then the effects.
11. Structure variety beats a straight run: interludes and chapters (Dr1zzy, HENYCREATE), a "bonus ending" tease (sanchezae), two-song switches (Hero's Saka edit), ranked countdowns.
12. The cold/aura format, going by captions and TikTok pages only (not watched), is still photos and freeze frames with an icy grade hit on the beat (kb93.edits "coldest celebrations", Mourinho cold edits; a Yamal "aura farming" Short at 750K views: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M5aHU7BVbF8). Cheap to make and easy for the audience to tag and share. Treat this one as a hypothesis until someone can open the TikToks.
13. Craft baseline in the scene is "4K upscale + 120 fps interpolation + custom CC": the Flowframes 120 FPS tutorial hit 492K views, captions advertise "4K 120FPS", and scene-pack channels (Reverse_Ftbl, Neymar pack 1.7M) give editors clean clips. Top editors also sell promos ("dm for promos") and run paid edit comps (350 USD, 50 USD), so these pages are both competition and potential distribution partners for our client songs.
14. Gaps to close with a user-allowed browser or VPN: watch the TikTok entries (especially 23-28 and 31) frame by frame; go deeper on Instagram (each popular tag grid lists only 12 reels and I opened about ten reels in total); and read Reddit threads. The browser-extension route needs the user to pick one of three connected browsers, which I could not do as a sub-agent.
