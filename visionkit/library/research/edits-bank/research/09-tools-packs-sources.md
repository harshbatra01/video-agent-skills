# 09 Tools, plugins, presets, packs and clip sources (research lane 9)

Written 13 Oct 2026 for the edits-bank. Section 1 entries are tools, packs and clip sources (not edits). Prices are USD unless stated. "(unverified)" means I could not open the vendor page or only saw a search snippet.

## Read this first: method, scan numbers, our rig

- Sources used: vendor pages, news posts, and YouTube metadata only (titles, views, subscribers, description text via yt-dlp; nothing downloaded). The shared web-search quota ran out mid-lane, so aescripts, Toolfarm, Motion Array and Gumroad prices are partly snippet-based and flagged.
- The scan: I read the descriptions and tags of 47 popular free After Effects editing-pack videos (8k+ views each, found with 5 YouTube searches on 13 Oct 2026) and counted plugin names. Totals: Sapphire 15, BCC/Continuum 13, Twixtor 13, Magic Bullet Looks 11, Deep Glow 10, Twitch (Video Copilot) 5, Topaz 4, Trapcode/Particular 2, Optical Flares 1, RSMB 1, Instant 4K 1, Element 3D 0, Red Giant Universe 0. Many packs list no plugins at all, so these are floors, not shares.
- The standard stack, written as an identical five-line block by many creators: Sapphire, BCC, Twixtor, Magic Bullet Looks, Deep Glow. Seen in Juraij Editzz (1 Aug 2024), Eagle Stan (2 Mar 2025), Parthedits (10 Nov 2025), Redtrick (14 Dec 2025, plus Omino and Glitchify), Madridae (24 Mar 2026), FEARLESS (22 Apr 2026), and an AVTEK EDITZ project-file giveaway (10 Oct 2026). AL4RS (5 Jan 2026) lists the first three. Zweng's NBA-edit pack lists Deep Glow, Twitch, Sapphire, Trapcode, Omino, Glitchify, Magic Bullet Looks, Optical Flares, BCC. Links are in entries 37 to 46.
- Our rig (read-only check of this laptop): AMD Ryzen 9 8940HX, 31 GB RAM, NVIDIA RTX 5070 Ti Laptop GPU with 12 GB VRAM. Adobe After Effects 2026, Premiere Pro 2026, Media Encoder 2026, Photoshop 2026. Third-party already installed: Mister Horse Animation Composer 4 and Premiere Composer, Video Copilot FX Console (quick effect launcher). Not installed: Sapphire, BCC, Twixtor, Deep Glow, Saber, Magic Bullet, Trapcode. RTX 50-series (Blackwell) is new: check each GPU plugin or AI tool for RTX 50 support before paying (unverified for most items below).
- Licensing in one line: every scenepack, most SFX packs and most free editing packs here come with a copy-pasted "fair use" disclaimer and no licence. For a paid music-promo client that is a real risk. Clean options are flagged in entries 49 to 59.

## 4. Patterns noticed

1. The scene runs on one copy-pasted plugin stack. Sapphire, BCC, Twixtor, Magic Bullet Looks, Deep Glow appear as the same five-line "Plugins Required" block in packs from 2024 to 2026 and in an Oct 2026 project-file giveaway (entries 37 to 46). We have none of them, so downloaded packs will not run as intended. The packs are cheap or free; the plugins are the cost.
2. Free packs are AE .ffx files, not Premiere. They open with missing-effect warnings without the plugins. For our Premiere builds the free path is Film Impact (entry 19), Animation Composer (entry 20) and our own presets.
3. Giveaway culture: milestone "100k special" packs on Mega, Drive or ad-gated links; the real money is small, $4.50 to $6 for a project file, $5 to $6 for a main CC, $87 for a full bundle. Editors sell presets and project files, not plugins.
4. The football pipeline is visible in HOBO Ae's timestamps: mark beats, swap clips, track the head, Twixtor slow-mo, zoom, Y shake, export, Topaz, CC, watermark. Beat markers first, effects second.
5. Quality is a selling point: "4K", "60fps", "No Watermark" and "Topaz" are in scenepack titles (8 distinct uploads carry Topaz) and in tutorials with 100k to 300k views. Clip quality is part of what "elite" looks like.
6. Slow-mo is a noun ("twixtors"). Clips are bought or downloaded already slow-mo-ready, then ramped on the beat. Flowframes (RIFE) is the free route; Twixtor Standalone ($149.95) is the cheap paid route.
7. Mobile is a parallel scene, not a toy: CapCut tutorials hit 500k to 1M views, Alight Motion "AE like" XML presets spread by Telegram, and scenepack makers list CapCut, VideoStar, Alight and Blurr as targets alongside AE.
8. Beat sync is automated: the most-watched beat-marker tutorials use a free one-click script (204k, 187k, 68k views); BeatEdit is the paid version. "Every hit on the beat" is table stakes.
9. AI roto changed in 2026: Premiere 26 Object Mask (Jan) and AE 26.2 Object Matte (Apr) give one-click subject mattes inside our own apps. That makes roto-cutout, text-behind-subject, color-pop and masked glow strobes cheap. No creator pack has caught up yet.
10. Rare tools: Element 3D, Red Giant Universe, Mt. Mograph Flow/Motion Tools and RSMB barely appear in edit packs (0 to 1 of 47). Do not spend on them first.
11. Licensing is the weak spot: scenepacks, SFX packs and free editing packs all lean on "fair use" boilerplate, and part of some packs (Zweng's SFX and overlays) is third-party. Clean sources found: Sonniss (free, commercial OK), Freesound CC0/CC-BY, Pixabay, Epidemic (check plan scope), Getty creative stock (generic players only). Named-star match footage has no clean free source.
12. Our laptop is strong enough (RTX 5070 Ti, 12 GB) for Topaz, Flowframes, Object Matte and GPU glow. The unknown is RTX 50-series support in older plugins: test with trials before paying.
