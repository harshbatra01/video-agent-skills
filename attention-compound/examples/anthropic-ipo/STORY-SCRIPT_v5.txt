Anthropic is having its IPO. So by law, it has to tell buyers what could go wrong.

The AI was told, hack it. You have no internet. But by mistake, the internet was on.

It tried about 1,800 moves and got nowhere. So it kept looking, and found a real company with almost the same name.

Its own notes called it a real website with 433 real users. It kept going. It took their email addresses, deleted a user's account, and pulled their chats.
