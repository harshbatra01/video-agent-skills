The single source of truth for this video. Read it before any change. Every new thing (an edit, a shot, an effect, a
motion graphic, a caption change, a sound, a script line) goes through section 0 first, and is logged in section 9.
Sections 1 to 8 are hand-written; the inventory between the AUTO markers is generated from the real edit by
`python3 tools/daddy.py <job> sync`, so it cannot drift. Older notes: READ-FIRST.md (history), REVIEW-v6-ANSWERS.md.

## 0. The review gate (run for every proposed addition, before building it)
1. Write the proposal as JSON: `{"what": "...", "kind": "name label | place label | hand callout | route map | ticker |
   shot | effect | sfx | caption | script", "t0": s, "t1": s, "text_on_screen": "...", "purpose": "what the viewer gains"}`.
2. Run `python3 tools/daddy.py <job> check proposal.json` (automatic rules A1 to A7 in section 3).
3. Answer the six judgment questions in one line each:
   - Q1 New: what does the viewer learn or feel here that they did not have one second earlier?
   - Q2 Theme: does it serve the logline (section 1) and the tone (cold, investigative, a little menacing)?
   - Q3 Language: does the reference do this kind of thing at this kind of moment? (section 2)
   - Q4 Cost: what does it take away (attention from the face, the caption, the pace, a deliberate break)?
   - Q5 Verdicts: does any user verdict in section 4 already rule on this (exactly or by analogy)?
   - Q6 Form: if it is worth adding, is this the best form, place and length, or should it change to fit?
4. Verdict: ADD, ADD WITH CHANGES (say what changes), or REJECT. A user verdict (section 4) beats everything else.
5. Log it in section 9 with an id, then build, then `daddy.py sync`.

## 3. Automatic checks (tools/daddy.py)
- A1 Redundancy: a label for a person or place the viewer already knows (named twice in the voice-over or shown in 2+
  shots before it) fails. Place labels warn after 2 prior shots.
- A2 Duplicate text: on-screen text that repeats the caption at the same moment fails.
- A3 Repetition: the same overlay kind or sound within 8 s warns; more than 3 of a kind (5 for hand callouts) warns.
- A4 Collision: an overlay region that overlaps the caption band (40 to 60% of the height) while a caption shows warns.
- A5 Facts: every number on screen must be in section 6, or it fails.
- A6 Rule breaks: nothing new on the black breather card; nothing but a speaker's name on the soundbite.
- A7 Purpose: a proposal with no stated purpose fails.
- A8 Sound files: a sound effect that contains several separate hits (each rising out of silence) fails; trim to one.
- A9 Splices: every voice-over split and soundbite edge must sit in real silence (under -50 dB); a cut inside a word fails.
  Word timings from the transcript are not enough: they glued "trillion dollars" and ended it 0.5 s early.
- A10 Moving targets: a callout drawn on a graphic that pushes in must zoom with it (`follow()` in the overlay), or it drifts.
