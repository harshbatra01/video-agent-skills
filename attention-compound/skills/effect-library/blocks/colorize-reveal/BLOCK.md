# Colorize reveal (test v1, 4 Nov 2026, waiting on the user's verdict)
Black and white with spin blur, colour sweeps from the left under a sideways smear, spin settles centre outward, glow fades.
Use: `python3 colorize_reveal.py in out.mp4 [--ss 42 --dur 3 --reveal 1.4 --spin 7 --smear 90]`. Video or image. About 65 s of machine time for 3 s. No install needed.
