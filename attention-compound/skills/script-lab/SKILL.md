# Script lab

One process from idea to a reviewed script. Built 20 Oct 2026 for a client job (e-commerce SEO); works
for any founder or expert reel.

## Who wins when sources disagree
1. The user's and the client's verdicts (dated, in the job's DADDY.md). Always.
2. Measured data: Rani's 11 reels with views, Cutroom's measured pages (`resources.md` lists them).
3. Practitioner advice without data: the Callaway transcripts. Useful, but he sells a tool and his "works every
   time" claims are not measured. Where he conflicts with 1 or 2, 1 or 2 wins.

## The house voice (user verdict, 20 Oct 2026)
- **Simple enough for anyone.** A 14-year-old and a store owner both follow every line. Rani's 4-cities and Gujarat
  reels are the bar: short sentences, everyday words, one picture per line.
- **The first line says what the video is.** The viewer knows the topic in one breath (Callaway: click
  confirmation, clarity).
- **Broad first, technical later.** The value can be about SEO, but the opening and most of the body are for
  anyone. Jargon (robots.txt, bots, conversion, GTIN) is cut or said in plain words; deeper technical detail goes
  in a later part of the script or a later video.

## The process (do the steps in order)
1. **Discovery** (`references/discovery.md`): a question a wide audience already asks, around names everyone knows.
2. **Facts with shock** (`references/discovery.md`, shock score): 5-10 verified facts, each scored for how few
   people know it. Only the high scorers make the outline.
3. **Packaging**: the title band (3-7 words) and the target emotion, written at the top of the draft.
4. **Outline**: pick a story structure (`references/scriptwriting.md`); bullet the units; each unit needs a fact
   most viewers don't know. No unique points, no script: go back to step 2.
5. **Hook** (`references/hooks.md`): pick a hook format the niche's outliers use, write it, run the 4 commandments
   and the house hook rules.
6. **Write** (`references/scriptwriting.md`): the body in the simple house voice, escalating to the biggest
   surprise, a turn line, one lived line at most, the CTA.
7. **Review** (`references/review.md`): the combined checklist and score, the job's `tools/script_check.py`, then
   the DADDY gate and the goal-alignment review.

## Rules that never bend
- Never invent a number, quote, poll or result; every number traces to a checked source.
- No superlative we can't source ("the biggest store on earth").
- No em dashes. No greeting. No "in this video" filler (Callaway's "today we're talking about" is long-form YouTube;
  in short form the title band and the first line do that job).
