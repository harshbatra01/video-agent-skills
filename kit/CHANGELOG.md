# Vision kit changelog

Newest first. Each entry says what changed in plain words, and "Action for you" lists anything to install or decide.

## v2026.10.15 (first shipment)

**Big picture**
- The first full copy of Kanishk's editing system: 10 skills, the house rules, 4 client files, a library of pipelines
  and research, and a Mac setup task list. Built from his editing work between 29 Sep and 14 Oct 2026, plus a
  friend's Mac skill set merged in (where the two differed, Kanishk's way stays the main one).

**Added**
- Skills: `premiere-ae-editing` (Premiere and After Effects by script, Windows and Mac), `editing-playbook` (how a job
  runs and how we cut, tell stories, hook, use B-roll, caption, handle reviews, plus the taste verdicts),
  `motion-design` (motion rules, beat-synced sports edits, an 18-effect After Effects library with taste marks),
  `research-and-sourcing`, `social-video-formats`, `production-automation`, `reel-scripts`, `podcast-trailers`,
  `meme-compilation-edit`, `kit-updater`.
- Client files in `~/VisionKit/kit/clients/`: Immufix, Vision Clipping music pages, Outcome, cracked sports edits.
  Each starts with "Start here": status and the next step.
- Library in `~/VisionKit/kit/library/`: pipelines (newsdesk, outcome-studio, edits-kit, ad-build-kit,
  youtube-research, review-pages), research reports and handoff notes.
- Weekly updates: `RECEIVED-LOG.md`, your own `mine/` layer, and `feedback-for-kanishk.md` for sending ideas back.

**Action for you**
- Install with START-HERE.md, then ask Claude to work through `~/VisionKit/kit/MAC-TASKS.md`. It tests every tool on
  your Mac and asks before installing anything.
- Decisions that are yours (marked in the skills): the rights calls in research-and-sourcing
  `licensing-and-rights.md`, whether to use the YouTube download route (it works around YouTube's bot check), and
  whether to install the optional Premiere bridge (it needs an Adobe setting that lets unsigned panels load).
- Accounts and keys you need your own of are listed in `~/VisionKit/kit/library/README.md` (types only).
