# Edit structure library (v1, 13 Oct 2026)

Measured with `tools/anatomy.py` on 22 reference edits (frame-level: shots, cuts, flashes, loudness, drops, breaths,
recurring motifs) plus the research bank. Times are from the real files in `refs/`. Pick a structure per edit from the
song, the subject and the footage; mix devices when they fit. Numbers are starting points, not laws, and the user's
verdicts beat these measurements (no black breath frames, the picture in a band rather than the full vertical frame).

## How to choose
| If the edit has... | Reach for |
|---|---|
| a song title or hook that names a measurable idea (time, light, charge, count, distance) or a sport prop (start lights, clock, scoreboard, added-time board) | 1. Progress motif |
| a quote or interview that frames the player (long-form YouTube) | 2. Quote-first tribute |
| a song-led promo (the song must stay audible) | 2 with the song's own intro lines as the "quote", or 1 + 5 |
| one iconic visual (eyes, pose, celebration) and a slowed / ethereal track, 12-18 s | 4. Single reveal |
| a history or era story, big design budget | 3. Two-act graphic to real |
| a song that repeats a phrase or drop | 6. Sequence callback |
| an 8-12 s hype loop | 7. Mograph strobe |
| lyrics that literally describe footage, or two subjects (senior and junior) | 9. Lyric story / mirror |
| none of the above | 5. Steady pulse with type |

## 1. Progress motif ("the DNA lights")
- One prop from the subject's world returns 3+ times and moves ONE STEP FURTHER each time, triggered by the same
  lyric or hook word; after the last step the edit releases into the payoff.
- DNA (Bela Films, 32.7 s): the word "DNA" lands over footage, then the F1 start-light grid: 2 lights at 13.97 s,
  4 at 17.31 s, all 5 at 20.69 s (each 0.7-0.8 s, 3.3-3.4 s apart). A 0.3 s breath (loudness dip) at 13.85 s right
  before the first one. After the 5th light: the race montage.
- Leclerc "Better Late Than Never" (40.5 s): the title becomes the prop: an analog clock at about 3, 9 and 23 s, the
  whole edit graded Ferrari red.
- Build: 3-4 inserts of 0.6-0.9 s spaced 3-4 s; state advances each time; release within one beat of the last state.
- The prop must come from the subject's REAL world. A made-up prop (a phone-charging battery for "I'm still charging",
  pilot v1-v3) was rejected by the user as a gimmick.
- Variant that worked, "story in numbers" (pilot v4): his real numbers, each huge BEHIND him (matte cutout in front),
  slammed on a strong hit with a 1-frame white silhouette, a small caption in the bottom bar saying what it is:
  2007 (born) on "gift since birth", 304 (his neighbourhood's postcode) on "I came from dirt", 16 (Euros record) after
  the goal. The device is the effect the user liked most; the numbers carry the story without extra footage. Facts
  must be checked before they go on screen.

## 2. Quote-first tribute
- Spoken soundbite(s) with keyword captions over a quiet beat, sparse cuts, then a breath and a dense montage.
- Jordan "SDP Interlude" (25 s): one speech shot 0-15.6 s, black breath at 15.6 s (62%), montage 16.5-25 s at 3-3.5
  cuts/s. Ja Morant (69.8 s): quotes and designed cards 0-48.7 s, drop at 48.7 s (70%). Mbappe "Nightcrawler"
  (56 s): press conference 0-33 s, montage 34-52 s. Messi "SHOCK" (35 s): hook 0-9.3 s (26%), wave 1, breath at
  about 24 s, wave 2, B&W end card 30-35 s. Curry "Runaway" (49.6 s): interviews 0-23 s, montage 24-49 s.
- For a song promo the song must stay audible: use the song's own intro lines or hook as the "quote" (lyric
  captions), keep the montage for the payoff zone.

## 3. Two-act: graphic to real
- Spain World Cup promo (62 s): flat-colour 2D act (silhouettes, colour blocks) 0-34.6 s, drop (+21 dB), real footage
  with kinetic numerals, red title cards 54-62 s.

## 4. Single reveal / push-in
- Gojo "Eyes" (EPHIR, 16.3 s, 4.4M views): only 8 shots. A slow push from a wide snowy shot to an extreme close-up
  of the eyes by 9-11 s, then black from 12 s so it loops. Less is more when the reveal is strong.

## 5. Steady pulse with type
- Speed x Ronaldo (sanchezae, 30.6 s, 2.5M): about 1 cut per second all the way, 24 flashes spread evenly, giant
  words and composites every 2-4 s, 2-3 s outro card. REINCARNATED (20.6 s): ~1.2 cuts/s, title word at 2.5 s, "44"
  number at 4.3 s, start lights at 5.8 s, lyric words over footage, two short B&W hits.

## 6. Sequence callback
- iluxhaep "Montagem Tomada" (32.7 s): a 5-shot sequence at 10.4-15.5 s replays at 20.3-25.3 s (9.84 s later)
  when the music repeats, with heavier effects. Recognition plus escalation makes the second drop hit harder.

## 7. Mograph strobe
- lanzdoesvisual "4 x 4" (8.2 s): 31 shots (3.8 cuts/s), white / black / orange solid frames alternating with
  collage cutouts and a giant "4"; the 1 s pattern plays twice.

## 8. Hook burst + slow section
- kma4 "Love me X Neymar" (89 s): 7 cuts/s burst in the first 2 s, a long slow type-led middle, montage at the end.

## 9. Lyric story / mirror
- anwftbl "His Eyes Were Like Mine" (19.3 s, 856k): Saka and Nwaneri mirrored, lyrics literal to the footage,
  8 shots, a white flash, logo end.

## 10. Fight cold open / silent stare-down (research pass 2, built as edit 01)
- 0-2.5 s a wordless B&W stare with a quiet name; a hard cut into colour on a snare; exchanges get shorter; the
  connecting punch on the lyric hit with impact frames; freeze on the reaction; end on the stare and the thesis line.
- Twoc_ "AURA IN UFC" (3.2M): "the silence is what people replay". Our version adds a hit list: real opponents'
  names (from the footage's own captions) struck through one per knockout on "get them all ticked off".

## 11. Bar build + beat-switch reset (built as edit 02)
- 0-2 s face; one play per beat-pair escalating (arrival, handle, finish, absurd); the song's beat switch is the reset
  (poster-silhouette strobe, B&W, a quiet cinematic shot, the title); the new beat drops on its strongest hit with
  colour back and clips half as long; biggest play last, slowed, frozen and held past the line.
- Itai Rosen's Ja Morant "Murder On My Mind" (2.6M). Songs with a famous switch (SICKO MODE) carry this base.

## 12. Theme compilation + loop (built as edit 03)
- One kind of moment (celebrations) across the edit, the song's hook landing on the matching gesture (a shrug on
  "¿Y qué fue?"); open and end inside the same shot so the loop seam disappears; the song title in the top bar.
- Yamal x "Y Que Fue?" (181.8M), Dkrzinn celebrations (12.6M).

## Rules measured across the set
- Hits land on the beat or slightly EARLY: Speed x Ronaldo cuts -0.3 frames, flashes -0.5; DNA flashes -0.75.
- Cut on strong kicks and snares, not on a metronome grid (grid sync was at chance level in every edit).
- A breath before the big moment: 0.3-0.6 s of quiet just before the drop or the first motif (in the references it
  measured as a loudness dip or a black frame: DNA 5.6 s and 13.85 s, Jordan 15.6 s, Messi about 24 s). In our edits a
  breath is a quiet shot, a freeze or an audio dip, never a black frame: the user rejected the black breath ("black
  frame near 20", v3 review, 13 Oct 2026) and black frames are a standing ban in these edits (a single black frame
  inside a strobe recipe is the only use). Kit copy reworded 14 Oct 2026 to match the motion-design skill
  (effect-vocabulary-and-taste.md).
- Payoff: long YouTube edits pay off after 55-70% (quote-first ones as late as 60-70%); short edits under 20 s drop
  at 20-45%.
- End cards 1.5-5 s, often B&W or black with the maker's mark (Hero Edit, Bela Films); the song card plays this role
  for us.
- Our GASS x Mbappe v1 for comparison: 26 shots in 20.6 s (median 0.62 s), drop at 4.1 s (20%), no motif, no
  breath, 3.2 s static end card.
