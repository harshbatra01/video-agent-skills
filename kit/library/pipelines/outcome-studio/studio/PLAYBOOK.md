# Outcome pages playbook (13 Oct 2026)

What to post, in what shape, and the lines we never cross. Built from research 11 to 19 and the client brief. Jaden's bar still rules: a post earns its place only if it is genuinely interesting, timely and carries a real number.

## 1. The pattern that wins
- Lead with a topic people already care about (the Ballon d'Or, tonight's big game, the Fed, the Anthropic IPO) and a game mechanic (pick a side, guess, rank, race). The real % is the twist, not the headline subject. (#ballondor 34.45B views vs #footballodds 1.6M.)
- Plain data cards flop on small pages (daily wraps under 300 views, whale alerts 0 to 4 likes). Every post needs a hook, a surprise or a fight.
- Make every post twice: a short video for TikTok (video gets about 5.6x the views of photo posts there) and a carousel for Instagram (carousels get about 9x the saves). The studio renders both.
- Slide 2 must work on its own: Instagram re-shows unswiped carousels starting from slide 2.
- Series numbering ("Part 3") on quizzes; same first 3 seconds within a format.

## 3. Week rhythm (draft)
Mon: AI vs crowd / upset retro. Tue and Wed (UCL): matchday card morning, pick a side midday, replay or upset at full time. Thu: Europa card, NFL. Fri: race swing round-up, market update. Sat: quiz, tier list. Sun (NFL): price tags or cheapest dreams, replay of the biggest swing. 1 to 3 posts a day per page to start; judge a format after 30 to 50 posts, not 5.
Dated hooks: CPI 14 Oct 12:30 UTC; UCL 13 and 14 Oct; Europa 15 Oct; Ballon d'Or ceremony 26 Oct (London Palladium); FOMC 28 Oct 18:00 UTC (last decision was a HIKE on 16 Sep); Anthropic IPO reports (investor day 14 Oct reported, not confirmed).

## 4. Captions
- First line = the hook with the number. Then one line of context. Then a question (ask, never order). Then the source + time stamp + risk line. 3 to 5 hashtags (topic, league, #football).
- `#ad` goes first (brand.json "disclosure"): paid or per-view posts are ads under UK, US and platform rules. Also switch on the platform's paid-partnership / content-disclosure toggle when posting.
- Never: "bet", "betting", "odds" in sportsbook style (+150), "tips", "picks", "lock", "guaranteed", "free money", "invest", "easy money", "you could have made $X". Say "chance", "the money", "traders", "call".
- Finance and macro posts carry "Not financial advice".
- Facts outside Outcome's data (form, quotes, news) need a source and date (in the caption or the slide footer).

## 5. Lines we never cross
1. Real numbers only, time stamped, from the market at build time. No invented stats or poll results (run a real Story poll first if you want a poll number).
2. Never cover Outcome's chart, the market names or the Outcome logo (the How-to uses a phone frame for this reason).
3. No fake or staged trades, no mock trading screens, no fake DMs (skip the brief's "AI-written reply from Kimchi" idea; Kimchi is not an Outcome trader).
4. Usernames and P&L cards of real traders only once the client says yes in writing.
5. No cash piles, win celebrations or youth-coded hooks. Losses shown as honestly as wins.
6. "Zero fees" and "world's fastest" are the client's own claims; research found a small close/settle fee since about 5 Sep 2026 and no speed benchmark. The CTA line is in brand.json: change it there the day the client approves new wording.
7. Do not aim posts at the countries Outcome blocks (handled by the project; do not hint at VPNs).
