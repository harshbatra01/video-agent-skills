## Licences used

- **Mixkit Stock Video Free License** (https://mixkit.co/license/#videoFree): free for commercial and personal use, including ads, social posts and YouTube; no attribution required. Every Mixkit clip here was checked on its own page and carries the Free licence. Mixkit also has a "Restricted" licence (personal use only, no ads or company social posts); those clips were excluded, which ruled out most of Mixkit's Bitcoin, stock chart, lightning and neon city clips.
- **Coverr License** (https://coverr.co/license): free for commercial and non-commercial use, no attribution required. It does not allow building a competing stock service or using the videos to train AI models, and it does not clear trademarks, landmarks or property. Only free (non-premium), non-AI clips were taken.

Selection rules applied with a frame check of every clip: no identifiable faces in focus, no readable brand logos, club crests or broadcast graphics, no famous people.

## Sources that could not be used

- Pexels and Pixabay: both answer scripted requests with a Cloudflare challenge (HTTP 403), so they were skipped. Their APIs need a free key.
- Coverr search API needs a key; the public tag pages (coverr.co/stock-video-footage/<tag>) were used instead.
