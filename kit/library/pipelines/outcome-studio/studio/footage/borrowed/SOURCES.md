# Borrowed footage sample pack: sources

Downloaded 14 Oct 2026 for the Outcome sample pack. TikTok clips came through urlebird (search) and tikwm (no-watermark download, HD where offered); photos through the Wikimedia Commons API; one lifestyle clip from Coverr. Views are as of download. Every clip was checked on a contact sheet. Files ending _clean or with a time range in the notes were cut with ffmpeg (H.264, CRF 17, AAC audio kept); files ending _src are the untouched downloads.

Licences in short: TikTok material is other people's footage with no licence (the agency approved borrowed use). CC BY-SA 4.0 photos need a credit line such as 'Photo: Bryan Berlin, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons', and edited versions fall under the same licence. CC0 needs nothing. The Getty and beIN photos are placeholders only. Audio: the Audio column names the TikTok sound. 'original sound' under the uploader's own handle is usually the clip's own audio (crowd, commentary); a song title, or 'original sound' under another account (for example ph0nkgam3r, trendformusic, alsa.music), usually means music was laid on top, so mute or replace it.
