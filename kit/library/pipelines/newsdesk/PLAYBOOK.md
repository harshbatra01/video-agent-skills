# Page manager playbook: @zonegbp and @beatswitchfm (from 8 Oct 2026)

The job is growing two brand-new pages, not running a clever system. A post earns its place only if people stop, watch, and send it. Built from Jaden's reviews (8 Oct), our own scan data, and research/08 to 10.

## 1. Who each page is for
- **@zonegbp**: UK rap and culture first. The beef, the charts, the clips UK fans are arguing about today. Fast, plain, a bit cheeky.
- **@beatswitchfm**: the biggest rap and music stories for UK fans, US included, plus money and music-business stories. Slightly more "news desk".
- Neither page posts filler. A day with 2 strong posts beats a day with 5 average ones.

## 2. The interest test (run it before building anything)
Score each 0 to 2. Build only at 7 or more out of 10.

| | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fresh** | broke in the last 12 h, or happens today | last 24 to 36 h | older, evergreen |
| **Tension** | beef, legal stakes, big money, a record, a shock | a surprise or reveal | routine (release, throwback, wholesome) |
| **Audience** | UK rap core, or a global story UK fans argue about | adjacent (pop, sport crossover) | niche |
| **First frame** | a face or motion plus a 5-word hook | a decent photo | no strong visual |
| **Added fact** | a number, time or context nobody else put on it | clear framing | repeats the source |

What our scan data says so far (it grows every run, see the scan's "What's working" section): court and legal stories beat their page's usual views most (about 1.4 to 1.7x, a third break out); fashion and fit posts get the most reposts and saves; throwbacks and plain release posts underperform. Small samples, so re-check weekly.

## 3. Engagement, done honestly
- Opinion VS posts with nothing behind them ("Central Cee or Dave?") are empty for a page with no audience. A VS only runs when it is tied to a live story (a beef this week, a chart race) and carries a real number.
- **Never invent numbers or poll results.** One fake stat called out in the comments ends a news page's credibility, and made-up figures presented as real are misinformation. Honest versions that do the same job:
  1. Real stats that start arguments: chart spots, streams, number-one counts, sales ("Only 4 rap songs in Apple Music UK's top 100").
  2. Our own Story polls, then post the real result with the vote count: "62% of 340 voters on our Story picked Dave" (our followers, not a survey). Poll one day, result the next. People argue with results.
  3. Real quotes from the scene (an artist or DJ picking a side).
  4. Our own record, once we have one: "We called 7 of our last 10 chart moves."
- Ask, never order (research/08). The tension goes in the headline itself, not in "comment below".

## 4. Formats and retention (research/10)
The first 3 seconds decide everything: Instagram's skip rate counts everyone who leaves before 3 s, and there is no CTR on Reels, so frame 0 is our thumbnail, our hook and our click.

**Reel, second by second (7 to 15 s news reel)**
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| 0.0 s | Headline fully on screen from frame 1 (our overlays already are), the artist's face big, sound starts on the hook. No logo intro, no fade-in. |
| 0 to 3 s | Something moves in the first second. Lead with the name and the conflict ("Digga D's reply drops tonight"), not the context. |
| 3 to 7 s | The fact plus our added number, time or chart spot (this is what makes it original). |
| 7 to 12 s | Proof: the video peak, the interview line. Speech gets captions, below the headline. |
| Last 2 s | A clock or an open question, never "comment X". End close to frame 0 so it loops. No outro card. |

- **Headline length**: people read about 15 to 20 characters a second over video, so a 70-character headline needs 4 to 5 s. Keep headlines to 6 to 10 words, front-loaded; long ones only on clips over 12 s.
- **Safe zone**: the profile grid crops reels to 3:4 (240 px off the top and bottom) and the app buttons cover the right edge and the bottom. Keep the headline and face between about 15% and 65% of the height. (Our tweet and split layouts sit there; the uk video headline sits lower, a template change to test.)
- **Length is a test, not a rule**: brand data does not show shorter winning. Week 2 tests 8 to 12 s against 20 to 30 s, judged on skip rate and average watch time.
- **Carousels**: slide 1 works like frame 0 (claim or number, a face). 2 to 3 slides for news, 5 to 8 for rankings and timelines. One fact per slide, key words and numbers coloured (automatic for numbers; mark words with `*...*`). Make the last slide the one worth sending.
- **Covers**: use frame 0 so the grid looks consistent. Pin 3 posts that show what each page is about.
- **Polarising, honestly**: two named sides, one real fact, a clock, an open question ("Digga or Morrisson: who won the week?"). Us-vs-them framing is the strongest share driver in the research; orders to comment are the thing Instagram demotes.

## 5. Growth levers for a new page (research/09)
- **No follower gate.** Every new reel goes to a small test audience and widens only if people watch, like and send it (Mosseri's top signals: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach). So each post is judged on its own, from post one.
- **Originality decides whether we get recommended at all** (30 Apr 2026). Instagram's test: new information, context or a point of view not obvious from the original. Our own tweet cards, headlines with an added fact, graphics and VS cards count; a logo, subtitles, a credit or a screenshot of someone else's post never do. Never post the same asset on both pages (it reads as a repost).
- **Trial Reels**: check for the toggle on day 1. Use them to test two openings of the day's best story (only the first 3 seconds change).
- **Speed is the edge** a small page can win on: Kurrco's biggest moment was being first to confirm a release date; GBMC posts about 5 to 6 times a day, roughly 1.5 h behind the artist. Confirm first, then go.
- **Other people's audiences**: tag artists when the post is about them, one Collab post with a small UK artist from week 3, and reply early under the big pages' posts on X with our angle (guesswork, cheap to test).
- **Volume**: start at 2 strong posts a day per page (1 reel, 1 card), not 5. Add more only when the skip rate stays under our median. Quality first, as Jaden said.
- **No this-or-that in the feed** until the pages have an audience; polls live in Stories (followers only) and feed us real numbers for later posts.

### Metrics we judge by (Insights, per post, at 24 h and 72 h)
- **Skip rate** (views that leave in the first 3 seconds; replaced view rate in Aug 2025). Real data from about 140K brand Reels in 2026: 65.5% average for pages with 1K to 5K followers, 60.5% at 100K+. The "under 40%" lines online are vendor myths. Target: under 60% by week 4, then beat our own median. A reel with a cliff in the first 3 s gets a new frame 0.
- **Average watch time (AVD)**: the 2026 average is about 8.5 s. Read the Retention chart at 24 h: a drop in 0 to 3 s means the hook, a drop mid-reel means cut it shorter.
- **Sends, follows and profile visits per 1,000 reach**: keep formats in our top quarter, cut the bottom. A typical profile visit converts to a follow about 13.5% of the time.
- Reach rate at 1K to 5K followers: Reels about 9.8%, carousels 8.8%, single images 7.0%. So reels are the growth engine and cards support them.
- Instagram publishes no benchmarks: after 20 posts per page we switch to our own medians.

### 30-day launch plan
- **Week 1, baseline**: 6 launch posts per page, then 1 news reel (15 to 30 s) and 1 card a day each, 6 to 8pm UK (breaking news as soon as verified). Log the metrics above.
- **Week 2, hooks**: two openings for the day's best story (Trial Reels if available). Drop the weakest format. One sendable carousel (a beef timeline or a chart recap).
- **Week 3, double down**: 2 reels and 1 card a day if skip rate holds. First Collab post. Daily Story poll.
- **Week 4, decide**: keep formats above median on sends and follows per reach, cut anything with three reels in a row above median skip rate, set targets from our own numbers.

## 6. Daily and weekly routine
- Scan at least twice a UK day (morning and late afternoon); beef and court stories break in the evening and speed is the whole game for a new news page.
- Pick with the interest test, build, post in the window. Late breaking UK stories go out as soon as they are verified, schedule or not.
- Weekly review (Sunday): the user sends Insights screenshots per post (reach, plays, skip rate, average watch time, shares, saves, follows). I log them, compare story types and formats, and adjust this playbook.
