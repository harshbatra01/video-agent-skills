# Captions and headlines: house style

Built from how the leaders write (RapTV, Kurrco, Say Cheese, Daily Loud, GRM Daily, Mixtape Madness, Link Up TV, GBMC). Keep it plain. Do not overdo emoji, slang or hype.

## On-image headline
- Lead with the name(s). 6 to 14 words. Present tense. Facts only.
- Cards (uk, raptv, list, chart): the template uppercases. Mark 1 to 3 hook words with `*...*` (song title, a number, DEBUT, BACK). Song titles in single quotes: 'GASS'.
- Split video caption box: uppercase, max 2 lines, ends with 1 or 2 emoji (🔥 ‼️ 👀 😳 😭).
- Meme header and tweet card: sentence case, conversational, one emoji at most.
- Labels (small pill above the headline): NEW MUSIC, THROWBACK, HAPPY BIRTHDAY, CHARTS, COURT, BREAKING, INTERVIEW.
- Never: em dashes, invented facts, "allegedly" claims without a press or police source, anything hinting a song is being promoted.

## Urgency (Jaden's review, 8 Oct 2026: "need better caption, more urgency"; the user: only where it fits)
- Only for time-sensitive stories: a release just out, a drop or premiere later today, breaking news. Those read like breaking news: JUST DROPPED, OUT NOW, DROPS TONIGHT AT 7PM, TODAY, 🚨.
- Not for throwbacks, quotes, debates, chart milestones, features or wholesome stories: those lead with the fact and end on a question or a reaction. Never 🚨 on every post (it stops meaning anything).
- Put the time in when there is one (TONIGHT AT 7PM, TODAY AT 5PM UK) and post before it (`when` field).
- Numbers beat adjectives: #68, 1M VIEWS, 4 YEARS. The key number goes in the accent colour (`*#68*`).
- Platform stories get the platform logo badge (`logo: applemusic`) instead of a CHARTS pill.

## Post caption (text under the post)
- 1 or 2 short lines: the fact, then a reaction or a question. 1 or 2 emoji.
- Opinion posts end with a question: "Thoughts? 👀", "What score are you giving it?", "Who's running this back?" Ask, never order (see Engagement).
- Credit the clip or photo source when known: "🎥: @handle".
- Hashtags: 0 to 2 at the end (artist names, #ukrap). None is fine (they add context, not reach).
- UK slang only where it is natural ("cooked", "levels", "hard"). Avoid AI-sounding words (elevate, unleash, dive in, iconic) and tired clichés.

## Engagement and reach (from research/08-engagement.md, 8 Oct 2026)
- Reach comes from watch time, likes and sends (sends matter most for non-followers). Make posts people want to send to a mate.
- Originality (Instagram, 30 Apr 2026): accounts that mostly repost lose recommendations. Every post adds something the source does not show: a number, date, time, chart spot, background or the why. A logo, border, subtitles or a credit alone do not count. Our own graphics (VS, list, chart, quote cards) count fully.
- Ask, never order. "Who's winning this one?" is fine. "Comment W or L", "Tag a mate", "Like if", "Rate it 1-10" are engagement bait and lose recommendations. Vary the question; a strong fact can just end with no question.
- 1 to 2 emoji, 👇 only now and then.
- Per page per day: 4 to 5 feed posts, one engagement-first (VS, ranking, this or that), plus 2 to 3 Stories with a poll (`story: true` makes the 9:16 still; add the poll sticker in-app).
- Carousels get the most engagement and saves: `slides` adds photos after the card, each with one line of context.
- UK times: Mon to Thu 5 to 9pm, Fri 5 to 6pm, Sat 3 to 4pm and 5 to 7pm, Sun 6 to 7pm (Sunday best). Time-sensitive news goes out as soon as it is verified; engagement posts 6 to 8pm. Put the window in `when`.
- Engagement posts: pin our own take as the first comment (`pin`).
