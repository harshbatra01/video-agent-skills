# Effects catalogue (lane 06): the kinds of effect used in high-end edits

Written 13 Oct 2026 for Vision Clipping (vertical sports edits that carry a client's song). This is the catalogue lane: Section 1 is a list of 73 techniques (one "###" block each) instead of numbered standout edits, as the lead asked. Sections 2-4 follow the brief.

## How to read this file
- Every link below was returned by YouTube metadata (yt-dlp search, channel lists or single-video info) and then re-fetched once; titles, creators, views and dates are as of 13 Oct 2026. Nothing was downloaded.
- Evidence labels on each example: **[SEEN]** I played it in a browser and looked at frames sampled every 0.1-2.5 s (timestamps are good to about 0.3 s; effects shorter than the gap, such as shakes and 1-frame strobes, can be missed). **[MEASURED]** a frame-by-frame luminance scan of playback. **[TITLE]** the effect is named by the title, chapters, on-screen labels or description only; the frames were not checked.
- Kinds: EDIT (real sports/other edit), MV (official music video), DEMO (effect demo or pack preview), TUT (tutorial), GUIDE (multi-effect guide with chapters), PACK (overlay/transition pack), CLIPS (scenepack footage), REF (non-video reference).
- Tags are the brief's tag words; "NEW:" marks tags I had to add (black-flash, shape-wipe, ai-clips).
- Each entry has Looks like / Tags / Examples / Trending or dated / Music sync. "Music sync" is the usual editor convention (kick, snare, hat, 808, vocal, drop); where a tutorial states a workflow I quote its chapter order, otherwise it is general practice, not measured.

## Method and limits (read before trusting a gap)
- The WebSearch quota for this run was used up by the shared agents, so discovery came from yt-dlp YouTube searches (over 100 queries), channel lists and tutorial chapters, plus about 45 browser playbacks. Instagram/TikTok could not be read, Reddit was blocked, so there are no Reddit threads and no TikTok/Reels links (TikTok/IG creators are listed from tutorial titles only).
- Effects checked in a real edit: 44 of 73 entries. Demo/tutorial only: 10. Titles only: 19. The index table below shows which.
- Weakest spots (no frame-checked sports or AMV example): invert flash, stutter, shake (too brief for sampling), datamosh (the one real example is a streamer clip), pixel sort, slit-scan, colour pop (the example is a music video), scribble/doodle, VHS, film grain. They are real, named techniques with tutorials; the example is a demo or a title.
- Total distinct videos cited: 180.

## 1. Standout edits (catalogue of effects)

**FLASH**

### White flash (dip-to-white / flash frame)
- Looks like: 1-6 frames of full-white (or near-white) over a cut or hit, falling off in 2-4 frames; stacked 2-3 times on the biggest hits.
- Tags: white-flash, exposure-pop

[SEEN] bursts confirm two of them: 10.56-10.59 s two pure-white frames after a yellow light-leak build (10.38-10.53 s) cut to a 'HERO EDIT PRESENTS' card; 16.27 s one full-white frame hiding a cut (see match cut).

## 4. Patterns noticed

- **Two winning structures.** (a) Quote-first tribute: a talking-head quote with word-by-word coloured captions for the first 5-25 s, then the beat (P3DITS Curry, R4VE Jordan, cristiano7aep, Hero Edit Yamal/Saka/Mbappe, Dr1zzy). (b) Effect-dense montage with typography (iluxhaep, Hero Edit Messi, sanchezae). For a song-led promo the song's own hook line can play the role of the quote.
- **Density is modulated, not constant.** Hero Edit Messi has 12 near-white flash events in about 20 s; sanchezae's Speed x Ronaldo has 2 in 30 s and leans on typography instead. The strongest edits change what they stack per section; the reference edit's every-beat flash plus shake is the average, not the ceiling.
- **Typography is the premium tell in 2025-26.** Text behind the subject, 3D letters in the scene, giant cropped words, one lyric word per beat (sanchezae, iluxhaep, Dr1zzy, Shadey Edit). It costs roto time rather than plugins, which is why it separates edits.
- **Colour is a structural device.** Colour/gradient flashes and hue-cycle bursts on hits; B&W or tint for a quiet section; a single accent colour on B&W at music-video level (Lyrical Lemonade); a B&W end card carrying the creator tag (Hero Edit, P3DITS).
- **A repeatable transition is the signature.** Hero Edit reuses a lime/yellow blow-out plus vertical smear (Messi 25.6 s, Saka 20.6 s and 23.9 s); sanchezae's seamless transitions have a 'like Sanchezae' tutorial. Pick one signature transition for the Vision Clipping look.
- **Great transitions are stacks of 4-5 small effects over 6-12 frames.** Chapter lists show freeze frame + mask + white flash + pop-out + camera shake + lens blur (CAF, SLIPTIC); split clone glitch = speed ramp + roto + freeze + clone + venetian blind (Peter Sarellas).
- **Roto cut-out compositing is the 2025-26 skill gap.** Background swaps (clouds, particle fields), neon outlines, text-behind and masked shake all need a clean matte; tutorials from FicTic, Olufemii and Eagle Stan are all about it.
- **Tutorial channels name the month's trend, with a lag.** 'Microwave' started as slowed edits (Sep 2024) and was taught Dec 2024-Sep 2025; the freshest wave is Aug 2026: depth style (HOBO and MT_EDITZ 17 Aug, Edits27 21 Aug), rays shake (26 Aug), black smoke (19 Aug), time slice (30 Aug), plus Seedance AI clips (9 Aug). CAF Edits posts the 'trending' names weekly.
- **Replay mechanics exist.** 'Watch this at 0.5x/0.25x' edits (SweeperKeeperShorts 1.0M views, CR7LAND 1.9M) and long slowed 'aura' edits are built to be rewatched; relevant to a goal of making people replay the song, but slowed versions change the track.
- **Sound design is treated as part of the edit.** SFX packs for football edits (NO7AM 123k views), 'make your edit sound 2x better' (CAF, Apr 2026), 'marking beats' as step one of velocity and audio-sync tutorials (rdylt09 chapter order, Eagle Stan chapter 'Marking Beats').
- **Footage quality is a visible bar.** '[4K]' in titles, Topaz upscaling tutorials (FicTic https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5r43QGsQxww, heisty https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8J1o0443lQw, AnasFTBL Jul 2026 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UgWuP2RyPh4), 60-120 fps scenepacks (Reverse_Ftbl 'Neymar JR 4K Clips' https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQr86FZwHD4, 1.7M views) and the 120 fps interpolation wave in CapCut; low-res source with heavy effects reads cheap.
- **Signature outro on almost every elite edit.** Anime-eye stinger (iluxhaep), red glitch 'HERO EDIT' tag, 'R4VE' title, logo mark (sanchezae), 'P3DITS' on B&W. Our equivalent is the song card; the pattern says the end card is part of the style, not an afterthought.
