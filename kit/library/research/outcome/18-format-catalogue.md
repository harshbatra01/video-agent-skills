# 18 Outcome faceless format catalogue (13 Oct 2026)

Merged from reports 11 (prediction and trading), 12 (sports), 13 (finance and crypto), 14 (mechanics), 15 (data triggers), 16 (transplants) and 17 (my ideas). Numbers are the best verified example per format, read 13 Oct 2026; see the source report for links and dates.

## What the research says, in 6 lines
1. **Topics win, "odds" content doesn't.** #ballondor has 34.45B views, #footballodds 1.6M. Lead with the topic and a game (quiz, poll, ranking, race), and make the real % the twist.
2. **Plain data cards from small pages flop** (daily wraps 22 to 276 views, whale alerts 0 to 4 likes). Every post needs a hook, a surprise or a fight.
3. **Small pages break out with game mechanics:** a 7-slide quiz got 29.6M plays on a 22K page; pick-a-side polls hit 3.8M to 7.5M with 2.3% to 8.2% share rates; matchday % cards got 4.07M on a 287K page.
4. **Outcome has two things nobody else has:** real price history (Kane 50% -> 66%, Yamal 43% -> 28% in 8 days) and real trader results. Almost nobody makes "the line moved" content.
5. **Make two cuts of everything:** TikTok gives video about 5.6x the views of photo posts; Instagram gives carousels about 9x the saves. Slide 2 needs its own hook (Instagram re-serves carousels from slide 2).
6. **Start the price archive now.** Market history disappears the moment a market settles (Hyperliquid returns nothing), so chart replays, swings and upsets only work if we save prices while markets are live.

Legend: Proof = best verified result for a small or new page (A strong, B some, C none yet). Only us = needs Outcome data nobody else has. Auto = Full (data + template), Semi (one human pick or fact), Manual. Detectors D1 to D15 are in report 15.

## 8. Test plan
Each launch format gets 5 posts in 2 weeks. Judge on plays per follower and share rate (benchmarks: quizzes 1.3% to 2.0% shares, pick-a-side 2.3% to 8.2%). Keep the top 4, swap in wave 2. Same first 3 seconds within a format, numbered Part N. Rotate templates every 10 to 15 posts and never post the same file on two pages (originality rules).

## 9. Guardrails (short)
Real, time-stamped numbers only; skip thin books (Yes prices must add to about 100%, no 50% placeholders). Never "bet", "odds +150", "tips", "picks", "lock", "invest". Never cover the chart or logo. No cash piles or win celebrations. Risk line in captions. Losses as honest as wins. Fee wording ("zero fees") still needs the client's OK. Usernames on cards need the client's OK (O13 to O15, Top 3, P&L, Trader story).
