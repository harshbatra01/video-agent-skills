# 25 Borrowed-footage formats for Outcome (research, 14 Oct 2026)

Job: the user rejected data cards and motion graphics as boring and not native. This file looks only at viral formats built on borrowed or background footage (movie scenes, match clips, streamer and podcast clips, lifestyle B-roll, memes, AI footage), in niches that fit Outcome: money and lifestyle, trading and crypto, sports betting humour, football, aura and sigma edits, and anything that can carry a chance. Each format has frame-checked examples and a recipe for an Outcome version. Files 20 and 22 are not repeated; where a format overlaps one of theirs (K1, K2, K7, V10), this file adds the footage-based version with verified frames, and says so.

## Read me in 7 lines

1. I downloaded 74 videos (69 TikTok, 5 YouTube Shorts) into `raw\25\videos\` and looked at every one as a timestamped contact sheet in `raw\25\sheets\` (`<name>.jpg` = 20 frames, dense in the first 3 seconds; `combo_*.jpg` = 7 or 8 frames per video for the lighter checks). Every "Seconds" line below comes from those frames.
2. All numbers were read on 14 Oct 2026. TikTok: views, likes, shares, date, length and sound name from the tikwm single-video API. Followers: urlebird, a public TikTok mirror (rounded, for example "2.89K", and counted today, not on the post date). YouTube: yt-dlp and the watch page.
3. Discovery: TikTok's own search results read through urlebird (about 10 results per query, 172 queries), plus 45 YouTube Shorts searches. TikTok itself does not load on this PC (blocked in India) and tikwm's search endpoint sits behind Cloudflare.
4. Ratio = views divided by the page's followers (subscribers on YouTube). The sample is biased towards hits on purpose, so ratios show what is possible, not what is typical.
5. Sound: I could not listen. Sound lines give TikTok's own sound name; "original sound - X" means the uploader's own audio (often a song mix, a voice-over or both). All YouTube sound is UNVERIFIED.
6. Outcome numbers in the copy are either from file 19 (dated and verified there: Kane's Ballon d'Or price 48.6% on 5 Oct, 55.5% on 9 Oct 17:30 UTC, about 66% after the 9 Oct jump, about 62% on 13 Oct; Yamal 43.1% on 5 Oct, 37.4% on 9 Oct) or a [bracket] to be filled from live data at render time. No number here is invented.
7. Client rules apply to every recipe: never "bet", never cover the graph or logo, no profit promises, NFA on finance, "#ad" first in captions, real app screens only.

## 2. What the 74 videos say

1. **Percent and probability are already meme language.** The NFL runs a repeatable "win probability" template (two opened: 3.54M and 2.16M; a third titled "A probability thrill ride", 1.68M, not opened). An F1 page memes a probability board (397K). Clubs (Real Madrid 2.58M, FC Bayern) joined the "I was 80% happy" percent template. A page called @theoddsof, about 3 weeks old, hit 603,583 with "The Odds of Making it to the NBA". Nobody in this sample uses a live market price for any of these: open ground for Outcome.
2. **The default look is a letterboxed 16:9 clip on a black 9:16 canvas** with text above it or on it (Margin Call, Boiler Room, Adin Ross, parlay meme, POV betting meme, manifestation, Ballon d'Or AI film). Full-screen vertical crops are the minority. This makes borrowed footage cheap to produce: no reframing.
3. **Text is small.** The biggest hits use one line in TikTok's default white font with a black outline, 3 to 12 words, often lowercase slang and crying or skull emojis (😭💔☠️🥶). Big caption plates appear only in podcast clips. Several hits carry the hook in the post caption, not on screen (New York "sign" 15.2M, Powell meme 5.0M, luxury montages).
4. **Sound:** 53 of 69 TikToks use "original sound" (41 the uploader's own, 12 lifted from another account); 16 use a named library track, mostly slowed or sped-up songs ("still love you (Slowed)", "Jane! (Hoodtrap)", "PROS BANDIDO - ULTRA SLOWED", "Shock The World - Slowed-Melody Version", "The Final Countdown").
5. **Length:** median 26.5 s; 33 of 74 are 20 s or shorter. A second cluster runs long on purpose (21 over 60 s, up to 12.9 min): full movie scenes, Reddit stories, text stories. TikTok rewards both ends.
6. **Small pages win often:** 19 pages under 5,000 followers in this sample, 28 of 73 posts at 100x or more. Examples: @boruyti69 6.1M on 1,640; @c.moodboard 3.8M on 2,970; @tjrrare 2.4M on 1,860; @clipperzcom0 509K on 998.
7. **Bans are visible.** Two of the biggest football edit pages advertise past bans: @kylianftblbannedat185k (the handle) and @hudsfooty.0 (sound credit "Huds got banned at 220k"). Broadcast-footage pages lose accounts.
8. **In this sample, the prediction-market posts that went viral in this window were face-on-camera, not borrowed footage:** "Kalshi is your ticket out" 606,667 (12 Oct 2026), "This is the best BTC strategy on Kalshi (15 minute)" 260,624 on 992 (30 Sep 2026), a Ronaldo lookalike's Polymarket partner post 223,413 on 4.7M (6 Oct 2026). The first is also the kind of profit line Outcome must never use.

- **Sound:** "original sound" (the NFL's own; broadcast audio and music UNVERIFIED).

  - Caption: "#ad From [4]% to 100% in [19] minutes. Every number is the live Outcome price. #UCL". CTA: "Live match prices: link in bio".

- **Risk:** broadcast clips are the most claimed footage type (see section 6); the NFL posts this format with its own rights. Own screen footage of the app is safe. Thin in-match books can flicker: use goalcam 10-second prices where a game was filmed (project notes).
