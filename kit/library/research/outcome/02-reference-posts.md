Prepared 13 Oct 2026. Public pages only, nothing downloaded or opened as media. Counts were read on 12 and 13 Oct 2026 and will have moved since.

How to read the tables
- Views exist for TikTok only. Instagram does not show view counts to a logged-out reader, so those cells say n/a.
- "Face on camera?" is a call from the caption and from Instagram's automatic alt text for the cover frame. I did not watch any video. "Faceless (per brief)" means the brief files the format under faceless. "Unknown" means the text gave no clue, so someone should open the link.
- "Outcome mentioned?" covers the caption and visible page text only. Anything said or shown inside the video could not be checked.
- A "~" before a number means the page showed it without a label, so I matched likes and comments by position. Treat as approximate.
- Posted dates: TikTok dates come from TikTok's own timestamp (UTC). Instagram dates come from the post page, which shows US dates, so a post can be one day earlier than its UTC time. Two dates are decoded from the post ID and marked "(decoded)". The decoding matched the page date (allowing for the US versus UTC day) on all 30 Instagram posts that showed a date, and matched all 7 TikTok timestamps.
- Follower counts are the account's size on 13 Oct 2026, read from the Instagram embed header or the TikTok profile. Where I had to use a search result instead, it says so.

## 5. Patterns worth passing on

- Football pages share one caption recipe: an emotional hook line, a "[player or team] today" search line, then five hashtags that finish with the page's own tag or #football (guap.motion, mattyfc__, bennyclasher).
- Reaction posts go up on the day of the event. Benny's pre match and post match clips on 9 Sep were about 6 hours 40 minutes apart.
- Comment-keyword offers are common in finance and teaching reels: "REWARDS", "stocks", "invest", "UNIQUE", "AI", "Apple", "Pump", "Idea".
- Small accounts win here: jackpot_0_ (1,182 followers, 226,745 views), rynssport (284 followers, 9,524 likes) and shant.tradez (775 followers, 11,918 likes).
