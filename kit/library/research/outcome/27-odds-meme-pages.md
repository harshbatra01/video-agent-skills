# 27 Odds meme pages: how betting, crypto and prediction-market meme pages make a number instantly readable (research, 14 Oct 2026)

Job: find the meme and clip pages that already make odds, money calls and chances entertaining, show what is on screen and how the number is shown, and turn each proven format into an Outcome version with copy and a daily method. Context read first (not repeated here): `13-formats-finance-crypto.md` (news cards, explainers) and `22-video-formats-round2.md` (boards, races, then-vs-now board N4, partner videos of Sep and Oct 2026).

Read me in 7 lines:
1. Data: full recent X timelines of 32 accounts through the FxTwitter API (19,843 posts, 29 Aug to 13 Oct 2026, with views, likes, reposts, quotes and saves), 12-month top lists from Socialpruf (X and TikTok), single TikToks through tikwm, YouTube watch pages. 57 posts were downloaded (18 videos, 39 image posts) and turned into 25 frame sheets in `raw\27\sheets\`.
2. The finding in one line: **the number travels when it is the punchline or the story, never when it is the product.** Paddy Power's own price posts got 4 to 13 likes; its banter with a number as the punchline got 2,911 to 23,133.
3. The best device for Outcome is "the money said X%, then this happened": a longshot that landed (PolymarketSport "fishalive", $400k at 9%, 59,518 likes) or a favourite that fell (PolymarketSport "$1M on Spain at 92%", 85,710 likes and 10,739 quote posts after Spain failed to beat Cabo Verde).
4. Big brand accounts buy reach. A post with millions of views and under about 0.1% likes per view is marked "likely promoted (UNVERIFIED)" and ranked on likes, quotes, shares and saves instead.
5. On X the number sits in the first line of text and the picture is a face or a receipt. On TikTok and Reels the number is burned into the clip in a white caption box (ESPN BET, Zoofuh, SportsLine).
6. Limits: TikTok search and hashtags are blocked from this PC (Cloudflare), X search has no public route (Nitter stopped after X's legal letters, XCancel is suspended), Instagram was not measured, and the web-search budget ran out near the end. Gaps are marked UNVERIFIED.
7. Words: never "bet". Use "call", "the money says", "chance", "took Yes at 9¢", "priced at", "sold at". Also drop parlay, slip, acca, lock, cash out (for us), odds board, money glitch.

## 2. Format cards

Real Outcome numbers used in the copy below are mid prices read from the studio archive (`studio\data\archive\mids\20261013.jsonl`, about 22:45 UTC on 13 Oct 2026). They will have moved; every post must use the live price and a time stamp. Square brackets mean "fill from real data at post time", never invent. Football match markets on Outcome count 90 minutes plus stoppage time only.

### F1. Longshot that landed ("someone called it at 2%")

- **What it is:** a real person (named by the platform or anonymous) took a tiny chance and it happened, told with a receipt and the moment that decided it.
- **Verified examples:**
  1. PolymarketSport (180,428 followers), 15 Jun 2026: "Someone named 'fishalive' put $400k on Spain NOT to win vs Cabo Verde at 9% odds... This trade just cashed out $4,702,769.23 on Polymarket". 10,373,759 views, 59,518 likes, 3,496 reposts, 3,072 saves. https://x.com/PolymarketSport/status/2066588673039069640
  2. PolymarketSport, 11 Oct 2026: "Someone put $2,800 on Jacksonville to WIN their game vs Philadelphia at a 2% chance after the Eagles took the lead with 7 seconds left... The Jaguars ended up kicking a last second field goal". 1,323,021 views, 11,501 likes, 581 reposts, 647 saves (L/v 0.87%). https://x.com/PolymarketSport/status/2109337593179406520
  3. PolymarketSport, 8 Oct 2026: "A Polymarket user put $20 on UNDER 7.5 runs in Yankees-Rays at 0.1% odds after a home run made it 4-4... Officials then overturned the HR for fan interference". 41-second broadcast clip. 1,426,241 views, 10,166 likes. https://x.com/PolymarketSport/status/2108223688755786041
  4. The same Jaguars game told without a person: B/R Betting, 11 Oct 2026, "THE JAGUARS WERE 75-1 WITH 8 SECONDS LEFT. THEY WON THE FOOTBALL GAME." 470,712 views, 1,310 likes. https://x.com/br_betting/status/2109332051195503023 . The version with a person who called it got about 9 times the likes.
  5. Non-sports archetype: Pope Leo XIV was under 1% on Kalshi going into the May 2025 conclave (NBC) or 1% to 2% on Kalshi and Polymarket (CNN). News coverage, no post numbers.
- **On screen** (sheets `x_pm_kalshi_cards.jpg`, `x_big_odds_posts.jpg`, `x_pmsport_yankees_0p1_1.jpg`): two images side by side on X. Left: a hero photo (Trevor Lawrence pointing up). Right: a teal ticket card "PHI Eagles vs JAC Jaguars / JAC Jaguars / Cost $2,817.99 / Odds 2% / Profit +$122,426.01 (+4,344%) / Payout $125,244". The fishalive card is red: "You bought / Spain vs. Cabo Verde / Spain · No / Cost $427,952 / Odds 9% / To win $4,702,769.23". The Yankees post is a plain TNT broadcast clip of the catch and the umpire's call, the story is in the text.
- **How the number is shown:** a tiny % plus a small cost plus a huge payout, read top to bottom like an app receipt. The first line of the post repeats the % and the "7 seconds left".
- **The joke or tension:** "who does that?" and "it actually worked". The last-second moment makes it a story, not a brag.
- **Why it is shared:** saves and quotes. People send it as proof that anything can happen, or to tag the friend who always calls the underdog.
- **Outcome version:**
  - UCL tonight: "LASK were 9.6% to beat Liverpool before kick-off. Someone took Yes at 10¢ with $[50]. LASK won. $[50] became $[500]." (Only if it happens and the trade is real.)
  - In-game: "Roma were [3]% with 6 minutes left at 1-2 against Real Madrid. Someone took Yes anyway. Roma won 3-2." plus the goal clip.
  - Ballon d'Or (when it settles): "Yamal was [x]% on [date]. Someone took him at [x]¢. He won."
  - Bitcoin 15 min: "With 40 seconds left Bitcoin Up was [4]%. It finished Up."
- **Make it daily:** extend the studio archive with a trades log: poll Hyperliquid `recentTrades` for every live outcome coin each minute and keep price, size and time (public, anonymous). After settlement, pick the cheapest buy on the winning side with cost about $1,000 or less, pair it with the goalcam clip of the deciding minute, render the receipt card. Human check: confirm the trade and the result. UNVERIFIED: how far back `recentTrades` goes, so poll often.
- **Risk:** TikTok's rules say no winnings and nothing that looks like a slip (03-platform-rules.md, TikTok section), so keep F1 for X and Instagram, or make a TikTok cut with no dollar amounts (just "4% with 6 minutes left" and the goal). Client rule: realistic money (trade about $1,000 or less) and the real event shown. Never name a wallet or user without the client's permission. Write "Paid" not "Profit". No "bet".

## 4. Rules for making a number instantly readable (from the frames)

1. **One number per frame, attached to a face or a moment.** "92%" over McGregor; "28 points ... 28.5" over Wemby sitting down.
2. **Two numbers side by side make tension:** line and result (28 and 28.5), then and now ($635,000 and $96), cost and payout ($2,817.99 and $125,244), before and after (45% to [x]%).
3. **The receipt is a trusted container.** Cost, chance, paid. People read app UI at a glance; use Outcome's real position view, never a mock-up.
4. **Where the number lives depends on the platform.** X: first line of the text, the picture is emotion. TikTok, Reels and Shorts: burned in, a white rounded box with black sentence-case text, two lines at most, upper third or mid-frame, the clip's own score bug left visible.
5. **The number is the punchline, not the product.** "August 2026 - September 2026"; "1,140-point deduction".
6. **Brackets for tags:** "(39 pts)", "(+1250)", "(2%)".
7. **Absurd sizes win:** 0.1%, 2%, 9%, 92%, "A TRILLION DOLLARS" (B/R Betting, 7,528 likes, 14 Sep 2026, https://x.com/br_betting/status/2099307715004665866 ).
8. **Know what each format earns:** label memes earn shares (PrizePicks 37,220 shares), receipts earn saves (B/R Kamara 1,447 saves on 14,334 likes, https://x.com/br_betting/status/2107293477927035285 ), favourite-on-a-loser posts earn quotes ($1M Spain 10,739 quotes), polls earn replies.

## 8. Method, limits and UNVERIFIED items

**Method.** X: FxTwitter public API (`api.fxtwitter.com/2/profile/<handle>/statuses` with cursor paging, about 800 posts back per account; `/status/<id>` for one post with views, likes, reposts, replies, quotes, saves and media), scripts in `raw\27\tools\fx.py` (tl, top, st, get), `pct.py`, `grepall.py`; data in `raw\27\data\tl_<handle>.jsonl`. 12-month top posts: Socialpruf public pages (`sp.py`), each post re-checked through FxTwitter or tikwm. TikTok: tikwm single-video API (stats and media); Socialpruf for account top lists. YouTube: watch pages (`ys.py`, throttled after a rate limit), yt-dlp for downloads. Frames: ffmpeg from `render.py`'s FFMPEG, contact sheets with timestamps by `media.py`. Outcome prices: `studio\data\archive` (meta and mids, 13 Oct 2026 about 22:45 UTC). All reads on 13 Oct 2026 UTC (14 Oct in India).

**Limits.** TikTok search, hashtag, user and discover endpoints are behind Cloudflare or blocked (tikwm 403, tiktok.com times out, r.jina.ai blocked TikTok for part of the session); verified TikTok accounts return no post list.
