## 1. Key rules (short, with numbers)

### A. Carousels and TikTok photo mode
1. **TikTok: expect video to beat photo mode on reach.** Median engagement: video 3.39% vs carousels and photos 1.92% (Buffer, 19 Mar 2026) [8]. Jan to Feb 2026: video got 5.6x the views and 7.8x the interactions of image or carousel posts (Metricool, 2.31M posts) [9]. Vendor claims that photo posts win (20 to 40% more saves, 2 to 5x reach) show no method: UNVERIFIED [19]. Plan: carousels = volume and saves format, short video = reach format, run both from week 1.
2. **Instagram: carousels lead.** Median engagement 6.9% of reach vs Reels 3.3% (Buffer) [8]. Carousels earn about 9x the saves of single images (Metricool, 24.4M posts, 16 Jun 2026) [10]. Reels still reach about 1.36x more than carousels (Buffer, data to Oct 2024) [8].
3. **Make slide 2 a second cover.** Instagram often re-serves a carousel that someone scrolled past and starts it on slide 2 (Mosseri, 17 Oct 2024) [11]. Slide 2 needs its own hook and the key number, not "continued". No confirmed TikTok equivalent.
4. **Slide count: 5 on TikTok, 5 to 8 on Instagram, up to 10 for rankings.** Evidence is weak: TikTok 4 to 7 best, over 10 loses completion (calculator site, no sample) [19]. Instagram 8 to 10 with a dip near slide 3 (Socialinsider via Moneris, secondhand) [33]. Limits: TikTok 35 images, Instagram app 20 [11][24]. The Instagram API may cap at 10 (UNVERIFIED) [25].
5. **Slide 1 = one claim, one number, one name.** Hook types vendors list (stat or proof, numbered promise, controversial take, curiosity gap, before and after) are ANECDOTAL [34]. No finance-only or sports-only hook test exists. Outcome version: "[Team or asset] [N]% chance, [clock]" [I].
6. **Text on a 1080 px wide slide: body 48 px or more, headline 90 px or more, key number 200 px or more, one fact per slide, white on a dark plate** [I]. Maths: on a 360 dp wide phone 1 dp = 3 px, so 48 px = 16 dp, normal app body size. Keep text out of the bottom 25%, right 15% and top 10% [I]; check the in-app preview before a batch render.
7. **Keyword in real slide text and in the first line of the caption.** TikTok reads captions, sounds and hashtags [O08]. A vendor says on-slide keywords rank better in TikTok search than audio-only mentions: ANECDOTAL [19].
8. **Music on photo mode: no test data exists** (searched). Instagram carousels with music can appear in the Reels tab [11]. TikTok business accounts see only the Commercial Music Library in the app, and the general library is licensed for personal, non-commercial use, so a promo or per-view page should use the Commercial Music Library or licensed tracks (secondhand) [30]. Test with and without sound.
9. **Captions:** TikTok photo posts allow up to 4,000 characters (secondhand) [24]. No study on best length found. Hook plus keyword first, extra keywords after [I].
10. **Cadence: start at 1 to 3 posts a day per page** [I]. Vs once a week: 2 to 5 a week gave +17% views per post, 6 to 10 gave +29%, 11+ gave +34%, but the median stayed about 500 views; the gain is in the top tail (Buffer, 11.4M posts, 8 Oct 2025) [12]. Metricool: volume up about 80% year on year while views per post fell 31% [9]. More posts = more tickets, each smaller.
11. **Times:** Buffer (7.1M posts, 14 Sep 2026): top slots Sun 9am, Mon 1pm, Sun 1pm; evenings 6 to 11pm carry the most engagement; no time zone stated, no niche split [13]. Use as test windows. Target countries are not fixed yet [O00].

### B. Faceless short videos
12. **No head-to-head test of text plus music vs AI voiceover vs captions over footage in finance or sports exists** (searched). Do not claim a winner. Run the test in section 7.
13. **Sound is on for most viewing.** TikTok-sourced figures: 88% of viewing sound on, 93% of top videos use audio (secondhand, older) [22]. Reels audio on for over 80% of plays [O10]. Silent text video is the weak option. Text plus music is the floor; voice is optional.
14. **First 2 seconds:** clear text plus motion gave up to a 50% lift in 3-second retention vs a static open (vendor summary of Motion data, ANECDOTAL) [32]. Average hook rate for video ads 25 to 30%, top 40 to 50% [32]. Fits [O10]: headline on frame 1, something moves in the first second.
15. **Length: 20 to 30 s for one market or one trade, 45 to 60 s for an explainer; test both** [I]. Completion falls as length rises (Fanpage Karma) [20]. The 89%, 73%, 55%, 38% completion table for 7, 15, 30, 60 s is an estimate, not measured data [19]. Reach falls past 90 s [20]. The Outcome guide asks for about 1 minute with the plug at 50 to 70% [O00], which is 30 to 42 s into a 60 s video. Completion falls with length, so read your own Retention chart at the plug and move it earlier if the drop is steep [I].
16. **Loops:** since 31 Mar 2025 YouTube counts a Short view on start or replay with no minimum watch time; the old rule is now "engaged views" (secondhand) [17]. A seamless loop can push average percentage viewed above 100% (vidIQ, one case from 2021, ANECDOTAL) [18]. No official word for TikTok or Reels [O10]. Keep under 30 s, last frame = first frame.
17. **Number animation: no published test.** Test a count-up on the chance number vs a static number, 10 pairs [I].
18. **AI voice is not penalised by any rule I could read. Labels are about realistic people, scenes and sounds.** TikTok (2026H2 text, effective 24 Sep 2026) wants a label on AI content that shows realistic-looking scenes or people and does not mention voices [3]. Meta wants its disclosure tool on organic posts with realistic AI video or audio and may penalise skipping it [7]. YouTube needs no disclosure for a synthetic voiceover of your own script, but does for realistic AI of real people and for AI music [6]. Rule: generic narrator voice, no pretence of being a human expert, no cloned real person.
19. **YouTube will not monetise AI personas giving finance advice** (example: AI hosts giving investment tips) [5]. Monetisation only.
20. **TikTok viewers can turn AI content down** in Manage Topics; TikTok said it was still testing on 10 Mar 2026 [23]. Effect on AI-voice pages: no data.

### C. Originality and reuse
21. **Instagram, 30 Apr 2026 (PRIMARY):** the rule now covers photos and carousels. It is judged per account ("primarily" is not defined). Ineligible accounts lose recommendations; followers still see posts. Recovery = most recent posts original over a rolling 30 days. 75% of US recommendations now come from original posts [1].
22. **Counts as original:** content you designed, on-image text that adds context, graphics that give new information, remix, third-party material materially edited [1]. **Does not count:** borders, watermarks, subtitles, credit lines [1], speed changes, a screenshot of someone's post with their username [2]. The page has no template wording.
23. **TikTok 2026H2 guidelines (PRIMARY, effective 24 Sep 2026):** content is ineligible for the FYF "if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new" [3]. The For You feed drives about 7 in 10 views [9], so losing it = losing most reach.
24. **TikTok accounts:** several accounts are allowed, not to deceive. Deceptive behaviour can mean a ban on the account and on new ones, and limits on posting, search and FYF. It bans automation "designed to bypass our systems" [3]. Vendor-listed slideshow flags (ANECDOTAL): reposted images, other apps' watermarks, near-identical slideshows on several accounts, high-volume low-variation AI slides, identical caption structures, mechanical posting schedules [14].
25. **YouTube (PRIMARY):** a channel can lose monetisation for content that "looks like it's made with a template" or feels repetitive over several videos. Listed examples: image slideshows or scrolling text with minimal commentary, AI content from generic templates that looks mass produced, readings of text from websites or news feeds [5]. Partner Program only. Press says a July 2026 clarification added AI personas and manipulative clips (UNVERIFIED, no primary) [15].
26. **The test that passes on all three: could a viewer get this post by reading the source?** Each post needs one thing the source lacks: a time-stamped number, a change since yesterday, a comparison, a named reason, our take. Same layout is fine. Same facts are not.
27. **Template safeguards:** rotate visual templates every 10 to 15 posts, vary hook wording and caption structure (vendor, ANECDOTAL) [14]. Never post the same file on two pages [OPB]. No other app's watermark, no CapCut trial marks. Keep working files for appeals.

### D. Growth from zero
28. **No audited case study of a faceless finance, sports or prediction-market page from 2025 to 2026 found.** What follows is platform-level data or ANECDOTAL.
29. **Expect a slow start.** Median TikTok post about 500 views at any cadence; 90th percentile 3.7K to 14.4K [12]. 44% of TikTok accounts under 100K followers grew between 2025 and 2026 [9].
30. One first-person faceless Instagram page (not finance): 1,200 followers at day 30, no viral reel, about 18,700 at day 90, breakouts of 25K and 50K views came later. Self-reported, ANECDOTAL [28].
31. Sales-page claims (0 to 10K in 2 weeks, 100K in a week, 80,000+ from three viral reels) sell courses: UNVERIFIED [28].
32. **Judge a format after 30 to 50 posts, not 5** [I]. A TikTok creator says the algorithm decides around video 10 to 15 (ANECDOTAL).
33. **Series ("Day N of...", weekly trackers): no follow-conversion data found for finance or sports.** Treat as a test. Series that already fit our data: daily "Top 3 traders in the last 24 hours" [O00], weekly "biggest P&L of the week", "market of the day: chance now vs yesterday" [I]. A running-balance "Day N" tracker must show real, realistic money [O00] and stays inside 03's limits.

### 2.3 Hooks for slide 1 and slide 2
- Hook types vendors list (ANECDOTAL, no finance or sports test): a stat or proof number, a numbered promise ("7 ways"), a controversial take, a curiosity gap, a before and after preview [34]. A viewer decides in 1 to 2 seconds [34].
- Outcome versions [I]: live chance plus clock ("Spain 41% to win, kick-off in 3 h"), a big P&L number plus the one trade behind it, "Top 3 traders today", a mismatch ("market says 12%, our trader paid $0.20 and won").
- Because of the slide 2 re-serve [11], slide 2 repeats the number in a different wording and adds the payoff. It should make sense with no slide 1.
- Make the last slide worth sending: sends per reach is the top signal for non-followers [O08]. A "today's top 3 chances" list is sendable [I].
- FinanceTok audience: a vendor trend piece says viewers aged 35 to 45 are over-represented and practical breakdowns replaced finfluencer dunking (May 2026, ANECDOTAL) [27].

### 2.4 Text, layout and files
- Readable size maths: 1080 px canvas, 360 dp phone, 1 dp = 3 px. 48 px = 16 dp (body), 90 px = 30 dp (headline), 200 px = 67 dp (number) [I].
- Reading time over video: 15 characters a second [O10]. For a slide held 2 to 3 s, 30 to 45 characters of body text is the limit [I].
- One font family, 2 weights, one accent colour for numbers (matches the PLAYBOOK rule of coloured numbers [OPB]). Do not cover the graph, lines, names or the Outcome logo [O00].
- Export JPEG at high quality for TikTok photo posts, 1080 x 1920 [I from 24]. For Instagram carousels use one ratio for every slide.
- Photo post fields in TikTok's API: a 90 character title and a 4,000 character description (Adaptly's reading of the docs, secondhand, verify in the developer docs) [24]. Video caption limit 2,200.

### 2.5 Music and sound
- No test of music vs none, or trending vs library, on photo mode was found in 2026. A vendor says trending audio gives a second discovery surface through the audio page: UNVERIFIED [19].
- Licensing is the real constraint for a promo page: TikTok business accounts are locked to the Commercial Music Library in the app, and general-library tracks are licensed for personal non-commercial use (SMT 2020 and Soundstripe 2026 blog, secondhand) [30]. Per-view or paid Outcome posts are commercial, see 03 [O03].
- Plan: use Commercial Music Library or licensed tracks (our Epidemic Sound set-up, check that its licence covers paid or per-view promo posts) [I].

## 3. Faceless short videos: detail

### 3.1 Format choice
| Format | Cost to make | Risk | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text on screen plus music | Lowest, fully templated | Silent-looking if the music is weak; needs a hook in text | No head-to-head data |
| AI voiceover plus captions over graphics or stock | Medium (voice credits) | Labels only if realistic people or cloned voices; "mass-produced" look on YouTube | Vendor claims only |
| Captions over gameplay or loop footage | Low | Off-brand for finance and sports; no 2026 data found | None |
Vendor claims: captions add 15 to 30% views and animated captions double retention (ClipSpeed, no method) [21]. A caption-heavy "text beats voiceover" blog has no data in the excerpt [21]. CapCut's guide says text hurts when it repeats the voiceover and captions, so give each element one job [21]. All ANECDOTAL.

### 3.2 Sound
- TikTok sound-on figures (88% of viewing, 93% of top videos use audio) come from secondhand pages about TikTok's own material; I could not open TikTok's report [22]. Treat as UNVERIFIED but consistent with Meta advice and [O10].
- The Outcome guide names real music for the format [O00], so music is part of the brief.

### 3.3 Length
- Fanpage Karma: 0 to 10 s has the highest completion, completion falls steadily with length, engagement comes from medium lengths, reach drops past 90 s [20].
- Calculator site table (7 s 89%, 15 s 73%, 30 s 55%, 60 s 38%, 10 min 8%) says in its own text it is an estimate, not measured [19]. UNVERIFIED.
- One 2026 vendor analysis of 2.19M clips (page not pinned down in my results): 30 to 60 s is the most common length, 38.5% of content. A trend blog claims 1 to 3 minute videos now beat 15 s clips on watch time (ANECDOTAL) [27].
- Earlier file: brand Reels reach rate 5.2% under 30 s vs 5.6% at 30 to 60 s [O10]. Length is a test, not a rule.
- TikTok Creator Rewards needs videos over 1 minute (sources disagree on details). It is a payout scheme and does not apply to promo pages [19].

### 3.4 Loops
- YouTube, 31 Mar 2025: a Short view counts when it starts or replays, no minimum watch time. Engaged views is the old metric and still drives revenue share and Partner Program checks [17].
- Average percentage viewed can pass 100% with replays; vidIQ cites an animator going from about 100% to about 200% after looping (2021) [18]. A 2026 vendor case (115% vs 85% retention, 4x impressions) is one client test: UNVERIFIED [18].
- No TikTok or Reels statement on replays [O10].
- Method [I]: write the last line so it leads into the first line, end on the same background as frame 0, check the Retention chart for a cliff at the loop point.

### 3.5 Number animation
- No published retention test of count-ups or animated numbers.
- Nearest evidence is generic motion plus clear text in the first 2 seconds (up to 50% lift in 3-second retention, vendor, ANECDOTAL) [32].
- Organic tests are noisy because there is no stable traffic split [32]. Use pairs and repeat.
- Cheap to build in HTML, CSS or Remotion; the chance number is the natural thing to animate [I].

### 3.6 AI voices: labels and suppression
| Platform | Label needed for | Not needed or not addressed | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | AI-generated or significantly edited content showing realistic-looking scenes or people. Unlabelled content may be removed, restricted or labelled. ## 4. Originality and reuse: detail

### 4.1 Instagram (PRIMARY: [1], [2])
- Date: 30 Apr 2026. Reels rules from 2024 now cover photos and carousels.
- Targets accounts that "primarily" post unoriginal photos or carousels; no number given.
- Effect: not recommended in feed, Discover and other surfaces. Followers still see the posts.
- Original = work you wholly created or that reflects your own view. Examples named: photos or videos you took, content you designed (a photo series or how-to guide), third-party content you materially edited.
- Qualifying edits: unique on-image text with context, creative graphics with new information, remix. Meme templates and popular clips can be original when you add something.
- Test in the page's own terms: new information, context or point of view, not just restating.
- Not enough: borders, watermarks, subtitles, a caption credit [1]; speed change, or a screenshot of another post with the username showing [2].
- Recovery: most recent photos, carousels and Reels original over a rolling 30 days. Check Account Status; remove content or appeal [1]. Menu path: Settings, Account, Account Status [O03].
- Stat: 75% of US recommendations now come from original posts [1].
- Related older rule: Recommendation Guidelines exclude "unoriginal content mostly repurposed with minor edits" [O03].

### 4.2 TikTok (PRIMARY: [3])
- Guidelines released 25 Aug 2026, effective 24 Sep 2026.
- "You should only post content you created or have the right to share."
- Unoriginal or reused material "without anything new" = not eligible for the FYF. Posts stay up, with no FYF reach.
- Deceptive behaviour: ban the account, ban new accounts, restrict posting, search and FYF. Multiple accounts are allowed "for example, for fan content or creative expression", not to deceive.
- "We strictly prohibit automation tools, scripts, or other tricks designed to bypass our systems." I read this as targeting evasion, not official-API scheduling [I].
- I could not read the FYF standards page (blank through the reader) or the Help Centre label page (404), so I cannot confirm TikTok's separate "low quality" wording [O03 has the 2025H2 archive].
- Vendor slideshow guide (OpenClip, ANECDOTAL) [14]: flags come from reposted slides and screenshots, other apps' watermarks, the same slideshow on several accounts, mass AI slides with templated captions, and identical file hashes, caption structure and posting schedules across related accounts. It also says check post analytics for a "not eligible for recommendation" marker, rotate templates every 10 to 15 posts, and expect 2 to 3 weeks of original posts to recover.

### 4.3 YouTube (PRIMARY: [5])
- The policy is "inauthentic content" (renamed from "repetitious content", 15 Jul 2025) [5][16]. The page section reads "Generic or Repetitive Content".
- Definition: looks like it is made with a template, or feels repetitive after several videos in a row from the same channel.
- Not monetisable examples: "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative, commentary, or educational value"; similar content with minimal variation; AI content from generic templates that gives the impression of mass production.
- Allowed: same intro and outro if the bulk differs; AI used for scripts or a unique background visual; tools and templates as long as the result shows creative vision.
- Reused content: not monetisable if it is only "readings of other materials you did not originally create, like text from websites or news feeds". Allowed example in the sports space: "replays of a sports tournament where you explain the moves".
- Reviewers look at the channel's main theme, most-viewed and newest videos, and biggest watch time share. If they cannot tell you made it, monetisation can be removed for the whole channel.
- This is a Partner Program rule. A promo channel with no ads is not hit by it directly. The same patterns are what a reviewer would judge if the channel ever applies [I].
- July 2026 (secondhand, UNVERIFIED primary): reports dated 16 to 21 Jul 2026 say YouTube's trust and safety lead, Matt Halprin, described three categories: generic or repetitive, distressing or manipulative, and synthetic AI personas on sensitive topics [15]. Outlier Kit's own dates do not line up with its sources.

### 4.4 What makes a templated post original: the shared test
| Add this | Why it passes | Outcome example [I] |
|---|---|---|
| A number the source does not print | "Graphics that offer new information" [1] | Live chance with a time stamp; change since yesterday |
| Our own data cut | "Content you designed" [1] | Top 3 traders from the leaderboard JSON |
| A reason or take | "Context or a point of view" [1] | One sentence on why the chance moved |
| A comparison | New information | Today's chance vs last week's |
| Real commentary on a sports clip | YouTube's allowed example [5] | Stats card with our read, no broadcast clip |

Weak spots in the four client formats [O00] and fixes [I]:
| Format | Original part | Weak spot | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| How-to carousel | Own product screenshots, our market choice and payout maths | Same five slides on many pages | Different market per post, new hook wording, rotate layout every 10 to 15 posts |
| P&L card edit | The trader's real P&L card, date, our line on why it matters | Film-scene background is a third-party clip | Put the data layer first and large; use licensed or generic background |
| Top 3 traders | Daily leaderboard numbers | Movie still on slide 1 is third-party | A designed card with the number |
| Trader story | Real trades, match stats, our timeline | Match photos or footage rights | Stats cards and our commentary |
Rights and gambling or finance restrictions on TikTok and Meta are separate from originality, see 03. A perfectly original post can still be age-restricted or kept off the FYF if the platform counts it as gambling promotion [O03].

### 5.2 Case studies: what I found and how much to trust it
- **Medium, "I ran a faceless Instagram page for 90 days"** (search excerpt only, page blocked): general niche, not finance. Most reels under 200 views, some under 50. Day 30: about 1,200 followers, no viral reel. Day 90: about 18,700. Breakouts of 25,000 and 50,000 views came later; the author credits a hook line, not volume. Self-reported: ANECDOTAL [28].
- **Course and newsletter stories:** a member from 50 to almost 600 followers in 5 days, one reel to 100K people; "100K in a week"; "80,000+ with three viral Reels"; a fitness page said to reach 10K, then 46K, then 100K within about 2 to 3 months. All come from sales pages: UNVERIFIED [28].
- **Operators running 3, 5 or 10 accounts** (a vendor blog in my search results, page not pinned down, no names): UNVERIFIED. TikTok's account rules (4.2) make that a risk, see 03 [O03].
- **Monetised-faceless income tables** (finance CPM $15 to 20, "38% of new Instagram monetisation ventures are faceless") cite no sources: ignore [35].
- **Sports:** no faceless sports page case study with numbers found. Submagic's pricing page advertises a Sportskeeda case study ("2,500+ monthly videos"), a vendor claim about a media company, not a new page [26].
- **Prediction markets:** Kalshi is hiring a partnerships manager for social (X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitch) and a video editor for UGC TikTok, Reels and Shorts [29]. ### 5.3 Series formats
- No source measured follow-through for "Day N of..." or weekly trackers in finance or sports.
- Mechanism argument [I]: a repeated frame and a repeated promise give a reason to follow and a reason to open the next post. Instagram's signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach [O08], so a series helps only if each part also stands alone.
- Series built on data we already have [I]:
  - Daily: "Top 3 traders in the last 24 hours" (already a client reference format [O00]).
  - Daily: "Market of the day: chance now vs yesterday".
  - Weekly: "Biggest P&L of the week", "Biggest mover".
  - Match-day: "UCL tonight: where the chance sits".
- Constraints: money shown must be realistic and real [O00]; no profit guarantees; gambling and finance rules in 03.

### 5.4 Planning numbers for a new page [I]
- Weeks 1 to 4: 1 to 3 posts a day, mix of video and carousel, all with a unique data point. Expect most posts under 1,000 views.
- Judge formats on the 90th percentile and sends, not on the median.
- First outlier can take 7 or more weeks in the one documented case [28].
- Keep 5 to 10 posts of each format, then drop the bottom third.

## 7. Test plan (cheap, paired)
Organic tests are noisy. Run pairs on the same day, repeat 8 to 10 times, and judge on the 90th percentile, sends and 3-second hold, not on one post.

| Test | A | B | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slide 2 hook | Slide 2 repeats the number and hook | Slide 2 continues the story | Instagram reach and sends per reach |
| Format on TikTok | 5 slide photo post | 25 s video of the same story | Views at 48 h, sends, saves |
| Slide count | 5 slides | 8 slides | Last-slide reach, saves |
| Music | Commercial Music Library track | No sound | Views at 48 h |
| Number animation | Count-up on the chance | Static number | 3-second hold |
| Voice | Text plus music | AI voice plus captions | Average watch time, sends |
| Length | 25 s | 50 s | Watch time %, plug-point drop |
| Cadence | 1 a day | 3 a day (two pages) | Median and 90th percentile after 4 weeks |
| Series | Daily "Top 3" as a series | Same content as standalone posts | Follows per profile visit |
