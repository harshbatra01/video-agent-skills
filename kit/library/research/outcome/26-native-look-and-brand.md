# 26. Native look and Outcome brand (14 Oct 2026)

Why this file exists: the user said our first Outcome posts look like corporate data graphics and motion templates, not like native TikTok and Reels, and nobody would watch them. This file answers three things: (1) what native viral short-form looks like in Sep and Oct 2026 on no-creator pages, (2) what Outcome's own brand is, (3) a native look system for an Outcome page that carries the brand without looking like an ad.

How it was made: our 22 finals were checked frame by frame and measured; 148 TikTok posts from 96 pages and 19 YouTube Shorts were collected with exact numbers and stills; three helper agents covered written sources, Outcome's own voice and posts, and TikTok discovery. Everything not confirmed is marked UNVERIFIED. No numbers are invented: every view, follower and percentage comes from a named post, page or file.

---

## 0. Short version

**The problem in one line:** our posts are designed screens (cards, pills, charts, skins) with about 37 words on them at any moment; native posts are a real moment with one short line on it.

**The 10 look rules that matter most**
1. **Start from a real moment.** The whole frame is real: our own screen recording of the Outcome app, a phone pointed at a screen, our own filmed footage, a real screenshot, or licensed footage. No designed backgrounds, cards or skins.
2. **Use TikTok's own text and nothing else.** TikTok Sans (open font, it is TikTok's in-app "Classic" text): a white box with black text for the hook, or a 1 to 3 word white caption with a black outline for speech. Two text sizes at most.
3. **One text block at a time, 4 to 9 words, nothing tiny.** Native no-face posts: median 4 readable words on screen and 0 tiny words. Ours: 10.5 readable plus 27 tiny. Delete the handle, pills, counters, footnotes and legends.
4. **Text sits in the middle of the screen, inside the safe box.** Native text centre: 50% of the screen height. Ours: 26%. Keep text between x 120 and 918 and y 270 and 1250; keep cover text inside the central 1080x1350 band (Instagram crops the grid to 3:4).
5. **The hook is a plain statement about a real moment with one true number, on frame 0.** 4 to 12 words, no greeting, often "POV:". Keep the words "odds" and the venue name out of the hook (odds-titled countdowns did 0.22x in round 2).
6. **Show the chance the way traders see it.** The real Outcome app screen (recording, crop, or filmed on a phone) or one number in the text, on a brand-green chip. Never a designed % card, gauge, bar, race or weather icon.
7. **Brand inside the content, never on top of it.** No added logo or watermark. Outcome shows up through its own UI (header, chart watermark, YES / NO buttons), one mention at 50% to 70% of the video, and the line "Trade the outcome.". Brand on screen at most 25% of the runtime unless the app moment is the story.
8. **Movement must be real.** Natives either hold one real shot (20 of 49 measured no-face posts have no cut at all) or cut hard and fast (17 of 49 cut 3+ times per 10 s). Motion comes from the footage or the live price line, not from animated layouts. No slide-ins, wipes, counting numbers.
9. **Sound like a page owner.** 130 of 148 TikTok references run on "original sound" (105 under the page's own name): audio baked into the upload. Bake natural sound, a voice, or a licensed track; drop the whoosh / tick / ding packs; on a business account only Commercial Music Library tracks are usable in the app.
10. **Real and original, always.** Own recordings, own edits, own text. No other apps' watermarks, no reposted clips without changes (TikTok and Instagram both cut reach for that), no fake trades, wins or AI people.

**Brand colours (hex)**
- App UI (what viewers see in screen recordings): background #0B0C0F, text #F5F8FF, YES / UP button #99FFBC, NO / DOWN button #FF3D5E, live #FF0051, rewards gold #FFD699; logo mark cyan #00FFFF over magenta #FF00F2 (app icon only, used in 0 of 57 social images).
- Social / marketing palette (official brand guide, brand.outcome.xyz): Signal Green #1AFF57 (hero), Deep Forest #055D1C, Violet Ultra #8952EE, Twilight Indigo #240F4C, Near-black #171717, Off-white #F5F5F5, High-Vis Yellow #E6FF2F, Rally Orange #FF8825, Protest Pink #FF9DBE, Executive Teal #04AC7D, Protocol Blue #03379D, Alert Red #FD6C6B.
- For our page: number chips in Signal Green #1AFF57 (up / favourite / YES) and Alert Red #FD6C6B (down / underdog / NO), black text on both.

**Biggest finds:** (a) kalshi.sports, Kalshi's sports page, gets 494K to 934K organic views per post on 7,516 followers with real footage and one white TikTok box; across its 6 posts in the set the Kalshi app appears once, as a short insert at the payoff, and no post shows a logo or a number on the cover; (b) TikTok Sans is open source and is TikTok's own text font, so our burned-in text can be the real native font; (c) Outcome has a public brand guide (voice, banned words, palette) that we did not have before.

### 2.3 What fills the frame
- A real moment, in almost every post: match or practice footage, fan phone footage, a press conference, a podcast set, a sitcom or film clip, a phone filming a monitor, a real app screen. Designed graphics as the whole frame are rare and are always a known format: a bar chart race on black (hai02a 19.0M), a "what if you invested" line race on white (investment.timeline 747K on 1K followers), a crest list for fixture predictions (footballmaster3333, FACE). None looks like a product dashboard.
- Non-vertical footage is normal: 23 of the 71 measured TikTok videos are not 9:16, mostly square edits and clips. The black space above and below becomes the place for the hook.
- Split screens are the clip-page standard: a reaction or second speaker on top, the clip below, the hook box on the seam (mookie.clips 4.8M, overlapdaily 1.5M).
- AI is everywhere in football and money right now: AI player parodies (YaCombo 569K, Football Memes 132K on 252 subscribers, purejokes0 865K), a "millionaire trader" who looks AI-made (wallstreet.club 9.4M; our read, UNVERIFIED), plus cartoon animations (Football Diaries 1.45M, Sports Time 456K). It is a reach shortcut we will not use: fake people and events break the client's honesty rules and TikTok's AI-label rules.

### 2.4 Text: the five styles that recur
1. **TikTok "Classic" box:** black text on a white rounded box, sentence case, one or two lines, often one emoji at the end. With style 3, the most common text device in the set: kalshi.sports (5 of its 6 posts), NFL on FOX 929K, mookie.clips 4.8M, validclps 3.3M, viralrushhtv 2.0M. It usually stays on screen for the whole video.
2. **Plain white text with a thin outline or shadow,** small, centre of the frame: edits and clip pages ("Pure chaos" on UEFA's own 183K clip; jovahn 1.7M; rugengine.sol 435K; ap_butter1 488K).
3. **Word-by-word captions (the CapCut look):** 1 to 3 words, white bold caps with a black outline, sometimes one word in yellow or green (FieldFlix 393K, respawnedreality 4.7M, overlapdaily 1.5M).
4. **Title band:** a black or white band above letterboxed footage with one sentence and coloured keywords (wallstreet.club 9.4M, yeglazer07 301K on 2K followers, data.dragonn 391K).
5. **Edit text:** big stylised words at the drop (kylianftblbannedat185k 7.3M, filimadethat "BALLON D'OR WINNER?"). Edit pages only.
Never seen on a clip, meme, edit or fan-moment page: an avatar-and-handle header, category pills, slide counters, footnotes, legends, progress bars, more than two text styles in a frame. (Edit pages do stamp a small faint name watermark; media-style pages such as novig, dtfootylens and GoalBrief put a small corner logo on designed slides; clip, meme and fan-moment pages, kalshi.sports included, show no logo at all.)
- **Font:** TikTok Sans is TikTok's own text font. TikTok's developer blog says it is "available in the TikTok app as Classic, Bold, Oblique" and other options, and released it under the SIL Open Font License (GitHub repo created 25 Mar 2025). Older guides name Proxima Nova Semibold as the old Classic. Among caption apps, Montserrat leads (61% of 2M Submagic exports, Apr 2025 data).

### 2.5 Words, position and time on screen (measured)
- Words: native no-face posts show a median of 4 readable words at once (edits and clips 2 to 3; box-hook memes 7 to 9) and 0 tiny words. Even their busiest frame carries a median of 8 readable words (ours: 21).
- Position: text centres sit around the middle of the screen (median 50% from the top); box hooks sit between about 20% and 65%; nothing important sits in the bottom 20% or under the right-side buttons. Written guidance agrees: Meta keeps 14% top, 35% bottom and 6% sides free on Reels ads; the strictest box that fits TikTok, Reels and Shorts is x 120 to 918, y 270 to 1248 (helper report, section 3).
- Time: a hook box stays for the whole clip in the box-style posts (kalshi.sports, NFL on FOX, habibx03, yeglazer07). Spoken captions change between almost every pair of stills taken 0.5 s apart. Text appears at frame 0 in 32 of 49 no-face posts; OCR found some text in every measured post (small watermarks count, so a few edits that look text-free still register).
- Size: native caption lines measure about 2% to 4% of the frame height; ours are in the same range. Size is not our problem; count and clutter are.

### 2.6 Hooks
- A plain statement about a moment, often with a number, 4 to 12 words, no greeting: "They were handing out Meatball subs before Patriots vs Bills 💀"; "This angle of Evans to Diggs 'who are you' moment"; "If a Millionaire trader had only 1000$"; "POV: You invested $1000 in NVIDIA instead of buying V-Bucks"; "Bro gave me 3 warnings and i still fell for it 😭😭🙏🙏".
- POV and "When your ..." frames run the money and betting niches: "POV: Trump Tweeted During Your Trade" (kojiro_0o 647K on 621 followers), "When Your Parlay Keep Losing By 1" (ap_butter1 488K on 4K), "Pov : Your bro is trading with emotions" (billsricci 4.3M).
- Questions are fewer and usually sit in the post caption ("What are your predictions?"). Vendors say statements beat questions; no study found (UNVERIFIED).
- TikTok's own guidance: state the proposition by 3 s and land the hook by 6 s. Instagram's 2026 Reels guide: first-frame text matters about as much as the cover (helper report [1][28]).
- Round 2 still stands: odds-titled countdowns did 0.22x while "leaked" did 1.6x. The number goes in; the word "odds" and the venue name stay out.
- "Who wins" is the biggest football hook of the window, and much of it is fake: "Ballondor winner 2026 kylian mbappe" (py10_1, 14.8M views on 21K followers, 3 Sep), "Kylian Mbappé vient de remporter le Ballon d'Or 2026 !" (nalnetsport 1.1M, 1 Oct) and "BREAKING: Lamine Yamal has been told he's the 2026 Ballon d'Or winner" (GoalBrief 217K, 4 Oct) all went out weeks before the 26 Oct ceremony. Outcome's honest version of that hook is the market's chance with a date ("one name is at 62%"), never a fake result.

### 2.7 How numbers and odds appear

### 2.8 Covers
- The cover is simply the strongest frame of the video: a face, a fan, a player mid-action, or the app at the key moment, with the same hook text that is on frame 0 (sheet 35: every TikTok cover). Edit pages often use a text-free close-up; box-hook pages show the box.
- No pills, counters or slide numbers on any cover. Logos appear only on media-style designed slides (novig, dtfootylens) and as small name stamps on some edit covers ("SLAYYZ.", "Jovahn"); one clip shows an "OpusClip" watermark. Several covers start on black (fade-in), a weak habit to avoid.
- Instagram crops the profile grid to 3:4 (1080x1440 out of 1080x1920) since Jan 2025; TikTok's grid crop is disputed (3:4 or square, UNVERIFIED). Safe band for cover text: the central 1080x1350 (y 285 to 1635).

### 2.9 Pace, transitions, zooms
- Two native rhythms: (a) one continuous real shot with a box on top (kalshi.sports, iduate, the memes): no cuts, the footage itself moves; (b) fast edits and clip compilations: 3 to 19 cuts per 10 s (kylianftblbannedat185k 19.3, filimadethat 11.1). Our videos sit between the two with designed frames that change every few seconds: the worst of both.
- What the stills show: hard cuts, flashes and speed-ramp blur on edit pages; punch-ins on screen recordings; red hand-drawn circles and arrows; emoji stickers. Template slide-ins, wipes and eased counters did not show up in any still, but the videos were not watched, so motion styles are UNVERIFIED.

### 2.10 Sound
- 130 of 148 TikTok references run on "original sound", 105 of them under the page's own name (sound baked into the upload: clip audio, a remix, a voice). 18 use a named library track ("The Final Countdown", Coldplay's "Hymn for the Weekend", Paramore's "Ain't It Fun", Mozart's "Lacrimosa", Wham!).
- The Creator Guide's taste matches what works: young.kippa's 967K Polymarket post runs on Mozart's "Lacrimosa", a track the guide names.
- Rules: a business account sees only the Commercial Music Library, and many trending tracks are not cleared for business use. Baking a commercial song into an "original sound" does not license it. Which account type the page uses decides the sound plan (question for the client).

### 2.11 What instantly marks a post as an ad
From the reference set plus the written sources (`raw\26\a_written_sources.md`, source numbers in brackets):
1. A logo or brand name on frame 0 (vendor ad-tell lists [70]; TikTok: keep early branding subtle [3]).
2. Template chrome: handles, pills, counters, footers, legal lines.
3. A designed card or dashboard instead of a moment; centred, many-sized type in a brand font [70].
4. Stock footage, a studio look, template motion (slide-ins, wipes, counting numbers). TikTok asks for a "DIY or not overly polished" look [1][2].
5. A pitch on screen: "Trade now", URLs, "zero fees", payout claims.
6. Library music with no reason, whoosh and ding packs.
7. Text that explains instead of reacting: no POV, no feeling, no joke.
Platform rules push the same way: Instagram stops recommending accounts that mostly repost (30 Apr 2026) and a credited screenshot of someone else's post is not original [13][19]; TikTok keeps reused clips with no creative edits off For You and names other apps' watermarks [74]; TikTok's guidelines in force from 24 Sep 2026 can cut the reach of undisclosed ads [73]; WSJ found most Polymarket-linked "bet" TikToks showed fake bets [36][37], so anything that looks staged will be read that way.

## 4. Proposal: a native look system for an Outcome page

### 4.1 The idea in one line
The page is a football and money page run from a phone by someone who trades on Outcome. Every post starts from a real moment. Outcome shows up the way a sportsbook shows up in a fan's post: as the real app screen, one number in the text, and one plain closing line. Nothing on screen looks like it came out of a template.
This is the kalshi.sports pattern (sheet 24): a real moment, one TikTok text box, no logo, the real app screen only as a short insert at the payoff (one of its 6 posts), 494K to 934K organic views on 7,516 followers.

### 4.2 Canvas and layers (bottom to top)
1. **Base, filling the frame**, one of: a phone screen recording of the Outcome app (ours, owned; livetape and goalcam already make these); a phone filming a laptop or TV showing the app; our own handheld footage; a real screenshot (news headline, X post, the app); a licensed photo or clip. Non-vertical footage sits on black or on a blurred copy of itself. No flat colour, gradient or skin backgrounds.
2. **The hook box** (spec 4.3).
3. **Spoken captions**, only when there is a voice.
4. **One sticker at most:** an emoji, a hand-drawn red circle or arrow, or a "reply to comment" bubble for a real comment.
5. Nothing else: no handle, avatar, pill, tag, counter, legend, footer, progress bar or watermark.

### 4.3 Caption style (exact spec at 1080x1920)
- **Font: TikTok Sans** (SIL OFL 1.1; Google Fonts and github.com/tiktok/TikTokSans). Copy in `raw\26\fonts\TikTokSans-Variable.ttf` with its OFL.txt; move it to `studio\assets\fonts\` to use it. It replaces Anton, Archivo Black and JetBrains Mono, and Bebas Neue except for one optional brand signature (4.7). Inter only appears inside the app recordings.
- **Hook box (TikTok "Classic" with background), the default:** TikTok Sans SemiBold, 54 to 60 px, black #000000 on a white #FFFFFF box per line, corner radius about 18 px, padding about 22 px left and right and 8 px top and bottom, centred, 1 or 2 lines, at most 10 words, sentence case or lowercase. Box centre between y 330 and 1150. It is also the one style that fits Outcome's guide (no outlines or shadows on type).
- **Spoken caption (CapCut word look), only over busy footage with a voice:** TikTok Sans ExtraBold, 80 to 110 px, white, 6 to 8 px black outline, 1 to 3 words at a time, each 0.3 to 0.8 s, centre between y 1050 and 1250. This clashes with the brand guide's no-outline rule: ask the client, else use small boxes.
- **Numbers (Jaden's rule, every number coloured):** in a box, the number gets its own chip: black text on Signal Green #1AFF57 (up / favourite / YES) or on Alert Red #FD6C6B (down / underdog / NO). In an outlined caption, the number itself is Signal Green or Alert Red. Never colour a whole sentence.
- **Emoji:** at most one, at the end of the hook. If our renderer burns it in, use a licensed set (Twemoji CC BY 4.0 or Noto Emoji OFL); Windows' emoji look off-platform (see mock sheet). Better: type the emoji line in the TikTok app. Never in the Outcome closing line.
- **Never:** Anton / Bebas caps with yellow keywords, more than two text sizes in a frame, text under 40 px, text in the bottom 20% or the right-hand 160 px, text on the app's chart, logo or YES / NO buttons (client rule).

### 4.4 Text rules
- Target 4 to 9 readable words on screen at any moment and 0 tiny words (now 10.5 + 27).
- The hook box may stay the whole video; everything else comes and goes.
- The source line ("chance from Outcome, 13 Oct, 21:00 CEST") and the risk line go in the post caption, with #ad first (client rule). If counsel wants an on-screen label, use one TikTok-style line "ad" at 40 px for the first 3 s (India's ASCI asks for at least 3 s on clips up to 15 s) plus the platform's paid-partnership toggle.
- Write like the brand guide but sound like a fan: short statements, one number, no hedges, no exclamation marks, never bet / wager / gambling / punt / payout %. Lowercase is fine for meme lines.

### 4.5 How the chance appears (pick one per post)
1. **The real app, screen-recorded** (default): full height, or a punch-in on title, score and chart; the moving line is the motion. A visible status bar and a tap make it read as a phone. The app's own "Outcome" watermark and YES / NO buttons do the branding.
2. **Phone pointed at the screen, or phone in hand:** film a laptop showing the Outcome market with a phone, finger on the line, one red arrow drawn on (rugengine.sol did this with DexScreener); or film a hand holding a phone with the market open in front of the match on a TV or in a pub. The cheapest native devices we have. Each needs a couple of minutes of real filming per post; a recording pasted into a stock hand-and-phone clip usually looks fake, so film it for real.
3. **Screenshot plus box:** a crop of the real market card under a TikTok box. Slideshow-friendly.
4. **One number in the text:** "barca 76%" in the hook, the number on a Signal Green chip. Works over any footage, edits included.
5. **The trade panel as the "slip":** the real Buy panel (side, Amount, Payout) as a screenshot, only in how-to posts and only with real, small, dated amounts (client: under $1,000, never fake trades). Never call it a bet slip.
Never: designed % cards, gauges, progress bars, weather icons, marble races as data, 3D numbers.

### 4.6 Cover style
- The cover is the strongest real frame of the post (the moment, or the app when the line jumps) plus the same hook box as frame 0, at most 7 words, inside the central 1080x1350 band.
- No logo, no handle, no counters, no "???" and no number that is not final and true (studio rule from 13 Oct). Never start on black.
- Check at thumbnail size (about 360 px wide): if the hook cannot be read, shorten it.

### 4.7 How much branding
- We add zero logos. Outcome is visible where its UI is visible (app header, chart watermark, YES / NO buttons), named once in text or voice at 50% to 70% of the video (the client's plug window), and closed with "Trade the outcome." in the post caption or as the last box. Fee and speed wording stays on hold (13 Oct facts).
- Brand on screen for at most 25% of the runtime (Meta / Toluna: 4.8x more likely to rank top for direct response). The app can stay longer only when the app moment is the story (a goal moving the price).
- Optional signature, about 1 post in 5: Outcome's own line "the outcome was inevitable." as the last beat, Bebas Neue caps on a black strip (their og-image look). Ask the client first: their guide wants PP Neue Corp.
- Colour comes only from real app screens and the two number chips. No brand-colour backgrounds.

### 4.8 Movement and sound
- Pick a rhythm per format: one continuous real shot with a box (memes, fan moments, screen recordings), or a fast cut edit (3+ cuts per 10 s). Something real must move in the first second. Hard cuts, 5% to 10% punch-ins on the number or the goal, hand-held feel. No slide-ins, wipes, eased counters or 3D flips.
- Length: memes and moments 6 to 20 s; story posts 25 to 60 s (Creator Guide: about 1 minute, finance 40 to 60 s, plug at 50% to 70%).
- Sound: bake real audio (the moment's own sound, our filming, a voice) or a licensed track, as most native posts do; no whoosh / tick / ding packs. If the page is a business account, pick Commercial Music Library tracks in the app at upload; the Creator Guide's dramatic tracks (Lacrimosa, Lucifer Waltz) set the mood for story posts. Epidemic Sound licence scope for client posts is still an open question.
