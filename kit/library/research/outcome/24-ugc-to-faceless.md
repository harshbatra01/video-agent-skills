# 24 UGC to faceless: what creators post for prediction, betting, fantasy and trading apps, and the no-creator version for Outcome

Research, 14 Oct 2026. Track: creator videos (2025 to Oct 2026) for Polymarket, Kalshi, Novig, PrizePicks, Underdog, Robinhood, crypto apps and betting pages, and how each format runs on a page with no creator on camera. Read first for context (not repeated here): `00-client-brief.md`, `02-reference-posts.md`, `22-video-formats-round2.md` section 4.

**Read me in 6 lines**
1. 106 videos checked with stats (82 TikTok and YouTube in section 8.1, 24 Instagram reels in 8.2). 72 downloaded (media only) and watched as frame sheets, 65 with Whisper transcripts. One picture of the 12 winners: `raw\24\sheets\00_top12_overview.jpg`.
2. The partner videos that did best are not about the app. The market shows up as a 1.5 to 8 second insert, mostly between 20% and 77% of the runtime, framed as the stakes ("chance to win", "who do you have money on", "WHO GOT? PICK ONE"). Clip pages put it in the last seconds.
3. The shared visual is the real app screen (market page, chart, position card), never a designed graphic. Tiny accounts reach 88x to 1,040x their followers with one caption over a real screen.
4. Borrowed clips plus one relatable line are the cheapest wins: a film rant captioned "When Your Parlay Keep Losing By 1" did 122x, Thanos "throw that paycheck into Robinhood" 30x, a Mourinho interview 21x, a Polymarket banner under an NFL clip 345x.
5. Two warnings. "Kalshi partner" spam is now a meme of its own (a complaint video did 4,248,164 views on 5,890 followers), and Polymarket's paid videos with staged bets became a Wall Street Journal story. Outcome's edge is that every trade is on-chain: use real trades, real events, small realistic amounts.
6. Limits: TikTok is blocked in India, so TikTok numbers come from tikwm (single videos) and urlebird (search, hashtags, profiles). Followers are today's counts, not at posting. Instagram followers sit behind a login, so Instagram reels have plays but no ratio.

## 3. What the best videos have in common

1. **The market is a short insert inside content people would watch anyway.** Measured on 12 partner videos (frames seen): inserts last 1.5 to 8 s. Eight of the 12 put it between about 20% and 77% of the runtime (Hieuman 58 to 69%, ASP FC 66 to 73%, Stemplewski 66 to 77%, young.kippa's Arsenal video 49 to 57%, Barca Legion 60 to 67%, Xaid 21 to 25% and 52 to 56%, juicedupfootball 34 to 38%, slimdookiesports 40 to 44%). Clip-page style videos put it at the end (dwts50 90 to 100%, michael_talks1 88%, lucavlad_'s predictions 84%, HangTime the last 4 s, heard in the transcript). The Creator Guide's "8 to 10 s plug" is longer than what the winners used.
2. **The product is the real app screen.** Market page, chart card, position screen or a phone held up to the camera, in almost every partner video. The exceptions are caption-only posts (Bobbi Althoff, hotgirlstalksports) and a banner (Ice Cold Football).
3. **Hooks are situations, challenges or shocks, not odds.** "When Your Parlay Keep Losing By 1", "POV: Trump Tweeted During Your Trade", "I gave France one of their GOATS every simulation until they can beat Spain", "Someone dropped 2.9 million...". Odds come in later as the stakes. This matches round 2 ("keep odds out of the title").
4. **Short wins the ratio.** 9 of the 12 highest ratios in section 8.1 are under 20 seconds: one caption over one clip or one screen.
5. **Native text, no design system.** Small white TikTok text for memes and POVs; a white rounded box at the top for clip pages; word-by-word karaoke captions with one coloured word for voice-led videos; a white title card for TV clips.
6. **Sound.** Voice ("original sound") for stories, rants and sims; the clip's own audio or a trending sound for memes; a show theme (Dancing with the Stars) or classical (Mozart's Lacrimosa on young.kippa's Arsenal rant) for emotion.
7. **CTAs are rare.** Codes appear only in paid partner posts (Stemplewski's "DOWNLOAD KALSHI, USE CODE 'JAKE' TO GET $20 TO START!", the Ice Cold Football banner "Get $10 + early access with code: GET10"). Memes have none.
8. **Small accounts win with screens and memes; big creators win with production** (game sims, stunts) at 1x to 17x.
9. **Trust matters now.** Staged bets put Polymarket in the WSJ; Kalshi's "money hack" and "side hustle from bed" ads were called out by Media Matters. Outcome can show only real trades and say so.

## 6. Rules that matter for these formats

- Brief rules (`00-client-brief.md`): no "bet" or "betting"; never guarantee or exaggerate profits; realistic money (title under $10k, trades around $1,000 or less); the real event shown; "not financial advice" on finance and crypto; never cover the graph, lines, names, logo or buy buttons.
- Real trades only. The Polymarket story (TechCrunch on the WSJ, 21 Jun 2026: about 1,100 videos reviewed, many filmed on "near-perfect copies" of the site showing trades that were not real, creators told not to say they were paid) is the thing to avoid. Outcome's trades are public on Hyperliquid: say so on screen.
- Usernames and P&L of real traders: still on HOLD with the client (`00-status.md`). Mask until cleared.
- Fee and speed wording ("zero fees", "world's fastest") is not approved and partly inaccurate (`00-status.md`); scripts here avoid it.
- Clip rights: #1, #5 and #8 lean on borrowed clips; meme pages run on this, a brand-linked page is a bigger target. Prefer licensed reactions, short transformative cuts, or the page's own captures.
- Platform branded-content rules and India's law: see `03-platform-rules.md` and `00-status.md`. Paid or per-view posts need the upfront "Ad" label plus the platform's paid-partnership label.
- Trending sounds may not be available to business or branded accounts on TikTok (commercial music library only): UNVERIFIED for the page type Vision Clipping will use; check before planning sound-led formats.
