# Faceless VIDEO formats for Outcome (research, 13 Oct 2026)

Job: video formats (real footage, motion, story, variety) for the faceless Outcome pages, found by checking what performs in 2025 to 2026 in football, sports, finance, crypto, prediction markets and meme niches. 22 formats: K1 to K11 are the 11 formats the earlier research already found (kept and extended with second-by-second recipes, sound and length); V1 to V11 are new; five originals follow. All numbers were read on 13 Oct 2026, except those marked "earlier report".

Read me in 4 lines: (1) I opened no video. "Seconds" lines are my reading of the cover frame plus YouTube's three preview stills (about 25, 50 and 75 percent of each Short), titles and descriptions, marked (inferred). The stills are saved in `raw\20-video-formats\`. (2) Sound on YouTube Shorts cannot be measured this way, so it is UNVERIFIED unless stated; for TikTok, sound comes from TikTok's own metadata. (3) TikTok search is blocked, so the new formats are proven on YouTube Shorts and still need a TikTok test. (4) Reach ratio = views divided by the channel's current subscribers (or followers).

## 4. Five original ideas (only a prediction-market page can make these)

All five need data nobody else has: the minute-by-minute price line, news timestamps against price, or probabilities as numbers. None is proven; each is marked UNVERIFIED until a 5-post test.

### O-A. Calling the Line (a commentator reads the price like a match)
- **Hook (0 to 3 s):** a big "City 71%" and a calm AI commentator: "Kickoff. City, seventy-one percent."
- **Middle:** the voice calls every wobble like play-by-play ("seventy-two... seventy-three...") with the pitch rising on climbs and a crowd bed swelling; a goal: "OH! And it drops to thirty-eight!"
- **Payoff:** "Full time. Resolved: City, one hundred." or the sad drop to zero.
- **End and loop:** "Next kickoff, eight o'clock", then the opening line again.
- **Assets and sound:** minute prices, a script generated from the price deltas, a stock AI voice (not a clone of a real commentator), a crowd bed, SFX hits. **Length:** 20 to 30 s.
- **Why instant:** everyone knows sports commentary; the line is the ball.
- **Daily:** Full automatic. **Risk:** AI voice label; never imitate a real commentator.

### O-B. The Market Moved First
- **Hook:** a clock "18:02" and a lineup card with a name crossed out, then "18:03: -7 points".
- **Middle:** the line and a post timeline stacked; the price steps before the headline spreads. **Payoff:** "Prices moved 90 seconds after the team sheet." **End:** the next game's team-sheet time as the loop hook.
- **Assets:** archive prices, team-sheet or injury timestamps from a football data feed, post times read from the source (not guessed). **Length:** 15 to 25 s.
- **Why instant:** one clock, one cause.
- **Daily:** Semi (the timestamp must be checked). **Risk:** causation (say "moved after", not "because"); anything that hints at inside information.

### O-C. What a Goal Is Worth
- **Hook:** a ball bounces on an empty chart: "A goal in minute 5: +11 points. A goal in minute 88: +61."
- **Middle:** each dot is a real goal from archived matches with its swing; a curve builds minute by minute (median per 15 minutes) and the "most valuable goal of the month" glows.
- **Payoff:** "A late goal is worth about 5 times an early one" (computed, with the sample size N on screen). **End:** next month's update tag.
- **Assets:** the archive (needs 100 or more goals for a steady curve). **Length:** 20 to 30 s. Variants: red cards, penalties.
- **Why instant:** a simple question with a surprising number.
- **Daily:** Full once the archive grows; run it monthly. **Risk:** small samples (always show N); no edge claims.

### O-D. 100 Parallel Matches
- **Hook:** a grid of 100 tiny pitches and the title "What 63% looks like".
- **Middle:** each cell resolves in a quick pulse (blue = City win, grey = draw, red = upset), accelerating; a counter climbs to 63, 22, 15.
- **Payoff:** the real match result lights one cell: "This was one of the 15." **End:** the next match as the loop.
- **Assets:** probabilities only. **Sound:** a rising tick scale, a soft pop per cell. **Length:** 12 to 18 s. (Casino-style plinko is avoided on purpose; the grid is a standard risk-communication icon array.)
- **Why instant:** it shows what a percentage means in one picture.
- **Daily:** Full automatic. **Risk:** label "illustration, not a forecast".

### O-E. Matchday Forecast (the weather report)
- **Hook:** a TV weather map of Europe: "Tonight: seventy percent chance of City."
- **Middle:** sun for a 75%+ favourite, cloud for a mild favourite, storm for a coin flip, lightning for an upset shot of 15%+; "this week's outlook" for the title races.
- **Payoff:** next morning's "forecast check": how many sunny calls held. **End:** "Back to the studio" bumper, loop.
- **Assets:** a map, weather icons, a bland AI voice, a jazz bed, probabilities. **Length:** 20 to 25 s.
- **Why instant:** everyone knows what "30% chance of rain" means.
- **Daily:** Full automatic. **Risk:** low. Say "the crowd's forecast".
