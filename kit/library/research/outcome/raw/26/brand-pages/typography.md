## Typography

Outcome uses PP Neue Corp in two weights. Narrow Ultrabold is the display face, used for headlines and captions. Normal Medium is the body face, used for subheads and running text. Headlines are always uppercase.

### Font Family

PP Neue Corp Narrow Ultrabold

Display / Headlines & Captions

Ultrabold

`font-family: var(--font-neu-ultrabold);`

PP Neue Corp Narrow Medium

Body / Subheads & Running Text

Medium

`font-family: var(--font-neu-medium);`
