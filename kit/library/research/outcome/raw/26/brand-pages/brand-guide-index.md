## What Is Outcome

Outcome is a prediction markets protocol built on Hyperliquid. Users trade on real-world events: sports, crypto, macro, politics. Revenue flows from fees to a self-funded, self-governed community that staked $20M in HYPE tokens. 200K+ testnet users. Launched into a maturing institutional landscape. One market, built by its community.

## What Makes Outcome Different

- **No admin keys.** No parameter changes without community vote.

- **Data-driven.** Manipulation-proof.

- **Faster than any competitor.**

- **Cross-chain collateral.** Existing perps collateral usable across multi-chain instantly.

- **Near-instant claims.** Disputes have windows, then close. You get paid.

- **60x lower fees than Polymarket** (3bps vs 1.8%).

## The Promise

**You saw it. You acted. You were right.**

We are not the content. We are the race car. The driver competes, challenges themselves, disappears into the drive. The platform stays invisible. The drive stays.

## Design Language

View section →

Outcome's design language is built on a single posture: **quiet inevitability**. The product, the marketing, and the chrome around them all read the same way - confident, declarative, uncluttered. We state things. We do not pitch.

### Brand character

A reliable rebel. Outcome is the calm in a market that runs on noise. We don't shout because we don't need to - the outcome is inevitable.

### Voice & tone

Short, declarative English. We avoid hedging, qualifiers, and salesy adjectives. When in doubt, cut the sentence in half.

"The outcome is inevitable."
"One market, built by its community."
"A prediction-markets protocol on Hyperliquid."

### Visual signature

- **Type** - PP Neue Corp, Narrow Ultrabold for displays, Narrow Medium for body.

- **Colour** - green, fuchsia, turquoise, neutral. Four hues, five steps each. No pastels, no off-brand accents.

- **Logo** - the icon mark or wordmark only. Never reconstructed, never gradient-filled, never traced.

- **Imagery** - high-contrast, oil-painted, and shot wide. Negative space carries weight.

For the full system, see Logo, Colour Palette, Typography, and Iconography.

## In Two Words

**Reliable Rebels.**

Trustworthy infrastructure with a rebellious culture and approach. We are of the people we serve.

## Archetype: Sage + Outlaw

### The Outlaw Law

Liberty, disruption, giving power back. Anti-establishment. Community over corporation.

### The Sage Law

Truth-seeking, dynamism, accuracy. Finds answers. Willing to call out rigged systems AND replace them.

This combination means Outcome never just criticizes. It builds the alternative. It proves the point with working product, not manifestos.

## Brand Adjectives

- **Innovative.** We build what does not exist yet.

- **Transparent.** Open protocol, open governance, open books.

- **High-conviction.** We do not hedge. We ship.

Do

- Be the trusted contrarian who backs conviction with evidence

- Show rebellious energy through design and language choices

- Let the technology and results speak. Stay confident, not loud.

Don't

- Come across as gambling-coded or casino-adjacent

- Be generic, corporate, or flashy

- Be exclusive or low-IQ. Outcome is sharp, never dumb.

- Celebrate disruption without offering a better alternative

## Voice & Tone

View section →

## Voice

**Quietly forceful.** Never hedging. Short, declarative English. Do not celebrate it either way. Defined, no boosted qualifiers.

Outcome sounds like someone who already knows the answer and is not trying to convince you. The confidence is earned, not performed. We state. We do not pitch.

## Tone by Context

### Product UI / In-App

Minimal. Functional. No personality for the sake of personality. Let the interface be invisible. Clear labels, zero fluff.

### Social / Community

Sharp, direct, occasionally provocative. This is where the Outlaw archetype lives. Speak like a member of the community, not a brand account.

### Marketing / Launch

Confident, declarative, cinematic. Big statements backed by real numbers. No hype without substance.

### Documentation / Onboarding

Clear, helpful, precise. The Sage archetype. Respect the reader. No unnecessary jargon, but do not dumb it down.

## Banned Words and Phrases

These words are off-limits in all Outcome communications. They either signal gambling, corporate hedging, or generic marketing.

- **Never use:** bet, wager, wagering, gambling, punt, game-changing

- **Never use:** VCs, discounts, payout percentages

- **Avoid:** stacks, casino-adjacent framing

- **Avoid:** we believe, we think, perhaps, maybe, hopefully

Let the numbers connect. Let the results speak. If a sentence needs a qualifier to feel true, cut the sentence.

Do

- Use short, declarative sentences

- Speak with conviction. No qualifiers.

- Frame prediction markets as skill and signal

- State facts. Let the reader draw the conclusion.

- Sound like a community member, not a corporation

Don't

- Use gambling language (bet, wager, punt)

- Use corporate hedging (we believe, we think, perhaps)

- Be casual or use slang without purpose

- Over-explain. Trust the reader.

- Use exclamation marks or hype language

## Writing Examples

### Good

"Faster settlement. Lower fees. No admin keys."

"You saw it. You acted. You were right."

"One market, built by its community."

### Bad

"We believe Outcome could potentially revolutionize the prediction market space!"

"Place your bets on the future of decentralized wagering."

"Our game-changing platform offers incredible discounts for early users."

## What DESIGN.md Is

`DESIGN.md` is the machine-readable version of this brand guide. It is a single Markdown file that encodes Outcome's visual system, palette, typography, components, and layout rules in the format defined by Google's open-source Stitch DESIGN.md specification.

Drop it into any AI design tool or coding agent to generate screens, graphics, and components that match Outcome without having to re-explain the brand each time.

## How to Use It

### With Stitch

Place `DESIGN.md` in your Stitch project directory. Stitch passes the full file contents to Gemini alongside every prompt, so generated designs apply Outcome's specific hex values, type system, placement grid, and texture rules instead of making arbitrary decisions.

### With Claude Code, Cursor, or other coding agents

Add `DESIGN.md` to the root of your codebase. Reference it in your agent's system prompt, a `CLAUDE.md`, or `.cursorrules` so the model reads it as part of project context. Any UI code the agent writes will inherit Outcome's palette, component styling, and layout conventions.

### As a brand prompt

Paste the contents into any chat interface with an LLM. Ask it to generate social posts, landing pages, decks, or copy, and the output will follow Outcome's voice, color system, and visual hierarchy.
