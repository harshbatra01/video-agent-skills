## 2. Auto-caption looks
CapCut
- Presets named in 2026 guides: karaoke highlight, pop by word, bounce, typewriter, "TikTok classic". One click restyles the whole track [66]. Another guide says CapCut has no fixed preset names [65].
- Three looks: one word at a time; current word highlighted inside a visible phrase (karaoke); short multi-word blocks [65].
- The top animated packs need Pro [66].
Look trends (VENDOR, 2026)
- Word by word is baseline: 2 to 3 words at a time. Full-sentence blocks look dated [57].
- The bold yellow "Hormozi" combo (yellow, caps, sound effects, emoji) is saturated. Rising: "dynamic minimalism". White bold text, black outline, small entry animation, no emoji, no flashing colours [57].
- Inline emoji in subtitles is fading. Standalone emoji stickers remain [57].
- Counterpoint: Meta found emoji as a native element made ads 2.5 times more likely to rank top 20% for purchase intent. That is ads, not subtitles [20][21]. TikTok lists emojis as an attention trigger [3].
- Colour: white with black outline is baseline. Yellow #f7c204 for keywords. Pill backgrounds rising. Neon green and electric blue fading [57].
- Submagic data says red, neon green and neon yellow are the most common subtitle colours [63]. This conflicts with [57].
- Hormozi recipe: Anton font, caps, yellow stroke, pop-in, max 4 to 6 words over 2 lines [64].
- Fonts in Submagic exports (2M videos, Apr 2025 data): Montserrat 61%, Rubik 7.9%, Fira Sans Condensed 6.5%, Gabarito 6.3%, Poppins 5.1% [63].
Words per screen, size, position
- TikTok official: text at "5-10 words per second" as written. Too fast to read, so treat it as a ceiling [1].
- 1 to 3 words at a time for word by word, 3 to 5 for teaching [56]. 3 to 7 words per chunk, 1 to 3 s each [61]. About 6 to 8 words per screen in the TikTok default look [70]. 32 to 42 characters a line, 2 lines max [66].
- Size at 1080x1920: 55 to 75 pt [56]. By font: Montserrat 60 to 75 px, Anton 70 to 85, Bebas Neue 75 to 95, Archivo Black 80 to 100, TikTok Sans 60 to 75, Inter 50 to 60 [55].
- Outline 5 to 8 px thick, shadow 1 to 2 px. Whole caption block 12 to 20% of frame height [55]. Another guide: at least 48 to 60 px [62].
- Position: lower-middle third [56]. Centre to upper-middle, avoid bottom 25% and top 15% [61]. Centre third [62].
- TikTok's default captions sit in the bottom third [70], which is the busiest UI area (section 3).
Instagram and others

## 4. Hooks
- TikTok official: state the content proposition in the first 3 s, land the hook in the first 6 s [1].
- TikTok: 90% of ad recall impact lands in the first 6 s (a 2020 TikTok study) [4]. Creative Codes: hook, body, close. Keep branding subtle in the opening [3].
- Meta/Toluna (Dec 2025): a hook using both visual and audio cues in the first 1 to 2 s made a top-20% purchase-intent rank 1.5 times more likely [20][21].
- Instagram's latest official Reels guide (covered 1 Oct 2026): first-frame text can matter about as much as the cover. Text layers can be timed separately, so a question can open and the answer land later [28]. A hook need not be spoken [28].
- Hook text length (VENDOR rules of thumb): 4 to 9 words, readable in under 1 s [69]. First caption about 5 words, on screen at 0.0 s before any voice [68]. Hook under 3 s, ideally under 2 [60].
- Question or statement: no study found. One vendor says statements dominate and questions should be rare [60]. Another says most examples are statements [68]. UNVERIFIED either way.
- Numbers: concrete numbers help. Inflated numbers draw hostile comments [60]. VENDOR, no data.
- Curiosity gap: open one specific loop. Vague loops fail [60]. The payoff must be real [69].
- First frame: no greeting. Start on the best line or the result frame. Put the hook on screen as text [60]. Keep it off the bottom third and right edge [68].
- Sound: vendors say many people watch muted [60][70]. UNVERIFIED. TikTok says 88% of users call sound vital [4]. Meta says Reels play with sound unless turned off [12]. So text must work alone, but sound is on by default.
- Crypto and finance pages (Oct 2026 report): hooks with exact loss figures, price levels and dates, or a news headline style ("CRYPTO IS CRASHING"), reported to beat generic confessions. Not quantified [72].

## 5. What reads as an ad, and what reads as native
Platform stance
- TikTok: go for a DIY or not overly polished look. Use creators, employees or customers. Use trends, memes, challenges [1].
- "Don't Make Ads" (2021): fit the platform, use native tools (green screen, duet, react), real life over high production. Ads must still be labelled as ads [2].
- Creative Codes: think TikTok-first, shoot 9:16 at 720p or better, leave room for the UI, use music, transitions, movement, text overlays, emoji [3].
- TikTok research: TikTok-first ads drive 3.3x more action. 74% of viewers say they catch attention. 77% like brands using trends or memes [4].
- Creator ads: 70% higher CTR and 159% higher engagement at the same CPM (Feb 2024 to Jan 2025 data). Ads from a creator's account: 59% higher engagement, 16% higher 6-second view-through [5].
- Meta: creator-partner native Reels creative gave 5% lower cost per result and 11% higher conversion rate (15 A/B tests) [12].
- Meta/Toluna: brand on screen for at most 25% of runtime = 4.8x (direct response). Product shown more than once 2.7x. Everyday scenes 1.5x. Speech plus music 2.1x. Emoji 2.5x [20][21].
Ad tells and native signals (VENDOR)
- Ad tells: logo in the first frame, studio lighting, polished grade, smooth b-roll, stock look, custom brand fonts, centred text [70].
- Native: default TikTok caption look in the bottom third, handheld phone footage, natural light, jump cuts [70].
- Native opener patterns: reply to a real comment, POV scene, "I found this", demo first, problem in plain speech [68].
- Tension on branding: Meta says brand plus main message within 5 s made a top-20% purchase-intent rank 1.7x more likely [20]. Vendors call a logo in frame one an ad tell [70]. Creative Codes says keep early branding subtle [3].
- Polished still wins for product clarity, brand memory, complex demos, premium goods. Most stats in these pieces come from platforms with a stake [71].
- No independent test found on motion-graphics templates or brand colours as ad tells. UNVERIFIED.
Prediction-market peers
- Kalshi and Polymarket use humorous, meme-heavy captions and meme templates [35].
- Kalshi TikTok ads: young creators with on-screen earnings claims ("made $270 this week") [33].
- Staged "organic" clips with logos on props (Knicks fan chant) [34].
- Oct 2026 report on crypto and trading brands: raw footage under a consistent branded wrapper (Polymarket "TRENDING" on Instagram), animated characters (Pudgy Penguins), sci-fi world-building (Robinhood) [72].

## 6. Faceless formats in 2026
Format data
- Metricool (2.31M TikTok posts, 92k+ accounts, Jan to Feb 2025 vs 2026): video got about 5x the views and 6x the interactions of images or carousels [31]. Another write-up says 5.6x and 7.8x [30].
- Image/carousel posting rose about 140%, video 72%. For images: views down 23%, reach down 20%, interactions down 62% [30].
- Fanpage Karma (about 700k posts, Jan to May 2025): TikTok carousels had 81% higher engagement rate but only 3% more reach, shares about a third lower. Instagram carousels: 13% more reach, 1.5x the Reels engagement rate [32].
- MINE: carousels win per view, video wins on total reach. No faceless-specific or music-specific data found.
- Instagram Aug 2026: Bonuses pay eligible creators for photo and carousel posts [38].
Memes, clip + caption, screenshots
- Instagram meme formats, 2 Sep to 7 Oct 2026: image with text overlay; multi-slide carousel or screenshot; reaction image or screenshot; video template or Reel with text over photos. No sports or finance memes in that window [41].
- Screenshot pages: Instagram says a screenshot of someone else's post is not original, even with credit. Own text, edits or voiceover can make a meme original [19].
- Border, watermark, subtitles or a caption credit alone do not count [13].
- TikTok: reused clips with no creative edits are kept off For You. Subtitles over the original audio do not count [74].
- 30 faceless Instagram ideas (quote cards, niche memes, finance micro-lessons, anonymous advice on plain backgrounds), no performance ranking [77].
Bet slips, P&L, app recordings
- Oct 2026 crypto report: profit screenshots stay common but rarely show downside. Real app screen recordings (wallet trade demo, loan app walkthrough, feature UI reels) show up more on Instagram. "Relatable disaster" posts use exact losses and dates [72].

## 10. Disclosure look
- TikTok: switch on "Disclose commercial content", pick "Your brand" or "Branded content". The "Commercial content" label shows on the post or under the description. Third-party branded content reads "Paid partnership". Brand tag authorization is 60 days by default [9].
- Vendors say the label cannot be edited after posting. The official page does not say. UNVERIFIED.
- Instagram: "Paid partnership with [brand]" once the brand approves. Before approval it shows without the name. Works in Feed, Stories, Reels, Live. Exact on-screen position not stated [14].
- TikTok ad captions: white, fixed font, no links, @ or hashtags (non-Spark). Spark Ads captions max 4 lines [6].
- FTC (2019 guide): put the disclosure inside the video, not only the caption. Use audio and on-screen text. Overlay it long enough to read. Avoid the end of a post and "more" links. Do not bury it in hashtags. Do not rely on the platform tool alone [15].
- India ASCI labels: Advertisement, Ad, Sponsored, Collaboration, Partnership, Employee, Free gift, Affiliate, or a platform tool [16][17].
- ASCI timing: video 15 s or less, label on screen at least 3 s. 15 s to 2 min, at least one third of the length. Over 2 min, the whole mention section. Superimpose it if there is no caption [16][17].
- ASCI finance: investment advice needs SEBI registration and qualifications shown upfront. Crypto ads need the line "Crypto products and NFTs are unregulated and can be highly risky" on screen 5 s and spoken at the end [16].
- ASCI rules for betting or prediction markets: not found on the page I read. UNVERIFIED.
- How peers look: labels barely visible or missing, a hashtag naming only the agency, "partner" tags added to bios after press calls [34][37].
- MINE, not legal advice: use the platform label plus an on-screen "Ad" text for at least 3 s on short clips. Check with counsel.
