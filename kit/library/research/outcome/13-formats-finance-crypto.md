## 2. Ranked top 8 for a brand-new faceless page

Ranking logic: reach evidence (is the topic or shape proven?), fit with live Outcome data, how much of it can be automated, and compliance risk. Outcome-native formats (F03 to F05) rank high because the data is real, public and unique to us. I found no page publishing anything like it.

1. F03 Odds-as-news card. Daily scheduled card plus event cards (Fed day 28 Oct, IPO news). Media proof: CNN and CNBC put prediction-market odds on air, and Dow Jones puts them in stories and charts, all within the last 10 months (section 5, F03). Cheapest to build, safest wording.
2. F05 Probability ladder. The Anthropic first-day market-cap ladder (80% / 74% / 58% / 32% / 26%) is a post nobody else can make today. IPO news has proven reach (NBC 3.35M plays on one TikTok).
3. F04 Odds mover alert. Pure Outcome data, runs itself, repeats every day. Format is UNVERIFIED as a page style, so test first with 10 posts.
4. F07 Spike or crash explainer with one chart. Highest verified Shorts outliers (x240) and it matches the oil, gold, silver and BTC markets. Needs a fact check before posting.
5. F01 Breaking photo card for TikTok. A 171-follower page got 98,760 plays with a 2-slide photo post and a long caption. Use it for the F03 and F04 outputs.
6. F09 Event-day explainer. Carousel of 5 slides, high save value, education is allowed on TikTok.
7. F10 Chart comparison with a twist. Strong comment and send triggers. Keep it non-political (market vs market).
8. F12 Concept in 30 seconds / myth-busting. Lowest risk filler that also teaches "price = chance". Evergreen.

Second wave (add after week 3): F06 IPO explainer, F08 geopolitics-to-oil explainer, F11 politics-meets-money. Do not start with: F02 (rights), F13 to F16 (weak or unproven), F17 (low reach), F18 (risk jokes).

## 3. How the numbers were pulled, and the limits

- TikTok: tikwm.com `user/info`, `user/posts` and `feed/search` all return a Cloudflare "Just a moment" challenge from this PC (GET and POST, with headers, through r.jina.ai, and through WebFetch, which got 403). Only the single-video call works: POST `https://www.tikwm.com/api/` with `url=<video url>` gives plays, likes, comments, shares, saves, duration, photo-post image count, sound. For account lists I read TikTok's own profile embed `https://www.tiktok.com/embed/@handle` through `https://r.jina.ai/` in HTML mode: it returns the follower count, the pinned posts and the latest 9 to 13 posts with play counts. TikTok medians below are the median of those latest non-pinned posts. Post dates come from the video ID (ID >> 32 = Unix seconds).
- Instagram: follower counts from the public profile meta tag (facebookexternalhit user agent). Posts from hashtag pages (r.jina.ai HTML) and `/reel/<code>/embed/captioned/` (shows likes, comments, caption, owner and owner followers; never views). Post dates decoded from the shortcode and checked against a known dated post (Powell 29 Apr 2026). No post lists exist without login, so for IG I give "likes per follower" instead of an outlier score.
- YouTube: channel `/shorts` tab (median of the last 48 Shorts) and watch pages (views, likes, publish date, length). Direct access works from this PC.
- Outcome data: Hyperliquid public info API, no key (section 4).
- I downloaded no video or image files. Layout, fonts and colours were not inspected. "Looks like" lines are inferred from post type (photo or video), slide count, length, caption length and sound, plus the account's known house style where I say "from memory".
- Not verified or not reachable: Wall St Engine short-form accounts, Kobeissi Letter on TikTok, the Polymarket Sports TikTok posts, Kalshi and Polymarket official TikTok post lists (embed returns none), any candle-by-candle or countdown example.

Recipe that worked:
- `{"type":"outcomeMeta"}` returns 313 outcomes and 35 questions with templates: binaryPrice (98, the 15-minute and hourly up/down), priceTouch (16), companyIpoFirstDayMarketCap (10), companyIpoConfirmed (3), policyRateNoChange / Increase / Decrease, sports and awards.
- `{"type":"allMids"}` returns every token. Outcome 3600 has `#36000` (Yes) and `#36001` (No). The two always sum to 1. Yes price x 100 = the % chance. Payout multiplier = 1 / price (price 0.267 = x3.75).
- `{"type":"candleSnapshot","req":{"coin":"#36000","interval":"1d","startTime":...,"endTime":...}}` gives odds history for sparklines.
- `{"type":"l2Book","coin":"#36000"}` gives the order book (check the spread). `{"type":"recentTrades","coin":"#36000"}` gives fills.
- Guard rails for automation: only post a question when its Yes mids sum to 95% to 105%, the spread is small, and the last trade is recent. Show the time stamp on the card.

## 5. Detail per format

Common note for every Outcome version: show the market question, the % and the time stamp, put "not financial advice" on the card, never write "bet", never say a result "will" happen, and keep the chart and logo uncovered. CTA line is the client's; check the wording flag in section 7.

### F05 Probability ladder
- Platform: IG carousel, TikTok photo, Shorts.

- Tutorial pages that promise income ("$1,000 a day") get views but are the get-rich-quick pattern the client forbids and TikTok restricts.
- X-born card styles (Kobeissi, Wall St Engine) need their chart quality and speed; their short-form clones are small. Do not copy the look, copy the habit (one chart, one headline, fast).

## 7. Risk and wording notes for this client

- Banned words and claims: "bet/betting", profit promises, "you could have made $X", "easy money". Payout multipliers (x3.75, "$100 to $375") may be shown only as what the market pays if the outcome occurs, with the realistic-amount rule from the client brief.
- Finance posts carry "not financial advice, do your own research".
- CTA wording flag: the client line says "Trade with zero fees on Outcome.xyz". Earlier research in this folder (01-what-is-outcome.md) found a fee of about 0.05% to 0.14% on close or settle, so "zero fees" is not accurate. Ask the client for approved wording before launch. Platform and legal rules are in 03-platform-rules.md (TikTok branded-content policy for investment content, India's online gaming law for the editor's location, geo-blocked countries).
- Everything shown must be real: use Outcome numbers with a time stamp; mids can mislead on thin books (the second Fed listing sums to 143.8%). Facts from outside Outcome (Fed text, CPI, IPO terms, quotes) need a source slide.
- The Sep 2026 Fed hike (3.75% to 4.00% on 16 Sep, chair Kevin Warsh, 12 to 0 vote) and the SpaceX IPO terms came from search summaries of secondary pages and TikTok captions, not primary documents. Verify on federalreserve.gov and SEC filings before using them in any post.
