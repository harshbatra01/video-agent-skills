1. FACT CHECK
- Commonwealth Games, Kuala Lumpur (11-21 Sept 1998): she is not on Team England's 1998 roster (teamengland.org) and does not appear in the KL 5000m or 10,000m results (Wikipedia). So she missed the Games: confirmed.
- Why she missed them: a 2026 Stamford Mercury piece says she "fell ill and was later diagnosed with post-viral fatigue" after training for the Europeans and the Commonwealths. The European Athletics retrospective (seen only as a search snippet) says her summer was "curtailed by an energy-sapping virus". I found no 1998 press report giving the reason, so the exact wording "post viral fatigue syndrome" is UNVERIFIED against the press of the time.
- European Cross Country win: CONFIRMED. Ferrara, Italy, 13 Dec 1998, 5.6 km in 18:07, 3 seconds ahead of Annemari Sandell (FIN) and Olivera Jevtic (YUG). It was her first senior international title, four days before her 25th birthday.
- Rest of her 1998:
  - Jan: dropped out of the Durham cross country with flu.
  - 21 Mar: World Cross silver in Marrakech.
  - 5-mile road world best at Balmoral.
  - June: won the European Cup 5000m.
  - 2 Aug: won the 3000m at the British Grand Prix in Sheffield.
  - 19 Aug: European 10,000m in Budapest. She led the race but finished 5th in 31:36.51, then collapsed and was stretchered off.
  - 30 Aug: won a race at the SPAR British Challenge in Glasgow (PA caption).
  - Dec: 4th in the Brussels cross country.

Problems with the script:
- "My season was a write-off" overstates it. She won races and medals up to 30 Aug. Only the late summer was lost. Suggest wording like "my summer ended early".
- Immufix's own page (immufix.com/pages/paulas-journey) tells it differently: she took a holiday on two scoops a day, was training again when she got back, and went to altitude a month later. It never says "two weeks".
- The product was called "Breakthrough" in 1998 (immufix.com). Any 1998 packaging will not say Immufix.
- The Stamford Mercury article (14 Jul 2026) says "Paula has been taking Immufix since about 2002". That contradicts 1998, and the same article also says "more than quarter of a century". Worth asking them to correct it.

6. LICENSING AND RED FLAGS
- Allsport/Getty Sport frames: advertising use needs clearance from Getty UK ("contact your local office").
- PA/EMPICS frames on Getty are marked "UK OUT, IRELAND OUT". A UK client must license them from PA Images (PA Media) directly.
- Releases: Paula can sign for herself. Other athletes in the frame (Sandell, O'Sullivan and others), event logos and her 1998 kit logos need clearing, or cropping/blurring.
- Real headlines and mastheads need the publisher's permission. "Headlines" mocked up to look real but never published would breach the ASA rules.
- Biggest risk (not legal advice): CAP Code section 15 bans saying or implying that a food or supplement prevents, treats or cures disease, and testimonials are not exempt. "Post viral fatigue syndrome, then Immufix, then two weeks later training" implies treatment. Get CAP Copy Advice or legal sign-off before spending on media.
