## Premium effects research for the Paula Radcliffe 9:16 ads

I checked every YouTube title and channel name below. Motion Array, Behance and Adobe Help pages block my reader, so what I say about those comes from search listings.

### 1. Documentary photo montage
**Examples**
- Netflix trailers for the look you're aiming at: [UNTOLD Vol. 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j3IgmCgeMNo) and [UNTOLD 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fVNSt5c8-Uo).
- Elastic's [UNTOLD: Sign Stealer graphics](https://www.behance.net/gallery/206221371/UNTOLD-Sign-Stealer-Documentary-GFX) (Behance). These are textured, "pieced together" archive graphics made for the Netflix doc.
- Motion Array templates: [Photo Flash](https://motionarray.com/after-effects-templates/photo-flash/), [Torn Paper Slideshow](https://motionarray.com/after-effects-templates/torn-paper-slideshow-78485/), [Polaroid Photo Slideshow](https://motionarray.com/after-effects-templates/polaroid-photo-slideshow-132335/), [Digital Film Strip](https://motionarray.com/after-effects-templates/digital-film-strip/).

**Best tutorial:** Vane Motion, [5 EPIC Documentary Transitions (AE)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-2o0KA3RlyE).

**How to build it**
- **Premiere (fast):** hold each photo 3 to 6 frames with a slow scale push. Put a 1 to 2 frame white flash on the beats (a white Color Matte at opacity 0/100/0). Add grain and a light leak on top. For the polaroid look, use a white border PNG, a drop shadow and a slight tilt.
- **After Effects (more premium):** set the photos up as 3D polaroids scattered on a surface and push the camera in with depth of field. Make the flash an Exposure spike that blooms into the next photo. For torn edges, use a rough mask plus Turbulent Displace.

**SFX:** a shutter click and flash pop on each photo, a film projector rattle underneath, paper slides, and a low boom on the hero photo.

**Rights:** archive press photos need a commercial licence. "Editorial only" images can't be used in ads.

### 2. Newspaper and article headline callouts
**Examples**
- [Johnny Harris](https://www.youtube.com/@johnnyharris) is the reference style.
- Videohive [Newspaper Video Template](https://videohive.net/item/newspaper-video-template/51103043): AE, 4K, paper texture and highlighter reveals, $17.
- FlatpackFX [Vox-style unfolding paper](https://www.flatpackfx.com/blog/vox-style-unfold-paper-texture-effect-adobe-after-effects).
- [TypeFlow](https://www.typeflow.studio/news-article-animation-generator): a browser tool that makes headline and highlight clips for Premiere.

**Best tutorial:** Will Carmack, [Johnny Harris Style Text Animation: Full Breakdown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sxj0CSPmTjo), plus his [highlighter effect](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kxqJL2BMwnQ). For Premiere only: Flick Edit, [Vox/Johnny Harris newspaper in Premiere](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qjafHzNJLQ0).

**How to build it (AE)**
- Rebuild the headline as live text on a paper texture so it stays sharp in 9:16.
- Push the camera in slowly with a slight tilt.
- Highlighter: a solid with the Stroke effect on a mask path. Animate End from 0 to 100% and set the blend mode to Multiply.
- Cut-outs: rough-edged masks with drop shadows, stacked 2 or 3 deep with a stagger.
- Darken everything except the key line.
- Posterize Time at 12 fps gives the handmade, stepped look.

**SFX:** a paper flutter, a marker squeak on the highlight, a whoosh on the push, and a click when each cut-out lands.

**Rights:** check clearance before showing real newspaper mastheads in an ad.

### 3. Instagram-style Q&A sticker
**Examples**
- CreatorSet [Q&A Card Template V2](https://creatorset.com/products/instagram-q-a-card-template-v2) ($7.95).
- Envato [Instagram Quiz & Poll](https://elements.envato.com/instagram-quiz-poll-ZD64Q5J) (Premiere).
- Motion Array [Question & Answer Template](https://motionarray.com/premiere-pro-templates/question-answer-template-136595/).

**Best tutorial:** Motion Array, [Auto Scale Text Box in AE](https://motionarray.com/learn/after-effects/autoscale-text-after-effects/). The box resizes to fit each question, and you can save it as a .mogrt to use in Premiere. For the pop, see Motion Nations, [Message Pop-Up Animation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KLcl6ApKca0).

**Making it premium**
- A rounded card with a frosted-glass blur of the shot behind it.
- A header strip in the brand colour, and the question typing on word by word.
- Scale it from 0 to 106 to 100% over about 8 frames, with a soft shadow.
- Track it near her phone.

**Rights:** Meta's [brand rules](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/) say you need permission to show Instagram's interface in commercials. Make the card "inspired by" the sticker, without Instagram's logo or gradient.

**SFX:** a bubble pop, light typing ticks, and a swish out.

### 4. Only 1 of 5 stars lights up, plus review cards
**Examples**
- Concept references: Snowbird's [one-star review ads](https://www.adweek.com/creativity/this-ski-resort-turned-one-star-reviews-into-a-five-star-ad-campaign/) and Amazon's [Five Star Theater](https://www.adweek.com/creativity/amazon-casts-benedict-cumberbatch-to-turn-absurd-reviews-into-theater/).
- Envato [Star Rating Pack](https://elements.envato.com/star-rating-pack-VCLYRT8) (includes single-star reveals).
- Motion Array [Rating Elements Pack](https://motionarray.com/premiere-pro-templates/rating-elements-pack-587011/) (you set the stars inside Premiere).
- Envato [review cards](https://elements.envato.com/review-mockups-4W8S4DS) (Premiere).

**Best tutorial:** Framixel, [Star Rating Card Animation in AE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OTUYzw8qPwk).

**How to build it (AE)**
- Five star shapes with grey outlines, animating on 2 frames apart.
- Star 1 fills gold with an overshoot and a glow.
- Stars 2 to 5 flicker as if trying to light, then stay grey and sag slightly.
- Drive the fills from one "Rating" slider so the same .mogrt can show any rating later.
- A review card (name, 1 star, short quote) slides up.

**SFX:** a bright ding on star 1, a dull thud or fail buzz on the other four, and a swipe for the card.

### 5. Whiteboard tension reveal
**Examples**
- Mister Horse [Hand Drawn Stuff](https://misterhorse.com/products/hand-drawn-stuff/3306): a paid Animation Composer pack with 147 real-marker circles, arrows, lines and scribbles. It works in Premiere and AE, and it's the fastest route since the editor already has Animation Composer.
- Motion Array [Smart Marker Animator](https://motionarray.com/premiere-pro-templates/smart-marker-animator-1369524/).
- Envato [pixelated transitions for Premiere](https://elements.envato.com/premium-transitions-pixelated-for-premiere-pro-EUMHVEW).

**Best tutorial:** DuckTUT, [Write-On Hand-Drawn Marker Effects (AE)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C02oqUde_L0). To make graphics stick to the board: Surfaced Studio, [Mocha AE for Absolute Beginners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgwFQDWOKgs). For pixelating in Premiere: Maxwell Ridgeway, [Pixel Effect in Premiere](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W2bHT-3D4nI).

**How to build it**
- **Premiere:** put Mosaic on each item with a tracked rectangle mask. Premiere 26 masks track up to 20x faster ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/01/20/rebranded-adobe-premiere-26-arrives-with-one-click-object-tracking/)). When she names an item, flash a quick glow, then shrink the Mosaic blocks over about 6 to 8 frames.
- **After Effects:** do one Mocha AE track of the board. Corner-pin a single precomp that holds the mosaics, the 1-2-3 badge pops and the marker circles, so everything sticks together. Make the circles with Trim Paths and a light Wiggle Paths.

**SFX:** a riser while the list is hidden, a glitch blip when it un-pixelates, a pop for each badge, a marker squeak on the circles, and an impact on each reveal.

### 6. Kinetic type and counters ("16 YEARS", "2:15:25")
**Examples**
- Iman Gadzhi's style: bold type and custom graphics on grid backgrounds ([breakdown](https://sendshort.ai/guides/iman-gadzhi-style/)).
- Behance [Kinetic Typography Experiments](https://www.behance.net/gallery/72905057/Kinetic-Typography-Experiments).
- Animation Composer 4 added free timers and callouts ([Mister Horse](https://misterhorse.com/what-s-new)).

**Best tutorial:** olafmotion, [Animate number counters in AE](https://olafmotion.com/tutorials/how-to-animate-number-counters-in-after-effects/) (2026). For "2:15:25", use gfxhacks' free [timer expression](https://gist.github.com/gfxhacks/058618bf728accb85c1c9895bdb3ab1c). It counts up in HH:MM:SS and stops at an end time.

**How to build it (AE, export as .mogrt)**
- "16" counts up quickly and slows into place, then "YEARS" snaps in with a scale punch.
- The race clock runs and then locks on 2:15:25 with a flash.
- Use fixed-width digits so the numbers don't jitter.
- AE 26 can keyframe the weight of variable fonts, for example going from thin to black as the number locks ([Newsshooter](https://www.newsshooter.com/2026/01/22/whats-new-in-adobe-after-effects-26-0/)).

**SFX:** rolling ticks, a stopwatch click when it locks, and a sub-bass hit.

### 7. Centred name title (vertical)
**Examples**
- Envato [Vertical Lower Thirds & Titles](https://elements.envato.com/vertical-lower-thirds-titles-575KGXU) (1080x1920, no plugins).
- Videohive [Vertical Lower Thirds](https://videohive.net/item/vertical-lower-thirds/60452077).
- Envato [Clean Minimal Lower Thirds](https://elements.envato.com/elegant-lower-third-titles-MKZG9A6).
- Elastic's Untold graphics (in section 1) for the documentary tone.

**Best tutorial:** ask.video, [Documentary-style lower thirds in AE](https://ask.video/article/video/designing-lower-thirds-in-after-effects-documentary-style).

**How to build it**
- "PAULA RADCLIFFE" in wide-spaced capitals, with a small descriptor line underneath.
- The text rises from behind a thin line (track matte), and the letter spacing tightens slightly over about 1 second.
- Put it in the upper third. Reels UI covers about 220 px at the top and 420 px at the bottom ([Ignite](https://www.ignitesocialmedia.com/content-creation/what-are-the-safe-zones-for-tiktoks-and-instagram-reels/)), and the captions sit lower, so they won't overlap. Or pause the captions for the 2 to 3 seconds the title is up.
- Use a different weight from the captions, and a soft gradient behind it rather than a box.

**SFX:** a soft whoosh and a low thud, kept quiet.
