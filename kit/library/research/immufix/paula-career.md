### 6. Licensing routes and red flags
| Source | Route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| London Marathon | London Marathon Events, prior written approval | Its media terms make accredited material editorial-only, with no commercial use or implied association. The race TV is BBC-made, so it needs clearing twice. |
| BBC (London races, Commonwealth, SPOTY, commentary) | BBC Motion Gallery, sold through Getty | Getty lists "Prior approval required for all uses" and "All BBC Sport footage requires further clearance". Expect a refusal for a supplement ad. |
| ITN, Trans World Sport, Getty Sport Video | Getty's Rights & Clearance team | All marked "Not released". |
| Getty, PA and Alamy stills | Editorial licences cannot be used in ads | Need a commercial licence, plus clearance for event marks, other athletes, Nike and race sponsors. |

**Other risks:**
- **Health claims:** don't imply Immufix contributed to her records or recovery; under the CAP Code that's an unauthorised health claim. immufix.com says she has used the formula since 1998; get that substantiated before any ad says it.
- **"I'm 52 now"** stops being true on 17 Dec 2026 (she was born 17 Dec 1973).
- **"17 years" vs "16 years":** both lines are correct. She held the world record from 13 Oct 2002 to 13 Oct 2019 (17 years); the 2:15:25 time itself stood 16 years. immufix.com says 16, so keep the scripts consistent.
- **Moments to avoid:** the 2004 Athens drop-out and the 2005 toilet stop.
- **Reputation:** in July 2024 she apologised for wishing convicted rapist Steven van de Velde "good luck" (Guardian, per Wikipedia). Expect some of this in ad comments.
- **Fan uploads:** YouTube fan videos can't be licensed from the uploader; the rights sit with the broadcaster or the event.
