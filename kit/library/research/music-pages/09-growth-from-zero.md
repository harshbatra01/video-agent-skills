# 09 Growth from zero: two new UK music news pages (8 Oct 2026)

[n] = source below. Inference = my reading. Excerpt = seen only in search results. file 01, file 08, raw/uk-* = local notes in this folder.

## Key findings
- **No follower gate.** Instagram shows a new reel to a small group it predicts will like it, then widens it for strong performers, judged on engagement, not follower count [2].
- **Ranking (Mosseri, 22 Jan 2025):** watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. Likes count slightly more for followers, sends for non-followers. Comments were not named. The "sends are 3 to 5x likes" claim has no source [6][7].
- **Originality rule, 30 Apr 2026.** The 2024 reel rules now cover photos and carousels. Accounts that mostly post unoriginal work leave recommendations; followers still see them. Recovery needs most posts in a rolling 30 days to be original (no threshold published). Account Status shows standing and appeals [1][5].
- **Original, for a news page.** Yes: your own photos, video, designs, and edits that clearly improve a source (context text, new-information graphics, remix, commentary, voiceover). No: border, watermark, subtitles, caption credit, speed change, screenshots of others' posts. Credit alone never counts. Instagram's test: "new information, context or a point of view not obvious from the original". News pages are not mentioned [1][5]. Inference: a headline repeating the clip fails; add a fact the clip lacks (chart position, date, earlier diss).
- **Trial Reels (10 Dec 2024).** Shown to non-followers first, hidden from grid and followers; numbers after about 24 hours, compared with earlier trials; optional auto-share within 72 hours. No hook advice from Instagram [3]. Eligibility is undefined (a 1,000-follower minimum is reported, unconfirmed [13]), so check for the toggle on day 1. Inference: at 0 followers every reel already reaches only non-followers, so use trials to A/B hooks.

## How the big pages grew (evidence only)
No founder interview found for UKHipHopDaily, GBMC, Daily Loud, Say Cheese or HipHopAllDay. Link Up TV's founder story is a YouTube pivot, not Instagram (excerpt) [14].
- **The Shade Room, Instagram-only, 2014 (excerpts).** About 10,000 followers in two weeks. Other sites' stories in her own voice, near-hourly updates, followers' DM tips steering coverage [10]. That model predates the 2026 rule.
- **GRM Daily, YouTube first, 2009.** Daily content: one 20-minute interview cut into four-minute posts, plus weekend freestyles. Posty hand-messaged fans of established artists' pages. Instagram 1.7M by Sept 2020, no Instagram tactics given [9].
- **Kurrco, X first, 2021 (Complex, Jul 2026).** Three people, one runs Instagram. Jokes start on X, then move to Instagram and TikTok. Turning point: a Kanye reshare. Biggest moment: first to confirm a release date. Posted then deleted one Grammy report [8]. Our count: X 442K, Instagram only 26.7K [file 01].
- **Our tracking, not interviews.** RapTV began as one page in 2016 (its own site); now 12.3M on Instagram inside a nine-brand network. GBMC (X 26K): about 5.7 posts a day, 1h34m behind the artist's own upload, TikTok mirror in about a minute, still 733K and 825K views on two beef posts; @UKHipHopDaily (4K) beat it on early stages [files 01, raw/uk-*].
- **Repeats:** near-daily output, one repeatable format, growth via other people's audiences (GRM's messaging, Kurrco's reshare). **Guesswork, no evidence:** best frequency, reply tactics, collab posts, cross-posting timing.

## Metrics and benchmarks
- **Skip rate (Instagram's definition, paraphrased):** share of views skipped within the first 3 seconds. Replaced view rate in Aug 2025 [4].
- **Retention:** which moments keep or lose viewers; a flatter line is better [4].
- **Per reach:** out of everyone who saw it [7]. Inference: a ratio, so small pages are not handicapped.
- Average watch time, follows, profile visits: in Insights, no official definition found.

No official benchmark exists for any of them. Unverified guides: skip rate 30 to 40% healthy, over about 50% weak. Metricool 2026 (excerpt): average reel watch time 8.5 seconds [11].

**Working targets (my guess; use your own medians after 20 posts):**
- Skip rate under 40% keep; over 55% rewrite the first 3 seconds.
- Average watch time at least half a 20 to 30 second reel; no retention cliff before 8 seconds.
- Sends, follows, profile visits per 1,000 reach: keep the top quartile of formats.
- Founder terms: AVD = average watch time; CTR has no organic twin, nearest is 100% minus skip rate.

## Mistakes to avoid
- **Repost-only content** (logo, subtitles, credit, screenshots). Inference: at 0 followers, losing recommendations removes the only growth route [1][5].
- **Engagement bait.** Instagram won't recommend posts explicitly asking for comments, tags or a set word (5 Jun 2024) [12]. Our sample: Daily Loud's question reel 31 comments, RapTV's no-question reel 910. Scale and story beat the prompt [file 08].
- **Slow or wrong news.** Unverified: same-day uploads are over 30% of recommended Reels (Meta Q1 2026 call). Kurrco posted then deleted a report [8]; GBMC posts single-source claims as exclusive. Confirm first.
- **Drifting niche, or one asset on both pages.** Instagram matches reels to similar-interest viewers [2]; inference: mixed topics blur that, duplicates read as reposts. @zonegbp UK rap only; @beatswitchfm rap and music, UK-first.
- **Volume, one-account risk.** Instagram says only to post regularly [2]; peers post 3 to 5 a day [file 08]. No source shows a volume penalty, so the plan starts at 2 a day per page. No Jumper's Instagram was removed; Say Cheese lists a backup page [file 01].

## 30-day launch plan for both pages (proposal)
Every post: one added fact the source lacks, our own words, headline in frame 1, our own tweet card (never a screenshot), no "comment X" orders. Priority: UK drop or beef update, then chart slots (Official Charts Friday, midweeks Sunday to Tuesday), then calendar posts built a day ahead.
- **Week 1, baseline:** set up public professional accounts with 6 launch posts each; look for the Trial toggle. Then per page per day, 1 news reel (15 to 30 s) and 1 card. Post 6 to 8pm UK, news once verified. Log skip rate, retention drop point, average watch time, sends, follows, profile visits per 1,000 reach at 24 and 72 hours.
- **Week 2, hooks:** two openings for each day's best story, changing only the first 3 seconds (trial reels, or alternate days if no toggle). Drop the weakest format. Add one sendable carousel (beef timeline, chart recap).
- **Week 3, double down:** 2 reels and 1 card a day only if skip rate stays under your median. Tag artists only when the post is about them; invite one small UK artist to a Collab post. Start a daily Story poll (followers only); no this-or-that in the feed yet.
- **Week 4, decide:** keep formats above median on sends and follows per reach; cut any with three reels in a row above median skip rate. First Sunday ranking carousel. Check Account Status on both pages. Set targets from your own medians.
