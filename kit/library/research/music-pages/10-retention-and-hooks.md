# 10 Retention and hooks (8 Oct 2026)

[n] = Sources. [O] = our notes in this folder. [I] = my inference, untested. Secondhand = seen only in search excerpts. SMT = Social Media Today.

## Key findings
- Skip rate is the number to beat: the share of views that skip in the first 3 seconds, beside a Retention chart (Aug 2025) [1]. The founder's YouTube terms map to Average watch time (AVD) and the Retention chart; Reels have no CTR, nearest is skip rate [I].
- Instagram gives no "good" cutoff or watch-percentage average [15], and I found no Mosseri line on skip rate. His top signals (Jan 2025): watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach [2].
- Shorter is not proven to win: brand-page reach rate is 5.2% under 30 s vs 5.6% at 30 to 60 s [3]; typical views are 4,700 vs 10,374 at 45 to 60 s [4]. Test 8 to 12 s against 20 to 30 s.
- No primary study ranks faces, text or motion in a first frame. Secondhand Meta advice: people early, text for muted viewers, yet audio is on for over 80% of Reels plays [6].
- Carousels edge Reels on engagement; Reels win reach on small pages [3][7].
- Us-vs-them content drives shares (best study is political, so rap is my inference); ask it as a question. A fake number risks credibility far more than Meta penalties [9][11][12].
- Covers: no evidence they change follows [14].

## Hooks that work for music news
No test ranks hook types for music news. These recur on UK pages [O].
1. Conflict with a clock: "Digga D answers Morrisson, reply set for Thursday 7pm" (GBMC, 2 Oct; read as 8 Oct, unverified). Two GBMC beef posts reached 733K and 825K views on X by 7 Oct.
2. "X just did Y", with a date: "Skepta confirms 'The Fork and Knife' for 6 Nov" (GRM).
3. Surprising number: "'Raindance' (Dave & Tems) is #1 on the Official Hip Hop and R&B Singles Chart, 49 weeks" (our 7 Oct pull; confirm what 49 counts).
4. Curiosity gap, paid off by second 6: "Who is #1 on Shazam UK hip-hop?" then 'Raindance' (Shazam GB, 7 Oct). A tease answered late probably raises skip rate [I].
5. Quote first: open on the line, then the name (GRM: Stormzy, Headie One).

## Reel structure (7 to 15 s)
| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| 0.0 s | Headline fully on screen, no fade-in: 6 words or fewer, white on a dark plate, in the middle zone. Sound starts on the hook. No logo intro. |
| 0 to 3 s | Something moves in the first second [I]; artist's face large; headline stays. This is the skip window [1]. |
| 3 to 7 s | The fact (who, what, when) plus one added number, date or chart position, so it counts as original [08]. |
| 7 to 12 s | Proof: video peak or interview line. Captions only on speech, below the plate. |
| Last 2 s | Date teaser or open question, never "comment X". End on a frame like frame 0 so it loops. No outro card. |

- Text time: Netflix caps reading at 20 characters a second [5]; over video use 15: seconds = characters / 15, minimum 1.5 [I].
- Middle zone: the grid is 3:4 since Jan 2025 [13], so it crops 240 px off top and bottom of a 1080x1920 file (my arithmetic), and app buttons cover more. Keep headline and face about 15% to 65% down [I].
- Loops: I found no official word on how replays count. After 24 h read the Retention chart: a cliff in 0 to 3 s, rewrite frame 0; mid-reel, cut [I].
- Cover: use frame 0 for one consistent grid look; pin 3 explainer posts. No benchmark for new pages [14], so compare follows per profile visit over 3 to 4 weeks after any change [I].

## Carousels
- Slide 1 works like frame 0: claim or number, a face, readable on the 3:4 crop.
- No 2026 swipe-per-slide data found. Old Socialinsider data (secondhand, about 2020): 10-slide posts led engagement, dipping after slide 3 [8]. Use 2 to 3 slides for news, 5 to 8 for rankings and VS [I].
- One fact per slide. Make the last slide worth sending (ranking, chart): sends matter most for non-followers [2][I].

## Polarising without bait, and honest stat hooks
- Evidence: in 2.7M news and Congress posts, each out-group word raised share odds about 67% [9]. That is politics; rap beef is my inference. In our 7 Oct sample, page size beat prompts: a question-ending Daily Loud reel got 31 comments, RapTV's no-question one 910 [08].
- Frame: two named sides, one fact, a clock, an open question ("Who won the week?"). Instagram will not recommend explicit asks like a set word or emoji [10]. No live-case or "allegedly" claims without BBC, Sky or police [O].
- Honest versions of "25% think Dave is better":
  1. Our Story poll with the count: "[X]% of [N] voters on our Story picked [A]". Poll Tuesday, result Friday; say "our followers, not a survey".
  2. Public numbers side by side: chart peak, weeks on chart, first-week units (Official Charts); Spotify listeners with the date.
  3. Our record: "We called [X] of our last [N] chart moves."
  4. Taste labelled as ours: "Our take: A. The numbers say B."
- Policy (the page is framed around Facebook and names Instagram only for Community Notes): Meta removes only harm categories (violence, harmful health claims, voter interference). Other falsehoods get less reach, rated by fact-checkers outside the US; repeat sharers risk reduced distribution or removal [11]. Community Notes are US-only plus a hidden Latin American test; none for the UK found [12]. Meta will probably not catch a fake poll; screenshots will, and one exposed fake costs a news page more than the comments earn [I].

## Benchmarks
- Skip rate by followers, Jan to Jun 2026: 65.5% (1 to 5K), 63.4% (10 to 50K), 60.5% (100K to 1M) [3]. Vendor "healthy" lines (under 30 to 40%) cite unnamed trackers [15].
- At 1 to 5K followers, reach rate: Reels 9.78%, carousels 8.80%, images 7.00% [3]; engagement Q2 2026: carousels 0.50%, Reels 0.48%, images 0.33% [7].
- Average watch time per Reel: about 8.5 s in 2026, 4 s in 2025 (Metricool, secondhand) [16].
- Profile visit to follow: about 13.5% in a typical month, all accounts, data ends Jan 2025 [14].
- Starter target [I]: skip under 60% by week 4, then beat our own 4-week median.
