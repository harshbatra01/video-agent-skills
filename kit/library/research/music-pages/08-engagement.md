# 08 Engagement: growing the pages without looking like spam (8 Oct 2026)

[n] = Sources. [O] = our 7 Oct page notes. [I] = my inference. Excerpt = seen only in search results.

## Platform signals
- Mosseri's top three ranking signals are watch time, likes and sends. Likes count slightly more for followers, sends slightly more for non-followers. Comments and saves were not named [2]. So for non-follower reach, aim for posts people send to a mate, not comment counts [I].
- Originality rule, 30 Apr 2026: accounts that mostly post unoriginal photos, carousels or reels lose recommendation spots (followers still see them). Original means you made it or materially edited it: on-image text adding context beyond describing what happens, graphics adding new information, or remix. A border, watermark, subtitles or caption credit do not count. Recovery needs most posts in a rolling 30 days to be original; check Account Status for limits and appeals [1]. For us: every card and clip needs one added fact (stat, date, chart position) [I].
- Hashtags: capped at 5 since Dec 2025; Mosseri says they add context, not reach [7]. Metricool's 2026 data (24.4M posts) links hashtags to about 32% fewer views, correlation only [8].
- Carousels lead engagement and saves, Reels lead reach [8][9][13]. Posts with a question drew 36.7% more comments (general data) [8].
- TikTok (thin; its pages are blocked from India): official signals are interactions, video details (captions, sounds, hashtags) and device settings. Watch time as top signal is third-party opinion [10].

## Avoid list
- Orders to engage: "comment YES", "tag a friend", "like if", "share with 3 mates", "like for A, comment for B", like-follow-tag giveaways. Instagram (5 Jun 2024) will not recommend content that "explicitly asks for engagement through shares, comments, tags, or other actions", for example a set word, number or emoji. Open-ended questions are fine. It will not list penalised wording [3].
- Meta's engagement bait page covers Facebook only [4]; Instagram's guidelines list engagement bait and giveaways as content it may not recommend [5]. Penalty: less reach, not removal.
- "Rate it 1-10" and "W or L" sit close to that example: ask as questions, never orders, and vary the wording [I]. Comment CTAs drew 203% more comments, but comments are not reach [8].
- Re-uploads with only a logo, border, subtitles or credit; headlines that repeat the clip [1]; screenshots of other accounts (Tubefilter's reading, not on Instagram's page) [11]; other-app watermarks [12]; over 5 hashtags [7].

## Caption formulas
Fact first, prompt last, 1 to 2 emoji, 0 to 3 hashtags. Questions, never orders. [Brackets] are fills.
1. Anniversary: "[N] years ago today Skepta dropped 'Konnichiwa' 💿 Which track still goes hardest?"
2. Chart call: "'[Track]' is #[N] on the midweeks 📈 Number one by Friday, yeah or nah?"
3. Release: "[Artist] x [Artist], '[Track]', is out now 🚨 First listen: banger or skip?"
4. W or L: "[Artist] just announced [move]. Was that a W or an L?"
5. Head to head: "[A] and [B] both dropped Friday. Who ate the week?"
6. Score it: "[Artist]'s '[Track]' video is live 🎬 What score would you give it?"
7. This or that: "Grime or drill: which UK sound travelled further?"
8. Ranking: "Top 5 UK rap albums of the decade so far. Ours are in the slides, who did we miss?"
9. Hot take: "[Stat]. Our take: [mild opinion]. Are we wrong?"
10. Quote: "[Artist] on [topic]: '[short quote]' Does he have a point?"
11. Finish the bar: "[Artist], '[Track]'. Opening line: '[line]...' Finish it 🎤"
12. Who's next: "[Artist] passed [N] monthly listeners from [city] 📈 Who's the next UK name to blow?"

## Engagement posts worth making (ranked)
1. Ranking carousel (Top 5, power ranking): carousels lead engagement and saves; lists are easy to send [I].
2. This or that carousel, Story poll result as the last slide.
3. Anniversary card, "best track?": XXL got 132 comments in an hour [O]; the calendar fills it.
4. Chart call Tue or Wed, "we called it" card Friday: a reason to return [I].
5. Hot take or "W or L" image: most comments; keep to music, not private lives.
6. Daily Story poll: followers only, free research for 1 and 2.

## Suggested mix and UK times
- Per page per day: 4 to 5 feed posts (UK peers 3 to 5 [O]): one engagement-first, the rest news with a soft question, plus 2 to 3 Stories with a poll. Engagement posts are fully ours, so they protect the 30-day original share [I]. No proven best frequency; Sprout says timing beats volume [6].
- Weekly: ranking Sunday evening, this or that midweek, chart call Tue or Wed (result Friday). Pin a comment with our own answer (common, unproven).
- UK times, Sprout Mar 2026 (21.6M UK engagements): Mon to Thu 5 to 9pm, Fri 5 to 6pm, Sat 3 to 4pm and 5 to 7pm, Sun 6 to 7pm; best day Sunday. Labelled GMT; BST ends 25 Oct, so test an hour either way [6]. A scheduler vendor adds 12 to 1pm [14]. Plan: news once verified; engagement posts 6 to 8pm (test 12:30pm); Friday chart card 5:45pm, after the Official Charts reveal. Review after 4 weeks: watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach.

## Evidenced or just common
- Evidenced: sends, likes, watch time [2]; originality rule [1]; carousel lead [9]; questions bring more comments, a correlation [8]. Common, no data found: W or L, who won, rate it, finish the bar, this or that, pinned comments.
- Our sample [O]: Daily Loud always asks a question, yet a sampled reel had 31 comments; RapTV's sign-off (no question) had 910; Say Cheese's one line plus 👀 had 354. Page scale and story look bigger than the prompt.
