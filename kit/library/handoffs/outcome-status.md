Wanted: viral, instantly clear formats from what prediction-market brands and UGC creators actually post, translated to pages with no creator on camera; borrowed footage (clips, movie and aura edits, lifestyle and supercars) is fine.
Round 3 research (5 agents, about 400 posts checked as frames): `23-pm-brands-and-clipping.md`, `24-ugc-to-faceless.md`, `25-borrowed-footage-formats.md`, `26-native-look-and-brand.md` (includes Outcome's public brand guide at brand.outcome.xyz: Signal Green #1AFF57, "Trade the outcome.", bans bet / wager / gambling), `27-odds-meme-pages.md`.
Core finding: the number is the punchline or the stakes, never the product. Start from a real moment (clip, reaction face, the real app screen), one TikTok-style caption of 4 to 9 words, the chance as a short insert. Most posts carry no number. Benchmarks: PolymarketFC (1.21B views a year, about 10 posts a day), kalshi.sports (494K to 934K views on 7.5K followers).
