## The bio register, and why it matters

This went wrong once and the correction is the single most useful note in this document.

**Rejected:** poetic, atmospheric, observational bios. The example that failed was along the lines of "the 30 seconds before the walk and the 10 after the finish". It reads as written-to-be-admired, and the partner's response was that it sounded AI generated. He was right.

**What real bios in these niches actually look like:**

```
📷|Pictures,Videos,News
🥊|Everything about boxing
🔥|Daily uploads
```

Blunt. Emoji bulleted. Noun phrases, not sentences. No voice, no observation, no craft on display. The bio's job is to say what the page posts and how often, and then stop.

Within an 80 character TikTok limit there is barely room for anything else anyway.

## Naming: the thing that was actually figured out

The question "should the name sound human or corporate" was the wrong question. **The real tell is swappability.**

- **Pop Base**, **Culture Crave**, **Film Updates** are all `[topic] + [suffix]`. You could swap any suffix for any other and nothing changes. They read corporate.
- **Kurrco** (whose founder said on record that it "has no particular meaning"), **No Jumper**, **2Cool2Blog** are not swappable. They read human.

So the strategy that was settled on:
- **Football, fight and military** pages use topic brands, which is the convention in those niches, and get their human signal from the **bio**.
- **Meme and rap** pages use absurd or person-shaped names, because that is the convention there.

### An open lane that was identified and not yet used

Across a search of US, UK, Australian, Indian, Brazilian and Nigerian meme pages, there are **essentially no examples** of meme page names shaped as:

- fake job titles
- fake businesses
- apologies
- terms and conditions language
- system errors or notifications

That is a genuinely empty naming lane. `warmdietcoke_` is adjacent to it but does not occupy it. If more pages get spun up, this is the direction with no competition.

## Profile pictures

Design at 1080 x 1080. Renders as small as **110px**, circular crop, so keep content in the **middle 60 to 70 percent**.

**What was rejected:** public domain classical art and medieval marginalia. His exact response was that nobody keeps that as a profile picture, and he was right. The register was completely wrong for these niches.

**What landed:** actual currently circulating meme images. Jimothy the raccoon, tornado squirrel, nihilistic penguin, Chill Guy, Hide the Pain Harold, each with a direct image link so he could grab them immediately.

**The rule:** a profile picture in these niches is a current meme or a clean topic mark. It is never a piece of art that needs explaining.

One thing was declined on practical grounds rather than taste: a Charlie Kirk based image. The reasoning given was that it is an assassination meme, the page would get mass reported, and it ties a client's commercial campaign to a political killing. That is a reach and reputation argument, not a legal one.

## Branding rules that apply across all five pages

- **Never hint that a song is being promoted.** The entire premise depends on the audience not knowing. Any copy that gestures at it breaks the page.
- **No cross promotion between the pages.** No shared links, no following each other, no shared signature in the bios. See the multi-account risk section in `01-platform-rules.md`.
- **No shared visual signature.** Three pages that look like one operator look like a network.
- **Verify any scene term before using it.** Invented jargon has been caught twice. `93rdminute` was presented as established football language and is not. `limbs` and `tekkers` are named by FourFourTwo itself as overused social media clichés. Check that a phrase is real and not already tired before it goes on a page.
