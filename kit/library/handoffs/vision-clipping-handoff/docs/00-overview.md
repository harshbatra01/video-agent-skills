# The business

## The model

A music artist signs on. Vision Clipping then posts that artist's track as the **audio layer** behind large volumes of unrelated niche content across accounts it owns. Football edits, fight edits, military edits, meme posts, rap news. The audience follows the niche. The track rides underneath.

This works because of one platform mechanic: **every track has a sound page.** On TikTok, tapping the audio on any video takes you to a page listing every video using that sound. Volume concentrated on one sound page in a short window is what moves a track. The same logic applies loosely to Instagram Reels audio and less so to YouTube Shorts.

So the deliverable is not "good edits". It is **a lot of posts, all on one sound, in a tight window.**

## The sold shape

**3 pages x 3 posts a day x 30 days = 270 posts a month behind one track.**

That number is the pitch. It is also the operational constraint that everything else has to survive: 270 posts a month means roughly 9 finished vertical videos a day across three accounts, every day, without the accounts getting flagged as a coordinated network. See `01-platform-rules.md`.

## Why niche pages and not music pages

A page that posts music content attracts an audience that already knows it is being marketed to, and the ceiling on that audience is small. A football page attracts football fans, who are numerous, who do not know a campaign is running, and who will watch the same clip format for months. The niche is the acquisition channel. The sound is the payload.

This also means the pages have to be **genuinely good at their niche**, not thin wrappers. A football page that posts bad football content gets no reach, and a sound with no reach is worth nothing to the client.

## The five verticals chosen, and why

| Vertical | Why it was picked |
|---|---|
| **Football** | Highest volume of free, constantly refreshing footage, and the largest global audience. Scene packs made this the easiest to produce at scale. |
| **Fight** (UFC, boxing) | Promotions give away enormous amounts of broadcast quality footage free. Press conferences and weigh ins are clean and unused. |
| **Military** | Aura farming. Visually spectacular, and DVIDS gives US government footage away. Note: this page is explicitly **not** about homecomings or reunions. The partner rejected that angle. |
| **Meme** | Lowest production cost per post, highest shareability. Also the lane where the name itself can carry the page. |
| **Rap** | The news and reaction lane. Closest to the music, so it has to be handled carefully, but it is also where the artist's own moments can be posted legitimately. |

Basketball was added later as a sixth. See `03-sourcing.md`.

## The one risk that was never resolved

270 posts a month across three accounts, all pushing the same commercial track, is structurally close to what TikTok's own policy describes as a banned pattern. The mitigations identified are in `01-platform-rules.md`, but no one has tested whether they hold at this volume. Treat the first campaign as the test, and instrument it so a ban is detectable early rather than discovered at month end.
