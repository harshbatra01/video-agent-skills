## Read this first: coverage and honesty notes

- Headline: of the edits I could actually verify, none is clearly denser than our Mbappe reference in raw micro-effects (flashes, strobes, mirror edges, warps). What DOES beat the reference in this lane: typography, designed "card" scenes, quote-driven structure, and concept. The densest After Effects effect packs live on TikTok, which this network cannot open (tiktok.com refuses connections). Entries 33-40 are TikTok leads taken from search-result summaries only and are marked (unverified).
- How I checked: YouTube numbers are yt-dlp metadata read on 2026-10-13. Instagram likes/dates/captions are yt-dlp metadata (works without login for reels); Instagram "plays" are read from public profile/tag grids. Visuals: for 23 entries I scrubbed the video player in a browser tab and read a 12-frame contact sheet (nothing downloaded); 8 entries are "Thumbs only" (YouTube auto-frames) and 9 are "Not viewed". Each entry says which.
- Timestamps are approximate (frames were sampled every 2-6 s), so read them as "around".
- The shared WebSearch budget ran out mid-run, so I did no Reddit or Instagram "best editors" searches. Instagram public pages also started throttling late in the run.
- Tiers used in titles: [A] craft worth copying, [B] one useful idea or a reach example, [P] pro/studio piece, [U] unverified TikTok lead.
- Team tip 1: `python -I -m yt_dlp` fails on this PC (the module sits in user site-packages, which -I hides). I used a small wrapper that appends that path and then runs yt_dlp. Tip 2: yt-dlp also returns likes/date/caption for Instagram reel URLs with no login.
- Team tip 3 (TikTok is unreachable from this PC, so this matters): YouTube channels that re-upload TikTok/Reels NBA and NFL edits in bulk are listed in the creators table (BigBaller8, Strong-side, TouchDown). They are the easiest way to browse TikTok-origin edits.

## 4. Patterns noticed

- Structure beats effect count. The best YouTube NBA edits open with 20-50 s of spoken soundbites (interviews, podcasts, speeches) with colour-keyword captions, then hit the song drop and the highlights (rxmziy, R4VE, P3DITS, Sleepy_Am, Lion Trees, EK). A black-frame "breath" often marks the drop (R4VE at about 16 s).
- Typography is the main 2025 differentiator: giant lyric words per beat with radial zoom blur and glow (editbyag_, 1.5M plays), serif/handwriting words on black (6ixEd1tz), red serif over footage (Bo Nix), tiny lyric captions plus a brand mark (spoonsfx).
- Designed "card" scenes between footage runs add variety without more effects: article page with highlight, newspaper front page, neon sign, typed Wikipedia-style bio, photo-cutout card with signature (EK, Sleepy_Am). Each lasts 2-4 s.
- Light and glow are accents, not constants: one neon outline (hoop), purple eye glow, golden bokeh, particle title (Zweng, EK). The dark "villain aura" grade with VHS/RGB glitch is the other common look (Clutch Time Clips).
- Pop-culture crossovers drive engagement: LeBron x Omni-Man (Invincible) tags, NFL-to-NBA match-cut edits, GOAT-vs-GOAT pairings.
- Reach vs craft: the biggest numbers in this lane (65M, 21M, 11M, 5M) are concept Shorts (costumed streetball, animated VS jump battles, photo-snap compilations), not effect-dense edits, which cap around 0.2-2.6M. They are not music-driven, so they are format ideas, not sync references.
- Retention bait works for tiny accounts: "Watch till end" with an unrelated hook (Bo Nix reel, 908 followers, about 494K plays), tweet/comment cards as first-frame hooks (hockey, baseball), "VS" rounds with a twist.
- A preset economy exists. Editors sell presets/project files (Payhip: P3DITS, rxmziy, R4VE, Gled, Itai Rosen; Patreon: aiyuk), give free packs (Zweng, 687K views) and credit scene packs ("scp - twizzylogoless", "c/blisc on tt", "(handle/tt)"). The effects are copyable; the edge is taste, type and structure.
- Songs: Travis Scott dominates (SDP Interlude, FE!N, Highest in the Room); also Kendrick "Not Like Us", Yeat, Lil Uzi Vert "Just Wanna Rock", a sped-up YNW Melly, Drake, slowed/reverb tracks. Songs with a clear drop and a quotable lyric work best for lyric-led edits.
- Format: 15-35 s on Instagram, 50-90 s on YouTube; widescreen 16:9 still gets 400K+ likes on Instagram (editbyag_), but we should deliver 9:16.
- Studio ceiling is 3D/CG and big lockups (NBA "Gift of Game" toy-figure diorama, Eagles hype "LET'S HUNT"), which fan editors rarely reach; a 3D asset hook shot is the cheapest way to look "above" the usual edit.
- Gap to fill next: the dense effect-pack edits (glow strobes, mirror edges, 3D camera, roto cut-outs) live on TikTok. The strongest leads are @gunslinger.ae (3D NBA, presets), @TylerCreator.AE, @nsggc.edits, @mmaze.vfx (NFL), @jokerszn, plus the YouTube bulk compilations (BigBaller8, Strong-side, TouchDown) which re-upload TikTok edits and can be watched without a TikTok account.
