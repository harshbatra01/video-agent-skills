# 03 Combat sports, F1 / motorsport, other sports: edits research

Lane: UFC/MMA, boxing, F1/MotoGP, cricket, tennis, athletics, gym, skate/extreme. Researched 13 Oct 2026.

## Method and caveats (read first)
- Metadata (views, likes, dates, subs, descriptions) came from yt-dlp, run from a scratch folder. `python -I` hides the user-site yt_dlp module, so I added its site-packages path inside a helper script. Worth installing yt-dlp for the whole Python if you want `-I` to work as written.
- No video was downloaded. For a visual check I viewed YouTube's own thumbnail and storyboard images (one frame about every 1 s, finer on very short clips). "Checked: frames" below means that. "Checked: metadata only" means I did not see frames.
- TikTok and Instagram cannot be opened from this network (connection blocked), and the shared WebSearch budget ran out mid-task. So the TikTok section is leads only, marked unverified. No Instagram reel URLs were found. Instagram handles below come from links creators put in their YouTube descriptions.
- Honest read of the field: on YouTube, the edits that are clearly beyond our Mbappe reference (typography, mograph, VFX, compositing) are mostly small channels with low views. The 2-28M view Shorts in combat and cricket in 2026 are simpler (captions, aura grade, callouts) and win on hook and format. Tier tags in the titles: (A) clearly beyond reference in design or VFX, (B) viral 2026 formats, simpler than reference but proven hooks, (C) older classics still worth knowing, (D) TikTok leads, unverified.
- Many of the best pieces are not 9:16. Frame sizes are noted per entry (4:3, 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9 are common), which matters for how we would build vertical versions.
- Dates are upload dates. View counts are as fetched on 13 Oct 2026. "likes hidden" means the uploader hides them.
- MotoGP, gym, skate and ski: nothing standout beyond the official MotoGP titles (entry 17) and the entries below. MotoGP edit channels on YouTube are mostly meme-clip pages (top Short on MGPEDIT is 1.7M views, not a crafted edit).

# 4. Patterns noticed

1. Craft and reach are split. The most inventive F1 pieces (lanzdoesvisual, ProdBlend, Lingos, myst1c., 16chazlec) have under 3K views; the 2 to 28M view combat and cricket Shorts in 2026 are caption, aura and callout formats. Copy the design craft for polish and the format for the hook.
2. Song words drive the picture. The strongest F1 edits turn lyrics into on-screen type placed inside the scene and coloured in team livery (Bela Films: lime/teal for Hamilton, orange for Max, red for Leclerc). This is the direct lyric-text route for a client song.
3. One recurring prop is the edit's spine: the five-light start grid (DNA about 14, 17, 20 s; REINCARNATED about 8 s) and an analog clock (Leclerc about 3, 9, 10, 22 s). It acts as a visual metronome and ties the structure to the track.
4. Flash cadence is faster than our reference. "4 x 4" changes colour or graphic frame every 2 to 3 video frames, and solid white, orange or red frames replace dip-to-white as transitions.
5. The song card and other interface graphics are used as motifs, not only as an end card: a fake player bar with scrub and pause icons mid-edit (mileage, ProdBlend), a game character-select screen (fray), a numbered countdown (Wild Smile). These suit song facts (streams, BPM, features).
6. Cold open on type. F1 LIGHT holds about 15 s of small text on black before the first shot; isarel builds a spoken argument in type first. It makes the drop hit harder; pair with build-to-drop.
7. Single-look discipline. heavensent keeps one crushed black and white look for 142 s; Bela's Leclerc keeps one red grade; ZYZI keeps one cold grade and ends on a black and white echo. One extreme look beats mixed grades.
8. Fight VFX payoff on the hit. RAY ROD's freeze and shatter (29M views, 2020) and Shazam lightning (1.05M) put a physical effect on the knockout frame. Use a shatter, burst or flash on the beat drop.
