# 05 Captions and typography in edits (research bank)

Lane: captions, kinetic type, text behind the player, 3D text, title and name cards, song cards, caption trends.
Written 2026-10-13. Views and likes are YouTube numbers read with yt-dlp on that day. Dates are upload dates.

How this was gathered (read this before trusting a line):
- Metadata only came from yt-dlp (title, uploader, views, likes, date, description). No video was downloaded.
- To see the type itself I looked at YouTube's public preview stills (the auto frames at roughly 25 / 50 / 75 percent of each video). Where a line says "(~50%)" that is what the still showed, not a timestamp. The very first and last frames are not available this way, so hook-in-the-first-second and end-card claims are not verified.
- TikTok could not be opened from this network. Instagram public profile pages opened only as far as follower counts and post dates (no captions, no view counts, no video), so the sports-edit scene on TikTok and Instagram is under-represented. TikTok pages that appeared in search results are listed in section 3 as leads only.
- The WebSearch budget ran out mid-run. Everything after that came from direct page fetches and yt-dlp channel and video lookups.
- Lyric words seen in frames are not quoted (copyright). Entries describe the typography, not the words.
- "(unverified)" marks anything I inferred.
- NEW tags used (nothing in the brief list fit): NEW: title-card, NEW: keyword-highlight, NEW: type-on-prop, NEW: type-grid.

## 4. Patterns noticed

1. Type is the set, not a subtitle. The strongest pieces build the whole frame from type (tiled rows, giant cropped letters, stacked echo copies, starbursts): venushoyo, y6richo, COMPASS, poly, k3nny_am. The sports edits I checked keep type small in the lower third. A type-led composition is the clearest upgrade lane for a sports edit.
2. Oversized name or number BEHIND the subject is the 2024 to 2026 premium signature in sports: F1 titles 2025 and 2026, BallerFX 2024, EK's stat numeral, the 6ixEd1tz and kma4 serif banners, soold's stencil block letters, Shadey's hook word. It needs a clean cutout wherever the type shows, and the letters are cropped by the frame edge to imply scale.
3. One word (or one syllable) per beat at giant scale with a field flip. Charli xcx's Von dutch video does one ultra-condensed word per cut, alternating black and white fields; Folcha cloned it with flat colour bands; SpoodermanEditz and Shadey stack or repeat the hook word, and kma4 swaps the typeface (serif caps, serif italic, heavy sans) on each repeat of the same hook while keeping the placement behind the player. The word acts as the beat marker.
4. Type treated as a physical object or place: Sabrina Carpenter's diner receipts, BossMan Dlow's wristbands, XG's chrome letters in a CG city, TFBAGAS's gold letters in a room, Billie Eilish's tone-on-tone caption in the artwork scene. For sports: ticket stub, scoreboard slab, locker nameplate, programme page.
5. Palette discipline. Two or three colours plus one accent per piece (red/black/white: venushoyo, poly, Tarot, Timeless; green/black/white: y6richo, k3nny_am, Kirriko; plum/cream: JENNIE). Sports edits mostly get colour only from the footage grade while text stays white, so a club-colour accent is cheap differentiation.
6. Keyword highlight inside quote and lyric captions: one word flips to blue, yellow, red or pink (Hero Edit, Lion Trees, reon, BossMan Dlow). Wide-tracked small caps with a soft under-glow for quiet lines; bold italic with glow for lyric lines (Shadey).
7. 3D type is a named genre ("[3D Typography Edit]") with real lighting: bevel, chrome or gold, glints, bokeh, perspective tumbling (iluxhaep, frooky, lvc, MontagEdits, TFBAGAS, VINCHESTER's orbit text). Project files are sold or free (iluxhaep, lvc, TFBAGAS, y6richo xml), so the barrier is low. Tools seen: After Effects (most), Alight Motion (y6richo, k3nny_am, naterhythm, Bizarre Edits), DaVinci Resolve 20 (frooky), Blender for the 3D in iluxhaep's Ronaldo edit.
8. Name and stat cards are tiny lockups: first name small, SURNAME huge and condensed, a thin ornament rule, a role and place line (BallerFX); shirt number as a big outlined numeral plus name with glitch (F1 2024); record stat as a huge translucent numeral behind the player (EK); jersey-chest name tags in team colours (Hero Edit Saka).
9. Song card ideas that are not just an outro: a Spotify-style strip with the green liked check inside the layout (Kirriko), a waveform title panel (iluxhaep), the 2026 Spotify picture trend (tilted card behind a cutout, Picsart), and the 2023 Spotify-card lyric style (Hyaxintth, Technical Sohail tutorials).
10. Format cloning moves fast: "Timeless" mograph copied from @ainssshibal by several creators, the "Stereo Heart" typography template (Spissi 2022) becoming a football version (VINCHESTER), "@slicx7 like" tutorials, a 3D-typography series. Layout-driven mograph and 3D typography has mostly not reached sports edits: the sports edits that do use type lean on subtitles, name tags and text-behind-subject, so a full mograph-style sports piece is still an open lane.
11. Like-to-view ratios in this sample: short typography and mograph pieces mostly sit at 3.4 to 7.2 percent (poly 7.2, lvc 7.2, iluxhaep 6.1, frooky 6.0, Keo 5.7, Bizarre 5.7, venushoyo 5.4, y6richo 3.8) while the long ones are lower (COMPASS 1.3, Nightowlaep 2.0). Most sports edits sit at about 1.5 to 3 percent (Hero Edit Mbappe 2.7, Shadey 2.4 to 3.3, EK 2.3, 6ixEd1tz 2.0, kma4 1.5), with exceptions (Hero Edit Ronaldo 5.8, vrveffect 6.0). Small sample and different audiences, so treat it as a hint only.
12. Caption trends for speech-style captions (vendor blogs, unsourced numbers): word-by-word karaoke is the baseline; the Hormozi look (Montserrat Black or Proxima Nova Bold, white caps, keyword #f7c204, emoji between lines) is called saturated; "Dynamic Minimalism" (white bold, black outline, 2 to 3 words, subtle scale or fade, no emoji) and coloured pill chips are rising; inline emoji are declining while standalone emoji stickers remain common; butter yellow #F5D76E and a single red accent are rising for lifestyle and fitness. Specs quoted: Montserrat white with a 5 to 8 px black outline at 1080x1920, Poppins with a 1 to 2 px shadow, Proxima Nova with a 2 to 3 px outline; 3 to 7 words per chunk (OpusClip) or 2 to 3 words per beat (BlitzCut); keep text out of the bottom 25 percent and top 15 percent (OpusClip). TikTok Sans (Grilli Type origin, open-sourced, on Google Fonts) is now a free platform-native option. No emoji appeared in any sampled sports or lyric frame.

The 10 most-used font styles seen (classes, with named fonts where a source named them)
1. Ultra-condensed heavy grotesque caps (Impact, Bebas Neue and Anton class; Druk Condensed look): Charli xcx lyric video, Folcha; BlitzCut lists Bebas Neue, Anton, Impact for sports and hype.
2. Heavy extended or wide italic caps (Integral CF, Druk Wide, Monument Extended class): F1 titles, Shadey, COMPASS, BossMan Dlow. The TikTok summaries named Integral, Drew Wide and Monument Extended (unverified).
3. Bold geometric sans for captions (Montserrat Black or Bold, Poppins): the speech-caption default per BlitzCut; Lion Trees and Hero Edit style small caps are close to this.
4. Plain grotesque, often lowercase (Arial or Helvetica class; Inter): venushoyo, stcubing, poly, Bizarre Edits. The brat identity is Arial-based and stretched (The Nightly); voengi's text-effects tutorial names Arial regular and bold.
5. High-contrast serif or Didone display, caps or italic lowercase: JENNIE, k3nny_am, 6ixEd1tz, reon (Playfair, DM Serif Display and EB Garamond class per BlitzCut's minimal styles).
6. Slab serif and distressed woodtype: Nightowlaep, Keo, Sabrina Carpenter lyric video.
7. Script, brush and signature accents: F1 driver signatures, Sabrina's script ribbon, BallerFX brush-script numbers.
8. Rounded chunky italic with outline or gradient: SpoodermanEditz, Timeless mograph, MontagEdits (Nunito and Poppins class).
9. Wide-tracked small caps or lowercase subtitle lines, light weight with glow: Hero Edit, VINCHESTER, Billie Eilish LUNCH, venushoyo juno.
10. Chrome, gothic and glossy 3D display faces: XG, MontagEdits, frooky, iluxhaep. Also seen: small monospace micro-text in k3nny_am's poster layout, and platform-native TikTok Sans or Proxima Nova Bold for speech captions.
