## How this was gathered, and its limits
- Web search tool only, about 25 queries, 5 to 6 Nov 2026. Search covered YouTube (titles and descriptions), Quora (user answers) and news.
- **Reddit is blocked** for the search tool ("domain not accessible"), so no Reddit threads are in here.
- **Opening full pages is blocked** in this environment (WebFetch failed on TradingView, Yahoo and Bloomberg). Every fact below comes from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the page.
- Fact grades follow `story-script-writer`:
  - A = read at the primary source. Nothing here is A yet, because no page could be opened.
  - B = reporting that cites the source.
  - C = reported once, from a weak source, or sources conflict.
  - **Before the script locks, every B fact that goes on screen gets checked at its source** (the user can open the links, or allow the hosts).
