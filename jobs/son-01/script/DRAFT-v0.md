**Read-aloud text (279 words with the soundbites, about 258 for the narrator):**
This man bought Nvidia before the AI boom. Then sold it. Twice. He's basically the opposite of Warren Buffett. His name's Masayoshi Son, and the thing that made him one of the richest men alive is the same thing that keeps costing him fortunes. In 2000, for three days, he was the richest person on Earth. Then the dot-com crash hit, and he lost about 70 billion dollars. Around the same time, he met a young founder named Jack Ma, and after about six minutes, he handed him 20 million dollars. That bet on Alibaba was later worth more than 150 billion. So he did it again, bigger. In 2017, his new fund put about 4 billion dollars into a gaming chip company called Nvidia. Then the stock dropped, and in 2019 he sold every share. Months later, he was apologizing for WeWork. *[Son: "My own investment judgment was really bad."]* But Nvidia kept climbing. The shares he sold would later be worth about 150 billion dollars. *[Son: "The fish that got away was big."]* So you'd think he'd hold on next time. Last year, he bought Nvidia again, then sold every last share, 5.8 billion dollars' worth, to put the money into OpenAI. *[Son: "I was crying to sell Nvidia shares."]* Today he has 64.6 billion dollars in OpenAI, about an eighth of the company. He paid for part of it with borrowed money. So every time you ask ChatGPT something, you're using the company he bet SoftBank on. On November 10th, SoftBank reports its results. The first time he let Nvidia go, he missed out on 150 billion dollars. This time, he let it go on purpose.
