## Read-aloud text (248 words: narrator 232, his own clips 14; about 80 s of narration plus about 5 s of clips)
This might be the most expensive photo in AI. The man next to Jensen Huang sold Nvidia. Twice. And the second time, he cried doing it. He's basically the opposite of Warren Buffett. His name's Masayoshi Son, and the habit that made him a fortune is the same habit that keeps losing him one. In 2000, he was briefly the richest man on Earth, until the dot-com crash wiped out about 70 billion dollars. Around the same time, he gave Jack Ma 20 million dollars after a six-minute meeting. That bet on Alibaba grew past 150 billion. So in 2017, he went looking for the next one, and put about 4 billion dollars into a gaming chip company called Nvidia. Then the stock dropped, and in 2019 he sold every share. But Nvidia kept climbing. The shares he sold would later be worth about 150 billion dollars. *[Son, 2024: "The fish that got away was big."]* So you'd think he'd never let go again. Last year, he bought back into Nvidia. Then he sold every last share, 5.8 billion dollars' worth, to put the money into OpenAI. *[Son, Dec 2025: "I was crying to sell Nvidia shares."]* Today he has 64.6 billion dollars in OpenAI, about an eighth of the company, and part of it is borrowed. So every time you ask ChatGPT something, you're using his biggest bet. The first time he sold Nvidia, it was the fish that got away. This time, he's betting OpenAI is the bigger fish.
