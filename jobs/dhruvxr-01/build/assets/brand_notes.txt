DOMAIN FOR THE VOICE-OVER
- linear.app (the brand page itself is served from linear.app). Your assumption is right.

NAMING (summarised from the page)
- "Linear" is one word with a capital L. It names both the company and the app.
- Do not say "Linear app".
- Named releases are capitalised as proper nouns, for example "Linear Method".

USAGE RULES (summarised from the page)
- Give the assets plenty of space. They can be big or small but must not feel cramped or cluttered.
- The graphics are proprietary. Do not alter the files in any way.
- Do not show them in a way that implies a relationship, affiliation or endorsement by Linear.
- Do not use them in your own product, business or service name.
- Do not combine them with other graphics without written consent from Linear.
- Wordmark: use it in references to Linear where space allows. It has stronger recognition than the logomark.
- Logomark: for tight layouts or logo-only grids.
- Icon (rounded "chip"): for referring to Linear as a company, for example on social media. Not included here.

COLOURS
- Monochrome use is preferred, with these two accents:
  - Mercury White: RGB 244, 245, 248, #F4F5F8
  - Nordic Gray: RGB 35, 35, 38, #222326
- The primary brand colour, a desaturated blue, is mostly kept for backgrounds. The page gives no hex for it.
- Note: the light files in the zip are pure #FFFFFF, not Mercury White.

FLAGS FOR THIS SPEC FILM
- "No combining with other graphics" and "no implied endorsement" both matter for a spec ad.
  Label the post "spec, not affiliated", and keep the mark unaltered and on its own.
  Turning the mark black and white is fine, since it is already white.
  Grain, glow or distortion on the mark would count as altering it.
