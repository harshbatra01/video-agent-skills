## Colour and grade (STUDY, to be re-measured)
- Black base. Mean brightness 0.14 to 0.25, near-black 63 to 78%.
- Monochrome footage; colour only on product or brand pieces.
- WHP: fine vertical-line screen texture on live footage. ATN: grain and soft vignette.

## Type (STUDY section 4)
- Neo-grotesk sans, sentence case or lower case. Closest free match: Inter Regular or Medium (judged).
- Ink grey-white #AAAAAA to #DFDFDF on black; #F7F7F7 on bright snow.
- Ink height 2.2 to 4.6% of frame height (16 to 33 px on 720). Big lines: ATN "It gets ignored." 7.8%, "It's earned" about 10%, WHP wordmark 5%.
