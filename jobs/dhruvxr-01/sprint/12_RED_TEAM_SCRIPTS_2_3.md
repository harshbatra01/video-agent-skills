## Which to pilot first

Pilot script 3 first, with fixes 1 to 5 applied. Its burst is its argument: the first second of the video is the thing being wasted, so the hook and the message are the same, which is exactly what a stranger who decides in 1 s needs. It works with sound off (no VO), needs no product, no team photos and no talent release once the eye macro goes, and every asset is ours to make in PIL or a quick hand shoot, so a second version is cheap. Script 1 shares the same ATN grid, so it carries the same copy risk without the self-proving hook. Script 2 goes last: it depends on a VO nobody hears in a muted feed, sells a product that does not exist, and is the closest to the WHP layout.
