## 0. Short answer
- No source named a studio that "owns" this exact look (dark monochrome, 2 to 4 frame bursts, dot-matrix forms, tilted 3D UI, glitch hits, tiny grey sans). Searches for the look returned generic "launch video" sellers and stock templates.
- Work found comes from 4 groups: (a) in-house brand motion teams at SaaS companies, (b) solo designers and small studios selling "launch video" packages (Contra, Fiverr, Dribbble), (c) AI tools that sell launch videos without After Effects, (d) stock template sellers packaging the dot-matrix and glitch look.
- Tools named in sources: After Effects, Cinema 4D, Figma, Redshift, Octane, Blender (bonus skill), DaVinci Resolve (grade), Jitter (Figma to motion; exports video, GIF, Lottie), Rotato (3D device mockups), Remotion (code; has a halftone effect).
- @_dhruvxr identity not confirmed. A fueler.io profile surfaced; fueler.io is BLOCKED.
- Only data-style source on "what works": a vendor audit of 179 SaaS teasers (ADVIDS). 58 of 179 used music with no words (SNIPPET), which is the group our ATN-style piece belongs to.

## 2. How they make it (SNIPPET unless marked)
- UI built in Figma, animated in After Effects (AGFX, Nen listings; also https://contra.com/p/bY7clAN2-ui-to-after-effects-motion-graphics-for-product-launch, title only).
- Figma to Jitter: import with the Figma plugin, animate in the browser, export video, GIF or Lottie; Jitter has a Product Hunt launch GIF/video tutorial: https://blog.jitter.video/how-to-animate-your-figma-files/ . Jitter 1.0 launched 9 Feb 2023; Figma plugin 26 May 2021: https://www.producthunt.com/products/jitter/launches
- 3D shots: Cinema 4D with Redshift or Octane, comped in After Effects (Novra, Cleev.io).
- Tilted device/screen shots without 3D skills: Rotato, Mac and web app, turns screenshots and screen recordings into 3D device mockups and animations; maker listed as Otato GmbH, Zurich, founded 2019 (third-party listing): https://rotato.app/ai-info . Mixed reviews (one cites broken keyframes): https://alternativeto.net/software/rotato/about
- Grade in DaVinci Resolve (Steve Lemuel).
- Halftone / dot-matrix in After Effects:
  - Boris FX Sapphire HalfTone: https://borisfx.com/support/documentation/sapphire/ae/halftone/
  - BCC Halftone, "Circle or Square for dots or lines for a scanline effect": https://borisfx.com/blog/create-after-effects-halftone-plugin-2-methods/
  - Aejuice Halftone (AE, Premiere, OFX): https://aejuice.com/product/halftone/
  - Halftone plugin, "dots of varying sizes"; AEsweets Halftone listed at $24.99 (summary wording): https://www.toolfarm.com/?p=22391
  - No plugin: native "Ball Action" plus other native effects: https://lesterbanks.com/2016/12/quick-simple-halftone-effect-effects/
  - Code: Remotion halftone effect: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/effects/halftone
- JUDGED: in our ffmpeg + PIL/numpy engine, a dot-matrix form = sample luminance on an N px grid, draw a dot per cell sized by brightness. Same idea as "dots of varying sizes" above.

## 4. What the top videos did differently
- No source gave view counts or a ranked list of launch films. Not established which films are "top".
- ADVIDS audit, "179 SaaS teasers, 150 software companies, the first 15 seconds", uploads 6 May 2022 to 22 July 2026, post dated 14 Sep 2026 per summary (SNIPPET; vendor's own research): https://advids.co/blog/saas-teaser-15-second-hook-audit
  - 343 videos screened (0:15 to 1:00), 179 qualified.
  - 111 of 179 speak a line by 0:15; 58 use music with no words.
  - Speaking teasers: median 33 words before 0:15, about 2.2 words per second, middle half 26 to 38 words.
  - Visual findings use only 39 teasers with a full visual record.
  - JUDGED: 58 of 179 = 32% (computed) are music-only, like ATN. So text-over-music is a common, not fringe, choice.
- Vendor blogs (SNIPPET; claim-to-URL match not confirmed per line): hook in the first 3 to 5 s; show real UI within 10 s; front-load value in the first 5 s so it works with sound off; 30 to 60 s for a launch or homepage video; lead with the viewer's problem. Sources: https://www.flowjam.com/blog/saas-launch-video , https://flowjam.com/blog/30-best-launch-video-examples-checklist , https://www.atomikgrowth.com/blog/best-saas-product-launch-videos-of-2026-with-actionable-tips-real-examples , https://vidpros.com/product-launch-video/
  - A "73% drop off before 15 s (Wyzowl 2026)" stat was cited second-hand. UNVERIFIED, do not use.
- Counter-example (SNIPPET): a V21 Studio launch film used clean type and controlled transitions with "no unnecessary effects": https://contra.com/p/L736ZYNF-motion-graphics-project-01
